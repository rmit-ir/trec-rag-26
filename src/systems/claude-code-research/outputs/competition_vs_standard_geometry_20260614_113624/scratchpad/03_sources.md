## Power of a Point (chord-chord, secant-secant, tangent-secant cases)

## Incenter/Excenter Lemma ("Fact 5", arc-midpoint, MB=MC=MI)
- Evan Chen, "The Incenter/Excenter Lemma" (Fact5 handout): https://web.evanchen.cc/handouts/Fact5/Fact5.pdf
- Wikipedia, "Incenter–excenter lemma": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incenter%E2%80%93excenter_lemma

## Ptolemy's Theorem + pentagon/golden ratio
- Wikipedia, "Ptolemy's theorem": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ptolemy's_theorem
- AoPS Wiki, "Ptolemy's theorem": https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Ptolemy's_theorem
- Cut-the-knot, "Ptolemy": https://www.cut-the-knot.org/proofs/ptolemy.shtml
