## Worked mass-point example (constructed, self-checked)
- △ABC; D on BC with BD:DC = 3:1; E on CA with AE:EC = 1:2; cevians AD, BE meet at P.
- masses: B=1, C=3 (since mass_B/mass_C = DC/BD = 1/3); A: mass_A/mass_C = EC/AE = 2 ⇒ A=6. D = B+C = 4.
- AP:PD = mass_D : mass_A = 4:6 = **2:3**. Cross-check on BE: E = A+C = 9, BP:PE = 9:1. Consistent.
