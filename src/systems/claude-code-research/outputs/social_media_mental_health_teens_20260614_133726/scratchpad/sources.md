# Source list & key claims

## Authoritative syntheses
- **US Surgeon General Advisory 2023** (hhs.gov PDF) — 95% of 13–17 use SM, >1/3 "almost constantly", 8th/10th graders ~3.5 hr/day; >3 hr/day ≈ double risk poor MH (Riehm 2019); brain-structure changes; benefits for marginalized/LGBTQ+ youth. https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/sg-youth-mental-health-social-media-advisory.pdf
- **APA Health Advisory 2023** — "not inherently beneficial or harmful"; 10 recommendations; competencies + monitoring. https://www.apa.org/topics/social-media-internet/health-advisory-adolescent-social-media-use
- **NCBI Bookshelf NBK594763** (NASEM) — both positive & negative; Pew stats: 58% more accepted, 67% supported, 71% creative, 80% more connected. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK594763/
- **Pew Research 2025** — teens & parents on SM & MH. https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/04/22/teens-social-media-and-mental-health/

## Effect sizes / debate
- **Orben & Przybylski 2019** (Nature Human Behaviour; PNAS life-satisfaction) — small effects, ~350,000 adolescents; SM explains tiny variance; effects nuanced/reciprocal/gender-specific. https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1902058116
- **Twenge (iGen) / Haidt (Anxious Generation)** — inflection ~2011–2012 in depression/anxiety/self-harm/suicide; smartphones+SM as cause; contested (Nature review "rising hysteria"). https://www.persuasion.community/p/haidt-the-teen-mental-illness-epidemic ; https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/social-media/smartphones-youth-mental-health-jonathan-haidt-book-debate-rcna145928
- **2024 meta-analysis** (24 studies, 68 effects) — r = 0.217, high heterogeneity I²=99.66%. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12649677/
- **UK longitudinal** — little evidence SM causes poorer MH 2 yrs later. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9594056/

## Causal/experimental evidence
- **RCT meta-analysis 2025** (PMC12651081) — 10 RCTs, 1,491 ppl; reducing SM → ↓depression, g=0.28 (0.25 trim-fill); limited-use g=0.33 > abstinence g=0.15; caveats. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12651081/
- **Hunt et al. 2018** — 143 undergrads, limit to 10 min/platform/day 3 wks → ↓loneliness & depression. https://guilfordjournals.com/doi/10.1521/jscp.2018.37.10.751
- **Allcott/Gentzkow 2018 (Facebook deactivation)** — 2,743 users, 4 wks → +0.09 SD wellbeing. https://web.stanford.edu/~gentzkow/research/facebook.pdf
- **Reduce-use → ↓depression also in RCT meta** (above).

## Mechanisms
- **Sleep scoping review** (Springer Curr Psychiatry Rep 2024) — sleep displacement. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11920-024-01481-9
- **PLOS One meta** — SM addiction ↔ depression, anxiety, FOMO, loneliness, low self-esteem. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0329466
- **Burgess 2025 narrative review** — upward social comparison, cyberbullying, sleep displacement, rumination, distrust, negative self-image. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10784535251328925
- **Passive vs active** (Icelandic, PubMed 31361508; JCMC 141-study meta) — passive worse, active can help. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31361508/
- **Sex differences / body image** (self-objectification chain mediation PMC11396477) — girls more vulnerable. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11396477/

## Positive impacts
- AAP COE — connection, support, info, identity. https://www.aap.org/en/patient-care/media-and-children/center-of-excellence-on-social-media-and-youth-mental-health/qa-portal/...
- LGBTQ+/marginalized peer support & identity development (Surgeon General; NCBI).
- SM-based MH interventions — access, anonymity, community. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9483686/
