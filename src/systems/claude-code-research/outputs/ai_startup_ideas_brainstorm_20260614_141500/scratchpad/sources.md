  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posha_(company)

## Idea 2 — money-recovery / life-admin agent: competitive landscape (CROWDED)
- Pine AI: autonomous agent, makes phone calls + emails + navigates UIs; Pine Voice / Pine Assistant; avg ~270 min and ~$400 saved, claims 93% success on complex negotiations.
  - https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/06/pine-ai-aims-consumer-ai-agent-complex-customer-service-interactions/
  - https://www.19pine.ai/blog/best-management-and-subscription-cancellation-apps
- Vibrato: AI calls to negotiate bills / cancel subscriptions. https://www.getvibrato.com/
- Rocket Money: bank-linked (Plaid), cancels subscriptions, tracks spend. https://www.cnbc.com/select/rocket-money-review/
- Plaid PFM API. https://plaid.com/solutions/personal-financial-management/
- Differentiation wedge: PROACTIVE money-recovery (refunds/compensation owed) vs reactive user-initiated negotiation.

## Idea 3 — conversation rehearsal/coach: competitive landscape
- Yoodli: AI roleplay personas; live Zoom/Meet/Teams in-call prompts; interviews, sales, negotiations, difficult conversations. https://yoodli.ai/
- Final Round AI: interview prep (feature-rich, pricey).
- Gap/wedge: personal & relational high-stakes talks (not just sales/interview), counterparty-cloned avatar, consumer pricing.
