## Audience
Knows standard US HS geometry (Common Core scope). Zero competition experience.

## Sources (credible)
- **Common Core HS Geometry standards** — defines the *standard* curriculum scope
  (Congruence via rigid motions; Similarity; Circles: inscribed angles, tangents;
  Right triangles & trig; coordinate geometry; area/volume). 
  https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/HSG/
  - Congruence: https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/HSG/CO/
  - Circles: https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/HSG/C/

  power of a point, radical axis, Ceva, Menelaus, Ptolemy, homothety, inversion,

  https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Geometry/Olympiad

  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_of_a_point

  https://yufeizhao.com/olympiad/
- **AwesomeMath, *Lemmas in Olympiad Geometry*** — confirms lemma-driven style.
  https://www.awesomemath.org/wp-pdf-files/xyz/look-inside/lemmas-in-olympiad-geometry-soft-look-inside.pdf

## Key contrast axes (the spine of the post)
1. **Purpose**: HS = master a fixed toolkit + write two-column proofs of *given* claims.
   Competition = *discover* the right idea under time pressure for an unfamiliar figure.
2. **Proof style**: two-column / coordinate algebra vs. synthetic insight, auxiliary
   constructions, "angle chasing", directed angles.
3. **Toolkit gap**: theorems that exist beyond the standard class.
4. **"Bashing"**: heavy machinery (trig/coord/complex/barycentric) as a fallback.
