# FIFA World Cup 2026 — Live Streaming in English-Language Markets

Research strand s1. Tournament: 11 June – 19 July 2026, USA/Canada/Mexico. Data current as of 2026-06-15.

**Cross-market headline:** FOX in the USA switched its production from native 4K (used in 2022) to a **1080p HDR source upconverted to 4K** during the run-up to the tournament. The UK (BBC) likewise upscales an HD source to 4K. There is no native-4K English-language feed of this World Cup; "4K" everywhere means upscaled. (Dan Rayburn / @UHD4k: https://x.com/UHD4k/status/1985428351943594300 ; https://x.com/UHD4k/status/2057897347665789316)

## 1. USA — FOX / FOX One / FOX Sports App / Tubi (English); Telemundo/Peacock (Spanish)

**Rights holder / platform.** FOX Sports holds exclusive U.S. English-language rights; Telemundo holds Spanish. FOX airs 70 matches on the FOX broadcast network and 34 on FS1; every match streams on **FOX One** and via the **FOX Sports App**. **Tubi** (Fox's free AVOD) simulcast two opening matches free.
- Fox press release: https://www.foxcorporation.com/news/corp-press-releases/2026/fox-sports-unveils-historic-fifa-world-cup-2026-broadcast-schedule/
- Goal.com (70 FOX / 34 FS1, FOX One streams all in 4K): https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/watch-live-stream-world-cup-4k/blt1c69cd18966cc8df

**Pricing.**
- FOX One: **$19.99/month or $199.99/year**; 3-day free trial. Verizon myPlan/myHome add-on $15/mo; FOX Nation bundle $24.99/mo. (https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/cheapest-fifa-world-cup-deals/blt0c5a9df106855b0a ; Variety: https://variety.com/2026/shopping/athletes/get-fox-one-app-free-trial-streaming-offer-online-deal-1236779147/)
- Tubi: **free**, ad-supported, no cable login; free account required to stream the 4K simulcast. Only 2 live matches (group openers: Mexico v South Africa 11 Jun, USA v Paraguay 12 Jun) — NOT the knockouts/final. (https://tubitv.com/the-t/articles/fifa-world-cup-2026-on-tubi ; https://tubitv.com/help-center/Content/articles/41587415046043)
- Note: FOX airs the **final free over-the-air** on broadcast FOX (19 Jul); FOX One streams it. (https://tubitv.com/the-t/articles/how-to-watch-2026-world-cup-matches-live-for-free-on-tubi)

**Picture quality.** All 104 matches offered in **4K UHD with HDR** on FOX One (and most major pay-TV providers) and on the two Tubi simulcasts. CRITICAL: the 4K is **upconverted from a 1080p HDR source feed**, not native 4K (a workflow change FOX made vs. 2022's native 4K). HDR10 / Dolby Vision referenced as the consumer formats. (https://x.com/UHD4k/status/1985428351943594300 ; https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/watch-live-stream-world-cup-4k/blt1c69cd18966cc8df)

**Frame rate.** Not explicitly published for the 2026 feed in sources found. FOX U.S. sports 4K HDR workflow historically 59.94/60fps; source feed is 1080p HDR. (No authoritative fps figure located — FLAG as unconfirmed.)

**Bitrate.** No official FOX One figure published. FOX recommends **~25 Mbps** steady internet per 4K stream; the broader 4K range cited is 15–25 Mbps, and FOX's comparable upscaled-4K Super Bowl LX workflow needed ~15 Mbps. (https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/watch-live-stream-world-cup-4k/blt1c69cd18966cc8df — these are connection-speed recommendations, not measured encode bitrate; FLAG: true measured bitrate not published.)

**Audio / commentary.** English commentary on FOX/FOX One/Tubi (10 on-site commentary pairings). Spanish via Telemundo/Universo and streamed on **Peacock Premium** (separate platform). (https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/fifa-world-cup-2026-commentators-pundits-and-hosts/blt869f8826602d47bc ; https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/stream-telemundos-spanish-language-coverage-fifa-world-cup-2026tm-peacock-including-live-matches)

**Distribution factors.** Tubi 4K requires a compatible 4K device (Apple TV 4K, Roku Ultra, Fire TV Stick 4K) + free account. U.S. geo-restricted. FOX One is a standalone DTC app.

## 2. United Kingdom — BBC (iPlayer) + ITV (ITVX), STV in Scotland

**Rights holder / platform.** Free-to-air rights **shared by BBC and ITV**; all 104 matches split between them, both simulcast the final. Streaming via **BBC iPlayer** and **ITVX** (STV Player in Scotland).
- https://www.trustedreviews.com/explainer/world-cup-2026-stream-how-to-watch-live-4k-free
- https://www.techadvisor.com/article/730242/how-to-watch-the-world-cup-in-4k-uk-streaming-guide.html

**Pricing.** **Free-to-air / free.** (TV Licence required for live BBC viewing per UK law.) No subscription, no PPV.

**Picture quality.** BBC's **54 matches available in 4K (UHD) HLG HDR on iPlayer** — first World Cup BBC offers in 4K HDR via iPlayer (Euro 2024 was not). **ITV/ITVX limited to HD (1080p), no 4K, no HDR.** The 4K is **upscaled** — the source feed is HD, so iPlayer UHD is upconverted, not native 4K. HDR standard is **HLG only** (no HDR10/Dolby Vision); requires an HLG-capable TV. (https://www.techradar.com/news/bbc-iplayer-will-show-the-world-cup-in-4k-hdr-but-theres-a-dolby-atmos-catch ; https://www.whathifi.com/streaming-entertainment/how-to-watch/i-found-all-the-ways-to-watch-the-world-cup-2026-in-4k-but-theres-a-catch)

**Frame rate.** BBC UHD sports streams are typically **50fps** (UK/PAL standard); not separately re-confirmed for 2026 in sources retrieved (FLAG: 50fps is the established BBC iPlayer UHD frame rate but not explicitly restated for WC2026 in located sources).

**Bitrate.** No specific published Mbps for the 2026 iPlayer UHD stream in sources found. BBC iPlayer UHD HLG streams historically run ~24–36 Mbps adaptive; not confirmed for this event (FLAG: unconfirmed).

**Audio / commentary.** English commentary. **Dolby Atmos NOT available** for the 4K HDR iPlayer stream — the "catch" reported by TechRadar; standard stereo/surround only. (https://www.techradar.com/news/bbc-iplayer-will-show-the-world-cup-in-4k-hdr-but-theres-a-dolby-atmos-catch)

**Distribution factors.** UK geo-restricted (iPlayer/ITVX). iPlayer 4K is app-only (no linear BBC 4K channel). Capacity-limited: BBC has historically capped concurrent 4K iPlayer streams for big events.

## 3. Canada — Bell Media: TSN / CTV / Crave / TSN+ (English), RDS (French)

**Rights holder / platform.** **Bell Media** is exclusive Canadian broadcaster/streamer of all 104 matches. English on **TSN** (and select on **CTV**); French on RDS/RDS2/Noovo. Streaming via **TSN.ca, TSN App, TSN+, CTV App, and Crave**.
- https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/06/04/bell-media-to-carry-all-104-fifa-world-cup-2026-matches-across-tsn-rds-and-streaming-platforms/
- https://www.tsn.ca/soccer/fifa-world-cup/article/bell-media-is-canadas-destination-for-every-fifa-world-cup-2026-moment/

**Pricing (CAD, + tax).**
- TSN Direct (streaming): **$29.99/month**, or limited-time **3-month pass $59.99** covering the tournament.
- TSN+ standalone: **$8.00/month or $80.00/year** (included in TSN Direct).
- Crave (carries select ~44 matches incl. Canada's games + finals): from **$11.99/month**; Crave Premium with TSN & RDS bundle enables up to 4K + up to 4 concurrent streams.
- (https://www.goal.com/en-ca/news/tsn-ctv-rds-watch-and-live-stream-fifa-world-cup-2026-canada/blt9b2d846001b1c908 ; https://www.technobezz.com/how-to-watch-world-cup-2026-in-canada)

**Picture quality.** Bell Media states coverage is delivered **"live in brilliant HDR" on select platforms**; it has **not announced native 4K for the Canadian feeds**, BUT **Crave Premium (with TSN & RDS) subscribers can stream up to 4K**. So: HDR confirmed; 4K available via Crave Premium tier. (https://www.sportsvideo.org/2026/06/04/bell-media-to-carry-all-104-fifa-world-cup-2026-matches-across-tsn-rds-and-streaming-platforms/ ; https://www.technobezz.com/how-to-watch-world-cup-2026-in-canada)

**Frame rate.** Not published in sources found (FLAG: unconfirmed).

**Bitrate.** Not published (FLAG: unconfirmed).

**Audio / commentary.** English (TSN). **TSN+ carries exclusive multi-language feeds** — play-by-play in a local language of the competing teams, in addition to the main English call. French on RDS. (https://www.goal.com/en-ca/news/tsn-ctv-rds-watch-and-live-stream-fifa-world-cup-2026-canada/blt9b2d846001b1c908)

**Distribution factors.** Canada geo-restricted. Crave Premium: up to 4 simultaneous streams, up to 4K. Coverage spread across multiple apps (TSN/CTV/Crave) depending on the match.

## 4. Australia — SBS / SBS On Demand (exclusive free-to-air)

**Rights holder / platform.** **SBS** holds EXCLUSIVE Australian rights to all 104 matches, live & free on SBS TV and **SBS On Demand**. NOTE: Optus Sport (2022 rights holder) does NOT hold 2026 rights; not on Kayo/Stan either. (https://www.sbs.com.au/sport/fifa-world-cup-2026/how-to-watch ; https://www.finder.com.au/news/how-to-watch-2026-fifa-world-cup-live-free-australia ; https://samrogroup.com/sbs-optus-or-stan-streaming-the-world-cup-2026-in-australia/)

**Pricing.** **Free.** Ad-supported. Only a **free SBS account** (with an Australian postcode) is required. No subscription/PPV. (https://www.sbs.com.au/ondemand/fifa-world-cup-2026)

**Picture quality.** **HD only — 1080p, NO 4K, NO HDR.** SBS streams all matches, replays, highlights and mini-matches in 1080p HD. (https://www.whathifi.com/streaming-entertainment/how-to-watch/i-found-all-the-ways-to-watch-the-world-cup-2026-in-4k-but-theres-a-catch — "SBS … will do so in HD, not 4K … 1080p HD")

**Frame rate.** Reported at **25fps** (PAL-derived) for the SBS streams per What Hi-Fi's roundup. (https://www.whathifi.com/streaming-entertainment/how-to-watch/i-found-all-the-ways-to-watch-the-world-cup-2026-in-4k-but-theres-a-catch — FLAG: single-source; 25fps figure should be treated as indicative.)

**Bitrate.** Not published (FLAG: unconfirmed). SBS On Demand HD typically adaptive up to ~6–8 Mbps but no official WC2026 figure.

**Audio / commentary.** English commentary (SBS, an Australian broadcaster). Pre/post-game coverage + 30-min mini-matches. (https://www.sbs.com.au/ondemand/fifa-world-cup-2026)

**Distribution factors.** Australia geo-restricted (Australian postcode at signup). Available on web, mobile, tablet, smart TVs / wide device range. Free account required. (https://www.sbs.com.au/sport/fifa-world-cup-2026/how-to-watch)

## Data gaps / flags
- **Exact published encode bitrates (Mbps)** are NOT publicly disclosed by any of the broadcasters for their WC2026 streams. Only connection-speed *recommendations* (FOX ~25 Mbps) and historical comparisons exist.
- **Frame rate** explicitly confirmed only for Australia/SBS (25fps, single-source). USA (likely ~60fps), UK BBC (likely 50fps), Canada, India: standard regional rates assumed but not restated in WC2026-specific sources.
- The **"4K" picture in USA (FOX) and UK (BBC) is upscaled from HD**, not native — strongest, most newsworthy technical finding of this strand.
- **India** premise in the task (JioHotstar) is outdated — rights moved to **Zee / Unite8 / ZEE5**.
