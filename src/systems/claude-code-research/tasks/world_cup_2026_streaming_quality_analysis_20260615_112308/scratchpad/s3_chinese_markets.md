# FIFA World Cup 2026 — Live Streaming in Chinese-Language Markets

Research date: 2026-06-15 (tournament ongoing). Markets: Mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan.

> Important framing note: The tournament runs 11/12 June – 19/20 July 2026, co-hosted by USA/Canada/Mexico, expanded to 48 teams / 104 matches. Chinese-language sources frequently cite "Beijing time 12 June – 20 July" and the historical "64 matches" figure (an error in some recycled copy); the correct count is 104.

---

## 1. MAINLAND CHINA

### Rights holder & platforms
- **China Media Group (CMG)** — parent of state broadcaster **CCTV** — holds **exclusive free-to-air, pay-TV AND digital rights** for mainland China. Deal finalized only ~1 month before kickoff (mid-May 2026) after protracted negotiations over kick-off times and price. ([SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/broadcast-ott/fifa-world-cup-2026-china-cmg-cctv-rights-deal-may-2026/), [Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202605/1361055.shtml), [The Business Standard](https://www.tbsnews.net/world/china-state-broadcaster-reaches-world-cup-deal-fifa-1440196))
- **Deal value:** widely reported at **~US$60 million** — about one-fifth of FIFA's original ~US$300m target. (FIFA publicly pushed back that the price was "the highest ever," and some Chinese outlets speculated a higher figure; US$60m is the figure in reputable trade press.) ([SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/broadcast-ott/fifa-world-cup-2026-china-cmg-cctv-rights-deal-may-2026/)) The agreement reportedly also bundles 2030 Men's WC, 2027 + 2031 Women's WC.
- **TV channels:** CCTV-5 (sports, all main matches — cited as "92 matches live"), CCTV-5+ (12 key final-round group games simulcast), CCTV-1 (selected), plus Olympic Channel / CCTV-16. Key matches (opening, semis, final) on CCTV-5 main channel. ([CCTV official media plan](https://www.cctv.com/2026/05/18/ARTIZ7e9ZxedCdpksxcEQu9F260518.shtml))
- **Official CCTV streaming:** 央视频 (Yangshipin) app + yangshipin.cn, 央视网 (cctv.com / worldcup.cctv.com), 央视体育 (CCTV Sports) app, 央视新闻 (CCTV News) app — all free, ad-free, "stable quality," synced with CCTV-5. ([CCTV plan](https://www.cctv.com/2026/05/18/ARTIZ7e9ZxedCdpksxcEQu9F260518.shtml), [Fishnet guide](https://www.fishnet.net/2026-world-cup-live-stream/))
- **Sublicensed digital platform: China Mobile Migu 咪咕视频** — sublicensed for streaming (its third consecutive WC after 2018 Russia, 2022 Qatar). Migu carries **all matches**, with a free tier. ([SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/broadcast-ott/fifa-world-cup-2026-china-cmg-cctv-rights-deal-may-2026/), [NetEase/163](https://m.163.com/dy/article/KV5M17M30511BBQE.html))
- **Douyin 抖音 / Xiaohongshu (RedNote) 小红书:** Some sources say RedNote streams matches free and Douyin offers partial free coverage + short-form highlights under distribution deals — but these are NOT consistently confirmed by official CCTV/CMG sources and should be flagged as uncertain. Douyin's role is mostly clips/short-form. ([Sina/k.sina](https://k.sina.cn/article_7879995911_1d5af320706801xwu0.html), [Fishnet](https://www.fishnet.net/2026-world-cup-live-stream/)) FLAG: sublicense status of Douyin not firmly established in trade press.

### Pricing
- **CCTV channels + CCTV apps (央视频/央视网/央视体育):** FREE (free-to-air + free streaming). ([CCTV plan](https://www.cctv.com/2026/05/18/ARTIZ7e9ZxedCdpksxcEQu9F260518.shtml))
- **Migu 咪咕:** FREE to watch all 104 matches, BUT with a freemium quality wall — free tier reportedly **locked to 720p**; **4K / 50fps / HDR reserved for paid VIP membership**. ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1033184616_122342629), [HD-Migu VIP-vs-free comparison](https://www.hd-miguworldcup.com/migu-video-world-cup-vip-vs-free)) Estimated Migu VIP pricing ~¥30–50/month, with reports Migu raised 2026 pricing ~20% vs prior cycle (figures speculative/unconfirmed). ([Fishnet](https://www.fishnet.net/2026-world-cup-live-stream/))
- **China Mobile data perk:** China Mobile subscribers can claim **50GB of Migu-directed (zero-rated) traffic** so streaming doesn't consume their data plan. ([Migu via search summary / 163](https://m.163.com/dy/article/KV5M17M30511BBQE.html))

### Picture quality
- **CCTV:** 4K UHD broadcast confirmed — plans for a CCTV 4K channel (CCTV-16/4K UHD) with **Dolby audio**, plus a 4K live version inside the 央视频 app. ([search summary of CCTV plan / Fishnet](https://www.fishnet.net/2026-world-cup-live-stream/)) CCTV has prior 4K/8K WC trial history (2018/2022), but **8K for 2026 is NOT confirmed** in sources — flag as unverified.
- **Migu:** advertises support up to **4K/8K UHD** with **HDR Vivid** on some matches — but in practice the FREE tier is **720p**; 4K 50fps HDR is behind VIP. ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1033184616_122342629)) NOTE DISCREPANCY: a 6/15 commentary piece claims neither CCTV-5 nor Migu free feed reached even 1080p in practice — i.e., real-world delivered quality may be below marketed specs. ([Sohu article references](https://www.sohu.com/a/1033184616_122342629)) FLAG: marketed vs delivered quality dispute.
- Independent platform-comparison evaluation (Toutiao) lists delivered specs: Migu **4K 60fps SDR @ ~20 Mbps** (weaker dynamic range, ~12% less shadow detail than HDR); iQiyi Sports 4K 60fps HDR @ 22 Mbps; Tencent Sports 1080p 50fps HDR @ 15 Mbps; Douyin Sports vertical **1080p 30fps @ 8 Mbps**. ([Toutiao comparison](https://www.toutiao.com/article/7639302981560713764/)) (Note: this article is partly promotional for a platform called "9939直播" and cites a dubious "64 matches" figure — treat bitrate/fps numbers as indicative, not official.)

### Frame rate
- Migu marketed at **50fps** (VIP 4K/HDR/50fps); delivered comparison data cites **4K 60fps** for Migu and 50fps for some others. ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1033184616_122342629), [Toutiao](https://www.toutiao.com/article/7639302981560713764/)) CCTV broadcast frame rate not explicitly published; one source claims CCTV app feed ran 1080p/25fps. FLAG: CCTV fps not officially stated.

### Bitrate
- No official CCTV/Migu bitrate published. Third-party comparison cites **Migu ~20 Mbps (4K SDR)**; Douyin ~8 Mbps (1080p). ([Toutiao](https://www.toutiao.com/article/7639302981560713764/)) Use cautiously.

### Audio / commentary
- **Mandarin** commentary throughout. CCTV has its own commentary team; CCTV 4K channel adds **Dolby audio**. Migu offers multi-feed commentary switching, AI commentary (越位/射门 data), virtual studio, tactical replay, plus star commentators (e.g., 詹俊, 张路 cited). ([NetEase/163](https://m.163.com/dy/article/KV5M17M30511BBQE.html), [Fishnet](https://www.fishnet.net/2026-world-cup-live-stream/))

### Distribution factors
- **Great Firewall / geo-restriction:** All these services are geo-locked to mainland China; overseas Chinese need VPN/SmartDNS workarounds (tutorials exist for Migu abroad). ([51QuickFox tutorial](https://www.51quickfox.com/49023.html), [worldcup2026cn overseas guide](https://worldcup2026cn.com/live/))
- **Free vs paid split:** CCTV channels/apps free; Migu free at 720p, paid for 4K — a deliberate freemium conversion funnel. ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1033184616_122342629))
- **5G / device support:** China Mobile 5G + 50GB zero-rated Migu traffic; mobile + smart-TV (移动高清/咪视界) + web. ([163](https://m.163.com/dy/article/KV5M17M30511BBQE.html))
- **Timing:** Most group matches in daytime Beijing time (06:00/09:00/12:00); knockout/opening/final in early-morning hours — a recurring complaint that shaped the late, discounted rights deal. ([SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/broadcast-ott/fifa-world-cup-2026-china-cmg-cctv-rights-deal-may-2026/), [Fishnet](https://www.fishnet.net/2026-world-cup-live-stream/))

## 2. HONG KONG

### Rights holder & platforms
- **HKT / PCCW's Now TV** holds **exclusive HK pay-TV + streaming rights** to all 104 matches — its **third consecutive** World Cup (2018, 2022, 2026). Announced jointly by HKT (SEHK:6823) and PCCW (SEHK:0008) on **25 Feb 2026**. ([PCCW official press release PDF](https://www.pccw.com/assets/Common/files/press-release/2026/Feb/Press%20release_Now%20TV%20and%20ViuTV%20to%20exclusively%20broadcast%20the%20FIFA%20World%20Cup%202026%E2%84%A2%20in%20Hong%20Kong_EN.pdf), [CSI Magazine](https://www.csimagazine.com/csi/NowTV-2026-World-Cup.php))
- **Free-to-air complement: ViuTV** (HK Television Entertainment Co., free TV under PCCW) airs **selected matches live for free, including the opening match and the final**. ViuTV Channel 99 (Chinese) / ViuTVsix Channel 96 (English). ([PCCW press release](https://www.pccw.com/assets/Common/files/press-release/2026/Feb/Press%20release_Now%20TV%20and%20ViuTV%20to%20exclusively%20broadcast%20the%20FIFA%20World%20Cup%202026%E2%84%A2%20in%20Hong%20Kong_EN.pdf))
- **Streaming:** Now TV App + Now TV website (the "Now E" OTT brand / Now TV streaming services). No separate beIN or TVB rights — beIN holds MENA only; TVB does NOT hold 2026 HK rights. ([CSI Magazine](https://www.csimagazine.com/csi/NowTV-2026-World-Cup.php), [Now TV Event Pass listing](https://shop.theclub.com.hk/now-tv-emirates-fa-cup-24-25-event-pass-without-set-top-box-version-2))

### Pricing (HKD)
- **Now TV FIFA World Cup 2026 Event Pass:** **standard retail HK$980**; **early-bird HK$380** (all 104 matches) for designated Now TV / csl / 1O1O / NETVIGATOR / HKT Home Phone / Club Sim customers, valid until **24 April 2026**. ([PCCW press release](https://www.pccw.com/assets/Common/files/press-release/2026/Feb/Press%20release_Now%20TV%20and%20ViuTV%20to%20exclusively%20broadcast%20the%20FIFA%20World%20Cup%202026%E2%84%A2%20in%20Hong%20Kong_EN.pdf))
- Model is a **subscription Event Pass** (covers the whole tournament 12 June – 31 July 2026 HK time, incl. featured re-runs), NOT per-match PPV. ([Now TV Event Pass / HKTVmall listing](https://www.hktvmall.com/hktv/en/main/Mob-hk/s/H0762001/Gadgets-%26-Electronics/Gadgets-%26-Entertainment/AV-%26-Entertainment/Media-Player-%26-Set-top-Box/Set-top-Box/FIFA-World-Cup-2026-Event-Pass-without-SetTop-box-version/p/H0762001_S_nowtv-worldcup2026-pass))
- A "without Set-Top box version" Event Pass exists (stream-only, no STB rental). Fees exclude HD/4K Connection Service and STB rental. ([PCCW press release fine print](https://www.pccw.com/assets/Common/files/press-release/2026/Feb/Press%20release_Now%20TV%20and%20ViuTV%20to%20exclusively%20broadcast%20the%20FIFA%20World%20Cup%202026%E2%84%A2%20in%20Hong%20Kong_EN.pdf))
- **ViuTV:** FREE (selected matches incl. opener + final). ([PCCW press release](https://www.pccw.com/assets/Common/files/press-release/2026/Feb/Press%20release_Now%20TV%20and%20ViuTV%20to%20exclusively%20broadcast%20the%20FIFA%20World%20Cup%202026%E2%84%A2%20in%20Hong%20Kong_EN.pdf))

### Picture quality
- Now TV: **all 104 matches in HD and 4K** (4K "subject to terms and conditions," and may require the paid HD/4K Connection Service). ([PCCW press release](https://www.pccw.com/assets/Common/files/press-release/2026/Feb/Press%20release_Now%20TV%20and%20ViuTV%20to%20exclusively%20broadcast%20the%20FIFA%20World%20Cup%202026%E2%84%A2%20in%20Hong%20Kong_EN.pdf), [CSI Magazine](https://www.csimagazine.com/csi/NowTV-2026-World-Cup.php))
- HDR: not explicitly stated in the press release. FLAG: HDR unconfirmed.

### Frame rate / bitrate
- Not published. FLAG: no fps or bitrate figures available for Now TV HK.

### Audio / commentary
- Cantonese commentary expected (Now TV's standard for HK football; ViuTV broadcasts 24h in Chinese/Cantonese on Ch 99, English on ViuTVsix Ch 96). Press release does not explicitly state commentary language for matches. FLAG: Cantonese commentary strongly implied but not explicitly confirmed in the press release.

### Distribution factors
- **Devices:** Now TV App + website on Android, iOS, Smart TV, Web; **2 concurrent streams**; STB optional (separate "without STB" pass). ([Now TV Event Pass listing](https://shop.theclub.com.hk/now-tv-emirates-fa-cup-24-25-event-pass-without-set-top-box-version-2))
- **Geo:** Now TV available only in designated areas (HK). ([PCCW press release](https://www.pccw.com/assets/Common/files/press-release/2026/Feb/Press%20release_Now%20TV%20and%20ViuTV%20to%20exclusively%20broadcast%20the%20FIFA%20World%20Cup%202026%E2%84%A2%20in%20Hong%20Kong_EN.pdf))
- **5G:** HKT positions itself as HK's "true 5G provider"; bundle offers tie the Event Pass to its mobile/broadband plans. ([PCCW press release](https://www.pccw.com/assets/Common/files/press-release/2026/Feb/Press%20release_Now%20TV%20and%20ViuTV%20to%20exclusively%20broadcast%20the%20FIFA%20World%20Cup%202026%E2%84%A2%20in%20Hong%20Kong_EN.pdf))

## 3. TAIWAN

### Rights holder & platforms
- **ELTA / 愛爾達 (ELTA Sports)** is the **exclusive Taiwan rights holder / general agent** (總代理) for the 2026 World Cup — its **4th consecutive** tournament. Live from 12 June 2026. ([UDN Money / Economic Daily](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5612/9555240), [ELTA OTT](https://eltaott.tv/sports/play/1/2084))
- **Platforms:** all 104 matches live on **ELTA.tv** (OTT app/web) + ELTA Sports TV channels; **Chunghwa Telecom MOD** (中華電信MOD) and **Hami Video** carry full coverage (Hami Video runs a 24h dedicated channel + "Hami足球1–4台"). Free-to-air: **CTS (華視)** airs selected key matches free, **TTV (台視)** airs designated matches; **EBC / Eastern (東森)** cable shows selected matches. ([UDN Money](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5612/9555240), [NowNews](https://www.nownews.com/news/6845571), [BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/183025/post/202606030072/), [bnext](https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/91241/2026-fifa-world-cup-taiwan))

### Pricing (TWD)
- **ELTA.tv subscription:** ~**NT$499 quarterly/seasonal** (covers all 104 matches) or **NT$199 single knockout/final match** PPV. ([mrmad guide](https://mrmad.com.tw/2026-fifa-world-cup-live), search summary of UDN/ELTA plans)
- **Hami Video (TV Sports Hall 運動館):** **90-day plan NT$399** (≈ NT$3.84 per match across 104 games — billed as cheapest); **180-day plan NT$699**. ([NowNews](https://www.nownews.com/news/6845571))
- **Chunghwa Telecom MOD / mobile-broadband bundle:** as low as **NT$129/month** on a 12-month contract for Chunghwa mobile/broadband customers. ([NowNews](https://www.nownews.com/news/6845571))
- **Free-to-air (CTS 華視 / TTV 台視 / EBC):** FREE for the selected matches they carry (incl. opener & final on FTA). ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/183025/post/202606030072/), [TVBS](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/sports/3219947))

### Picture quality
- **ELTA: 4K / HD** full coverage of all 104 matches (ELTA promotes "最完整的104場全場次4K/HD直播"). ([worldcupfifa.com.tw / search summary](https://www.worldcupfifa.com.tw/post/world-cup-2026-watch-online-taiwan-elta)) HDR: not explicitly stated. FLAG: HDR unconfirmed.

### Frame rate / bitrate
- Not published. FLAG: no fps or bitrate figures available for ELTA/Hami.

### Audio / commentary
- **Mandarin (Chinese) professional commentary** — ELTA fields a Chinese-language pro-commentary lineup ("中文專業球評陣容"). ([worldcupfifa.com.tw / search summary](https://www.worldcupfifa.com.tw/post/world-cup-2026-watch-online-taiwan-elta))

### Distribution factors
- **Devices:** ELTA.tv on web, mobile/tablet app, smart TV; **up to 3 simultaneous devices per account**; App on-demand replay. ([worldcupfifa.com.tw / search summary](https://www.worldcupfifa.com.tw/post/world-cup-2026-watch-online-taiwan-elta))
- **Geo:** Taiwan-only (Chunghwa MOD/Hami Video/ELTA geo-locked to TW).
- **Free vs paid:** FTA (CTS/TTV/EBC) for headline matches; full 104-match coverage paid via ELTA/Hami/MOD.

## KEY DISCREPANCIES / DATA TO FLAG
1. **Migu picture quality dispute:** marketed as 4K/8K, but the FREE tier is reportedly **720p**, with 4K/50fps/HDR paywalled to VIP; a 6/15 op-ed even claims neither CCTV-5 nor Migu's free feed reached 1080p in practice. Marketed vs delivered quality is contested. ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1033184616_122342629), [HD-Migu](https://www.hd-miguworldcup.com/migu-video-world-cup-vip-vs-free))
2. **CCTV 8K:** No confirmation of 8K for 2026 (4K + Dolby confirmed). 8K is from prior-cycle trial history only — unverified for 2026.
3. **Douyin/RedNote sublicense:** streaming sublicense status not firmly confirmed by official/trade sources; likely clips + possibly RedNote free streaming, but treat as uncertain.
4. **Frame rate & bitrate:** No official published figures in any of the 3 markets except third-party (non-official, promotional) comparison numbers for mainland China.
5. **HK/TW HDR & commentary language:** Cantonese (HK) and Mandarin (TW) strongly implied by precedent but not explicitly stated in the primary press materials.
6. **Match count "64":** several Chinese sources recycle an erroneous 64-match figure; correct is 104.
7. **China deal value:** US$60m (trade press) vs FIFA's "highest ever" claim vs Chinese speculation of higher figures — contested.
