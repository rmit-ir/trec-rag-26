# FIFA World Cup 2026 — Live Streaming in Major SPANISH-Language Markets

Research date: 2026-06-15. Tournament ongoing (11 Jun – 19 Jul 2026; hosts USA/Canada/Mexico).
Every claim carries an inline source URL. Unavailable data is flagged explicitly.

## 1. USA-HISPANIC — Telemundo / Peacock / Universo / Telemundo app

**Rights holder & platforms.** Telemundo (TelevisaUnivision's US rival NBCUniversal) holds exclusive US Spanish-language rights. Peacock is the exclusive streaming home for all Spanish-language coverage; 92 matches air on Telemundo broadcast and 12 on Universo, and all 104 also stream on Peacock and the Telemundo app. ([nbcuniversal.com](https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/stream-telemundos-spanish-language-coverage-fifa-world-cup-2026tm-peacock-including-live-matches)) ([aol.com](https://www.aol.com/articles/telemundo-unveils-700-hours-world-214017310.html))

**Pricing.**
- Free: live matches the first three days (June 11–13) stream free on the Telemundo app with no login; the eight opening group matches (incl. Mexico v South Africa, USA v Paraguay) are also free across all Peacock tiers including the ad-supported Select tier. ([nbcuniversal.com](https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/stream-telemundos-spanish-language-coverage-fifa-world-cup-2026tm-peacock-including-live-matches))
- All 104 matches require Peacock Premium ($10.99/mo, ad-supported) or Premium Plus ($16.99/mo, ad-free); annual Premium $109.99. ([gamesradar.com](https://www.gamesradar.com/peacock-tv-costs-peacock-premium-prices-nbc-streaming-service/)) ([dealnews.com](https://www.dealnews.com/features/peacock/cost/))

**Picture quality.** Spanish-language coverage is HD (1080p) only — NOT 4K. ([goal.com](https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/watch-live-stream-world-cup-4k/blt1c69cd18966cc8df)) Peacock is the only US streamer offering Dolby Vision HDR for the World Cup, applied to the Telemundo Spanish feed (HDR on supported devices, but at HD resolution, not UHD). ([nbcuniversal.com](https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/stream-telemundos-spanish-language-coverage-fifa-world-cup-2026tm-peacock-including-live-matches)) ([techradar.com](https://www.techradar.com/televisions/the-world-cup-will-be-broadcast-in-dolby-vision-hdr-and-higher-quality-dolby-atmos-using-the-new-ac-4-streaming-format))
- NOTE: free 4K HDR alternative exists but in ENGLISH only — Tubi streams the opening ceremony + select matches free in 4K HDR; Spanish 4K not offered. ([goal.com](https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/watch-live-stream-world-cup-4k/blt1c69cd18966cc8df))

**Frame rate.** Not published in the sources consulted. FLAG: no confirmed 50/60fps figure for the Telemundo/Peacock Spanish feed.

**Bitrate.** Not published. FLAG: no Mbps figure available.

**Audio / commentary.** Full Spanish-language commentary on all 104 matches. Audio upgraded to Dolby Atmos using the new AC-4 codec on supported devices (Peacock is the only platform with Atmos/AC-4 for the tournament). ([nbcuniversal.com](https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/stream-telemundos-spanish-language-coverage-fifa-world-cup-2026tm-peacock-including-live-matches)) ([techradar.com](https://www.techradar.com/televisions/the-world-cup-will-be-broadcast-in-dolby-vision-hdr-and-higher-quality-dolby-atmos-using-the-new-ac-4-streaming-format))

**Distribution.** US-only availability ("across the U.S."). ([nbcuniversal.com](https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/stream-telemundos-spanish-language-coverage-fifa-world-cup-2026tm-peacock-including-live-matches)) Multiview feature lets subscribers watch simultaneous late-group-stage matches at once. ([nbcuniversal.com](https://www.nbcuniversal.com/article/stream-telemundos-spanish-language-coverage-fifa-world-cup-2026tm-peacock-including-live-matches)) Free-vs-paid split: 8 free opening matches / 96 behind Peacock Premium paywall. Device support: Peacock app + Telemundo app (mobile, web, connected TV). Exact simultaneous-stream/device limit not stated in sources (FLAG).

## 2. MEXICO — TelevisaUnivision (TUDN / ViX) & TV Azteca (Azteca Deportes)

**Rights holders & platforms.**
- **ViX** (TelevisaUnivision streaming) is the only platform carrying all 104 matches in Mexico. ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/))
- **TUDN** (pay-TV, Canal 5 / Las Estrellas open-TV) carries 72 of 104 matches; streaming via TUDN En Vivo. ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/))
- **TV Azteca / Azteca Deportes** carries 32 matches free (Azteca Uno, Azteca 7) and via the Azteca Deportes website + iOS/Android app. ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/)) ([eluniversal.com.mx](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/tendencias/mundial-2026-tv-azteca-transmitira-32-partidos-gratis-y-en-vivo/))

**Pricing.**
- Azteca Deportes streaming: FREE, no registration required. ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/))
- ViX free tier (ad-supported): some group-stage matches. ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/))
- ViX Premium: ~MX$149/month; annual MX$1,499 one-time (annual plan includes Pase Mundial). ([sdpnoticias.com](https://www.sdpnoticias.com/negocios/cuanto-cuesta-vix-premium-en-mexico-precios-actualizados-en-2025-para-cada-plan/)) ([tudn.com](https://www.tudn.com/mundial-2026/pase-vix-para-mundial-2026-precio-y-promocion-para-nuevos-suscriptores))
- **Pase Mundial 2026** add-on (all 104 matches): MX$999 regular, promo MX$499 one-time for first 25,000 subscribers with an active monthly subscription. ([tudn.com](https://www.tudn.com/mundial-2026/pase-vix-para-mundial-2026-precio-y-promocion-para-nuevos-suscriptores)) (Earlier secondary report cited MX$799; the official TUDN figure is MX$999 / MX$499 promo — prefer this.)
- TUDN's 72 matches require a paid cable/satellite subscription. ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/))

**Picture quality.** Sources do not specify resolution/4K for ViX, TUDN or Azteca. FLAG: no confirmed resolution/HDR data for Mexico.

**Frame rate.** Not published. FLAG.

**Bitrate.** Not published. FLAG.

**Audio / commentary.** All coverage in Spanish (Mexican Spanish commentary across ViX/TUDN/Azteca). ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/))

**Distribution.** Mexican signals are geo-restricted to Mexico (blocked abroad without VPN). ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/)) Free-vs-paid split: 32 free (Azteca) + some free on ViX ad tier; full 104 only via ViX Pase Mundial. Devices: ViX app, TUDN En Vivo, Azteca Deportes app (iOS/Android) + web. ([speedify.com](https://speedify.com/blog/es/como-ver-mundial-2026-mexico/amp/))

## 3. SPAIN — DAZN (primary), RTVE (free-to-air), Mediaset

**Rights holders & platforms.**
- **DAZN** holds exclusive rights to all 104 matches (acquired via Mediapro, Feb 2026); streamed on DAZN. ([eldiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/vertele/noticias/mundial-2026-calendario-partidos-fase-grupos-emitir-rtve-dazn_1_13293515.html)) ([dazngroup.com](https://dazngroup.com/press-room/la-copa-mundial-de-la-fifa-2026-se-vivira-al-completo-en-dazn-por-1999e/))
- **RTVE (La 1 / RTVE Play)** sub-licensed a free-to-air package: 34 matches — all Spain national-team games, opening match, semifinals and final (17 group + 17 knockout). ([eldiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/vertele/noticias/mundial-2026-calendario-partidos-fase-grupos-emitir-rtve-dazn_1_13293515.html))
- DAZN channels integrated into Movistar Plus+, Orange TV and Vodafone TV for football-package customers. ([eldiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/vertele/noticias/mundial-2026-calendario-partidos-fase-grupos-emitir-rtve-dazn_1_13293515.html))
- NOTE: Initial search suggested Mediaset/Telecinco; the authoritative el diario/DAZN sources confirm the free-to-air package sits with RTVE, not Mediaset. Treat Mediaset involvement as unconfirmed (FLAG — likely superseded by the DAZN+RTVE deal).

**Pricing.**
- RTVE La 1 / RTVE Play: FREE (34 matches). ([eldiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/vertele/noticias/mundial-2026-calendario-partidos-fase-grupos-emitir-rtve-dazn_1_13293515.html))
- DAZN: World Cup add-on €19.99 one-time, but it must be attached to a DAZN plan (cannot buy the World Cup pass alone). ([dazngroup.com](https://dazngroup.com/press-room/la-copa-mundial-de-la-fifa-2026-se-vivira-al-completo-en-dazn-por-1999e/))
- Cheapest full-tournament route: DAZN "Made in USA" plan €7.99/mo (no commitment) for the two tournament months + €19.99 pass = €35.97 total for all 104 matches. ([xataka.com](https://www.xataka.com/seleccion/dazn-baja-precio-su-suscripcion-premium-para-ver-mundial-tiene-truco-esta-forma-barata-para-ver-104-partidos))

**Picture quality.** DAZN's "DAZN Mundial" channels are NOT 4K — the signal is HD, and Movistar Plus+/Orange/Vodafone cannot upconvert HD to UHD. ([adslzone.net](https://www.adslzone.net/noticias/streaming-tv/mundial-2026-4k-dazn-tdt-espana/)) Notably, RTVE's free TDT broadcast reportedly offers comparable/better quality vs DAZN's stream (headline: "la TDT humilla a DAZN"). ([adslzone.net](https://www.adslzone.net/noticias/streaming-tv/mundial-2026-4k-dazn-tdt-espana/))

**Frame rate.** Not published. FLAG.

**Bitrate.** Not published. FLAG.

**Audio / commentary.** Spanish (Castilian) commentary. RTVE narrators incl. Juan Carlos Rivero; DAZN team incl. Miguel Ángel Román, Sandra Díaz, Pablo Pinto + ex-players (Pepe Reina, Javi Martínez). ([eldiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/vertele/noticias/mundial-2026-calendario-partidos-fase-grupos-emitir-rtve-dazn_1_13293515.html)) ([dazngroup.com](https://dazngroup.com/press-room/la-copa-mundial-de-la-fifa-2026-se-vivira-al-completo-en-dazn-por-1999e/))

**Distribution.** Spain geo-locked. Free-vs-paid split: 34 free on RTVE / all 104 on paid DAZN (70 DAZN-exclusive). ([eldiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/vertele/noticias/mundial-2026-calendario-partidos-fase-grupos-emitir-rtve-dazn_1_13293515.html)) Devices: DAZN app (mobile/web/TV), RTVE Play, plus operator set-top boxes (Movistar Plus+, Orange, Vodafone).

### Key cross-market findings
- **No Spanish-language 4K anywhere.** All four markets stream Spanish coverage in HD/1080p; only English-language Peacock English feed gets Dolby Vision UHD-class treatment, and even Peacock's Spanish feed is HD (Dolby Vision applied at HD). 4K/HDR Spanish: only English Tubi 4K HDR exists as a free 4K option in the US. ([goal.com](https://www.goal.com/en-us/news/watch-live-stream-world-cup-4k/blt1c69cd18966cc8df)) ([adslzone.net](https://www.adslzone.net/noticias/streaming-tv/mundial-2026-4k-dazn-tdt-espana/)) ([losandes.com.ar](https://www.losandes.com.ar/deportes/donde-ver-el-mundial-2026-tv-y-streaming-estos-son-los-canales-y-las-plataformas-argentina-n5993509))
- **Audio innovation is the US differentiator:** Peacock's Dolby Atmos + AC-4 codec is a tournament-first, exclusive to Peacock. ([techradar.com](https://www.techradar.com/televisions/the-world-cup-will-be-broadcast-in-dolby-vision-hdr-and-higher-quality-dolby-atmos-using-the-new-ac-4-streaming-format))
- **Frame rate (50/60fps/HFR) and bitrate (Mbps) are NOT publicly disclosed for ANY Spanish-language platform in ANY of the four markets** — significant data gap across the board. FLAG.
- **Free-to-air remains strong** in Mexico (Azteca, 32), Spain (RTVE, 34) and Argentina (Telefe 32 / TV Pública 10); the US has the most limited free window (8 opening matches + first-three-days app window).
- All four markets are **geo-restricted** to their home territory.
