# US VC Macro Overview — June 2021 to June 2026

Scope: US startup/venture-capital macro picture, full 5-year window with heavier weight on Jun 2023–Jun 2026. Primary trackers: PitchBook-NVCA Venture Monitor, CB Insights State of Venture, Carta, Crunchbase. Figures are US-focused unless noted "global."

---

## 1. US VC deal value & deal count by year (the ZIRP peak → correction → AI-driven rebound)

Primary source: PitchBook-NVCA Venture Monitor (annual/quarterly).

- **2021 (ZIRP peak):** US VC-backed companies raised **$329.9B across ~17,054 deals** — nearly double the prior record of $166.6B (2020) and an all-time high for both value and count. Driven by near-zero rates, excess liquidity, and an influx of non-traditional investors. US VC firms also raised a record **$128.3B** in fund capital. [NVCA press release](https://nvca.org/press_releases/u-s-venture-capital-soars-to-new-highs-in-2021/); [PitchBook 2021 record charts](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/2021-record-year-us-venture-capital-six-charts)
- **2022 (correction begins):** **~$236.2B across 18,290 deals** — value fell sharply from 2021 as rates rose, but deal count stayed high (long tail still active). [PitchBook 2023 final-data newsletter](https://pitchbook.com/newsletter/final-data-for-2023-illustrates-the-extent-of-vcs-tough-year)
- **2023 (trough):** **~$168.8B across 15,379 deals** — VC's toughest year of the cycle; ~$72B below 2022. [PitchBook final data 2023](https://pitchbook.com/newsletter/final-data-for-2023-illustrates-the-extent-of-vcs-tough-year)
- **2024 (AI-led recovery starts):** **~$213.2B across ~15,250 deals**; AI/ML captured **47.2%** of US VC deal value. [PitchBook — AI share of VC](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/artificial-intelligence-share-vc-deals)
- **2025 (near-record, AI-fueled):** **~$339.4B across 16,709 deals** — the **second-highest annual deal value ever**, just **$18.8B behind 2021**. Deal count up 9.6% YoY (largely AI). AI/ML captured **65.6%** of deal value ($222B of $339B). [Inc./PitchBook-NVCA](https://www.inc.com/brian-contreras/venture-capital-rebound-ai-pitchbook-nvca/91284645); [Q4 2025 Venture Monitor PDF](https://nvca.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/q4-2025-pitchbook-nvca-venture-monitor.pdf)
- **Q1 2026 (record concentration):** **$267.2B deal value in a single quarter** — exceeded every full-year total except 2021 and 2025. But **five deals = 73% of the $267B**; without the top 5 deals/exits, figures fall 73.2%/86.6%. AI is the most-invested vertical at every stage. [PitchBook Q1 2026 Venture Monitor](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q1-2026-pitchbook-nvca-venture-monitor); [GamesBeat summary](https://gamesbeat.com/q1-2026-hit-big-vc-records-but-top-5-ai-deals-were-most-of-the-funding-pitchbook-nvca/)

**Trajectory shape:** 2021 ZIRP peak (~$330B) → 2022–2023 correction (down to ~$169B, a ~49% drop) → 2024 recovery (~$213B) → 2025 near-record (~$339B) → Q1 2026 single-quarter record ($267B). The dollar rebound is real but is almost entirely an AI + mega-deal phenomenon; deal counts never returned to 2021–2022 highs (16.7K in 2025 vs ~18.3K in 2022).

## 2. AI concentration & mega-rounds

- **AI share of US VC deal value:** 2024 = **47.2%**; 2025 = **65.6%** (PitchBook-NVCA). In Q1 2026 AI accelerated further, the most-invested vertical at every stage. [PitchBook AI share](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/artificial-intelligence-share-vc-deals); [Inc./PitchBook-NVCA](https://www.inc.com/brian-contreras/venture-capital-rebound-ai-pitchbook-nvca/91284645)
- **Global framing (CB Insights):** AI companies raised **$226B in 2025 = 48%** of total venture funding (largest share on record). The US attracted **85% of all AI funding** and 53% of AI deals. [CB Insights State of Venture 2025](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/venture-trends-2025/)
- **Mega-rounds ($100M+):** Globally surged **77% to 738 rounds in 2025**, capturing **$307B = 65% of total venture funding**. [CB Insights State of Venture 2025](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/venture-trends-2025/)
- **$1B+ rounds — extreme top-heaviness:** The six largest 2025 rounds were all AI: **OpenAI $41B, Anthropic $32.5B, Scale $14.8B, xAI $12.8B, Databricks $5B, Aligned $5B** — together $111B, = 49% of all AI funding and **24% of total venture capital**. [CB Insights State of Venture 2025](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/venture-trends-2025/)
- **2024 baseline (CB Insights, global):** AI = 37% of venture funding and 17% of deals (then all-time highs); in Q4 2024, $52B (60% of the quarter) came from $100M+ mega-rounds. [CB Insights State of Venture 2024](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/venture-trends-2024/)
- **Concentration metric (PitchBook):** In 2025, **half of all venture dollars went into just 0.05% of deals**. [Q4 2025 Venture Monitor PDF](https://nvca.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/q4-2025-pitchbook-nvca-venture-monitor.pdf)

## 3. Stage dynamics, valuations, down rounds, the "barbell" (Carta)

- **Seed (Q4 2025):** median post-money valuation hit an all-time high of **$24M**; median seed raise ~$4M, but **95th-percentile seed = $16.6M (>4x the median)**. [Carta — record-setting valuations](https://carta.com/data/record-setting-valuations/)
- **Series A (Q4 2025):** median post-money valuation **$78.7M, +37% YoY** (from $57.5M). An AI foundational-model startup at Series A may raise at a **$300M median** valuation — a narrow segment. [Carta record valuations](https://carta.com/data/record-setting-valuations/); [Carta State of Private Markets Q1 2026](https://carta.com/data/state-of-private-markets-q1-2026/)
- **Series B/C (Q1 2026):** primary pre-money valuations up **17.2%** and **12.5%** respectively since Q1 2025. [Carta Q1 2026](https://carta.com/data/state-of-private-markets-q1-2026/)
- **Down rounds:** fell to **11.4% in Q1 2026**, back to 2019–2020 levels (recovery from the post-2022 reset, when ~30% of rounds were flat/down). [Carta Q1 2026](https://carta.com/data/state-of-private-markets-q1-2026/); [PitchBook down-round article](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/vc-startup-down-rounds-decade-high)
- **The barbell / disappearing middle:** pre-seed rounds <$250K rose steadily through 2025 to a record **35% in Q4 2025**, while the largest pre-seed financings kept growing — hot (mostly AI) deals command premium terms while everyone else clusters around $2–4M. AI cuts both ways: lets some startups do more with less, while capital-intensive AI models trigger mammoth pre-seed rounds. [Carta — disappearing middle](https://carta.com/data/disappearing-middle-pre-seed-market/)
- **Total early-stage activity (Q1 2026):** Carta tracked ~3,000 US startups raising pre-seed in Q1 2026 (~$2.3B, est. ~$2.9B once data settles); overall $30.4B raised in Q1 on Carta, with **>60% going to AI**. [Carta Q1 2026](https://carta.com/data/state-of-private-markets-q1-2026/); [Carta State of Pre-Seed Q1 2026](https://carta.com/data/state-of-pre-seed-q1-2026/)

## 4. Exit environment & dry powder

- **2025 US exit value: $297.8B, +92.7% YoY** — IPOs generated **$119.4B from just 62 IPOs**; acquisitions **$112.7B across 995 deals**. Globally exit value reached $549.2B (up >$200B over 2024). [SiliconANGLE / PitchBook](https://siliconangle.com/2026/01/07/pitchbook-ai-dominates-global-venture-capital-2025-deal-value-nears-record/)
- **IPO recovery is real but narrow:** strongest performers were AI/crypto/cyber. **CoreWeave** (Mar 2025 IPO, $40/share, raised $1.5B; up ~91%), **Circle** (June IPO $31, peaked $263, holders up ~140%), **Chime** (modest), **Figma** (July IPO $33, opened $115.50, later fell below IPO price by Jan 2026). VC-backed 2025 IPOs averaged ~450% post-debut returns vs 18–22% for PE-backed. [Inc. biggest 2025 IPOs](https://www.inc.com/libby-kane-2/biggest-ipos-2025-coreweave-figma-chime/91272994); [Crunchbase VC-backed IPOs 2025](https://news.crunchbase.com/public/venture-backed-ipos-2025-post-debut/)
- **Q1 2026 exit value: $347.3B — highest quarter on record**, but **xAI + Wiz exits = 81.2%** of it. Pending SpaceX/OpenAI/Anthropic IPOs could generate ~$2.5T in exit value (more than all VC-backed IPOs this century combined). [PitchBook Q1 2026](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q1-2026-pitchbook-nvca-venture-monitor); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/q1-2026-hit-big-vc-records-but-top-5-ai-deals-were-most-of-the-funding-pitchbook-nvca/)
- **Dry powder:** NVCA reported **~$307.8B in US VC unspent capital** (close to a record but receding amid slow fundraising). Large firms with dry powder are dominating and pushing into seed/early stages. [Talking Seed / NVCA](https://www.talkingseed.com/news/u-s-vc-trends-2025-record-dry-powder-deal-dynamics/); [Mahdlo $300B cash pile](https://www.mahdlo.net/blog/dry-powder-silicon-valley-investors-and-the-300-billion-cash-pile)
- **Fundraising weakness persists:** 2025 full-year US VC fundraising was only **$66.7B** (one of the lowest in a decade, hurt by weak liquidity/returns); but Q1 2026 already raised **$47.8B across 172 funds**, signaling recovery. [PitchBook Q1 2026](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q1-2026-pitchbook-nvca-venture-monitor)

## 5. Investor-quality signals — megafunds & most-active firms

- **Fundraising concentration:** In 2024, **9 firms collected ~half of all money raised by US VC funds**; General Catalyst + a16z alone captured ~44% of US VC fundraising. [PitchBook — 9 firms half of fundraising](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/us-vc-fundraising-concentration-andreessen-horowitz); [PitchBook — GC/a16z 44%](https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/general-catalyst-a16z-funds-lp-commitments)
- **2024 megafunds:** a16z ~$7B; General Catalyst ~$8B; Sequoia ~$950M (two early-stage funds); Thrive targeting $6–8B. [Newcomer](https://www.newcomer.co/p/big-vcs-raise-billions-in-fresh-capital)
- **2025 megafunds:** **a16z closed >$15B** (largest VC raise in Silicon Valley history; >$90B AUM); **Thrive Capital raised $10B** (its largest, ~double the prior); **Lightspeed**'s six-fund haul was the biggest venture raise of 2025; **Founders Fund** $4.5B growth fund; **Dragoneer** $4.3B. [a16z $15B](https://www.newcomer.co/p/andreessen-horowitzs-fresh-15-billion); [Value Add VC](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/a16z-15b-fund-vii-the-biggest-vc-raise-in-history-and-what-it-signals)
- **2026:** General Catalyst in talks to raise **$10B**; mega-funds "are back" (GC, Spark rumored to be raising billions). [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/11/vc-mega-funds-are-back-with-general-catalyst-spark-rumored-to-be-raising-billions/)
- **Most-active investors 2025 (Crunchbase):** Y Combinator, Andreessen Horowitz, Accel, General Catalyst, Sequoia (each >100 reported rounds); Lightspeed prominent in Q1; Khosla, Nvidia (corporate) also rising. The same longstanding names topped rankings and got busier. ## 6. Full 5-year window vs most-recent 3 years (separating ZIRP hype from durable momentum)

- **Full window (Jun 2021–Jun 2026):** Defined by the **2021 ZIRP bubble** ($330B, 17K deals, cheap money, tourist capital) → a **sharp 2022–2023 correction** (deal value ~–49% to $169B) → an **AI-driven re-acceleration** from 2024. The headline "VC is back" masks that 2025's near-record $339B is a *different* market from 2021: broad-based exuberance then vs. concentrated AI capital now.
- **Recent 3 years (Jun 2023–Jun 2026) — the durable signal:** This window is unambiguously **AI-concentrated and top-heavy**. AI share of US deal value went **47.2% (2024) → 65.6% (2025)** and higher in 2026; mega-rounds and a handful of foundation-model companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Databricks, Scale) absorb most dollars; **half of dollars hit 0.05% of deals**; Q1 2026 had 5 deals = 73% of value. Meanwhile the long tail starves (35% of pre-seed rounds <$250K), down rounds only normalized in 2025–26, fundraising hit decade lows in 2025, and exits — though record by value — are driven by 1–2 names (xAI + Wiz = 81% of Q1 2026 exit value).
- **Interpretation:** The post-correction momentum is *real in aggregate dollars* but is **not broad-based**. It is a barbell: enormous AI/infrastructure mega-deals at one end, a thin and underfunded long tail at the other, and a "disappearing middle." Durable conviction in the data points to **AI/AI-infrastructure, a small set of category-leading labs, and the top ~9 megafunds** — not to a generalized return of 2021-style risk appetite.

## Top themes visible in the data (where capital is concentrating)
1. **AI/ML is the market** — ~66% of US 2025 deal value, climbing in 2026; the most-funded vertical at every stage.
2. **Extreme deal concentration** — a few $1B+ AI rounds and 1–2 mega-exits drive headline totals; half of all dollars go to 0.05% of deals.
3. **Capital concentration among megafunds** — a16z ($15B), Thrive ($10B), Lightspeed, Founders Fund, General Catalyst control an outsized share; 9 firms ≈ half of all US fundraising (2024).
4. **The barbell / disappearing middle** — premium AI valuations at the top, a starved long tail (35% of pre-seed <$250K), and a hollowing middle.
5. **Exits recovering but narrow** — record 2025/Q1-2026 exit value, but concentrated in AI/crypto/cyber names and 1–2 blockbusters; broad IPO window still not fully open.
6. **Fundraising/liquidity lag** — record dry powder (~$308B) coexists with decade-low 2025 fundraising; recovery signs only in Q1 2026.

## Confidence note on data quality
- **High confidence** on PitchBook-NVCA annual deal value/count, AI shares, and Carta valuation/down-round figures — these come directly from the primary trackers or first-party reporting on them.
- **Medium confidence** on a few cross-tracker comparisons: PitchBook (US-only) and CB Insights/Crunchbase (often global) use different methodologies and geographies, so do not add their totals together (e.g., CB Insights' $469B global 2025 ≠ PitchBook's $339B US 2025). AI-share definitions also differ by tracker.
- **Caveats:** Q1 2026 figures are preliminary/"first look" and subject to upward revision (PitchBook routinely revises recent quarters up). 2024–2025 totals partly reflect retroactive data-completeness adjustments. The headline rebound is heavily skewed by a handful of names; medians and counts tell a more sober story than aggregate dollars.
