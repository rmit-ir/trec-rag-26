# US VC/Growth Investment in Energy / Climate / Power (June 2021–June 2026)

Weighted toward June 2023–June 2026. Focus: nuclear fission (SMRs), fusion, grid/storage, geothermal, and the AI-data-center-power nexus.

---

## 0. Headline thesis (the through-line)

The dominant story of 2024–2026 is that **AI compute demand reframed energy/power as the single highest-conviction "hard tech" VC theme**, displacing the broad, diffuse 2021–2022 "climate tech / ESG" wave. Capital concentrated into a small number of very large rounds in **firm, baseload, clean (or at least carbon-free) power** — nuclear fission SMRs, fusion, and enhanced geothermal — explicitly underwritten by hyperscaler power demand and offtake/PPA agreements.

- Data-centre electricity demand surged **~17% in 2025**; data-centre power consumption is set to **double by 2030**, with AI-focused load poised to **triple**. ([IEA](https://www.iea.org/news/data-centre-electricity-use-surged-in-2025-even-with-tightening-bottlenecks-driving-a-scramble-for-solutions))
- Physical grid-scale power is now described as the **primary bottleneck** for data-center expansion. ([Energy IB](https://ibinterviewquestions.com/guides/energy-investment-banking/data-center-power-boom-ai-demand-hyperscaler); [Morgan Stanley](https://www.morganstanley.com/insights/articles/powering-ai-energy-market-outlook-2026); [WEF](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2026/05/electricity-data-grid-connectivity-strategic-bottleneck-ai-transformation/))
- The five largest tech companies' capex topped **$400B in 2025** and is set to rise ~75% in 2026. ([IEA](https://www.iea.org/news/data-centre-electricity-use-surged-in-2025-even-with-tightening-bottlenecks-driving-a-scramble-for-solutions))

## 2. Nuclear Fusion

Total private fusion investment crossed **~$13B+ cumulative by 2025** (€13B ≈ $15B per FIA late-2025); **17 fusion startups have each raised >$100M**. US and China are the global leaders. The framing: AI data-center power demand "turns fusion from speculative science into a credible infrastructure play." ## 5. The AI-Data-Center-Power Nexus (the reframing — Q1)

How AI compute demand turned energy into a high-conviction VC theme 2024–2026:

- **Hyperscaler nuclear deals** became the validating events that re-rated the whole space:
  - **Microsoft / Constellation (Sep 20, 2024):** 20-yr PPA to restart **Three Mile Island Unit 1 (Crane Clean Energy Center, 835 MW)**, dedicated to Microsoft AI data centers in PJM. Constellation's largest-ever PPA; reportedly ~$16B value; service ~2027–2028. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/09/20/constellation-energy-to-restart-three-mile-island-and-sell-the-power-to-microsoft.html); [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001868275/000186827524000058/ceg-202409208kexh991.htm))
  - **Amazon / X-energy** (~$500M, 5+ GW by 2039) — see §1.
  - **Google / Kairos** (up to 7 SMRs, 500 MW) and **Google / CFS** (200 MW fusion PPA), **Google / Fervo** (geothermal equity) — Google diversified across fission, fusion, and geothermal.
  - **Meta** RFP for up to 4 GW nuclear; deals/intent with TerraPower (8 plants), Oklo, Constellation, Vistra (up to 6.6 GW total).
- **VCs followed the hyperscalers.** Nvidia's venture arm (NVentures) invested in CFS; SoftBank, Lightspeed, General Catalyst, Breakthrough Energy, and AI-aligned individuals (Altman, Schmidt, Hoffman, Suleyman, Collison) anchored the largest fission/fusion rounds.
- **Mechanism:** offtake PPAs and corporate strategic equity de-risk these long-dated, capital-intensive bets, giving VCs a credible path to revenue and an "infrastructure" (not "science project") framing.
- Energy's share of climate-tech funding rose to ~35% in 2024 (from 30% in 2023); AI-climate ventures raised $1B+ more in 9 months of 2024 than in all of 2023, "driven by the anticipated rise in energy consumption due to AI." ([carboncredits](https://carboncredits.com/climate-tech-vc-investments-drop-for-the-3rd-year-in-a-row-ai-to-the-rescue/))

## 6. Contrast with 2021–2022 "Climate Tech / ESG" Wave (Q2) — ZIRP hype vs durable?

**The 2021–2022 wave was substantially ZIRP-fueled and broad; the 2024–2026 wave is narrower, demand-anchored, and corporate-validated.**

- **2021–2022 boom & bust:** At least **69 climate-tech companies went public via SPAC in 2021**. By early 2023 they were down **~70%** from the start of 2022, underperforming the Nasdaq by >2x. Only **2 of 69** (NuScale, Dragonfly) stayed above the $10 SPAC price. SPACs broadly drove **≥21 bankruptcies in 2023** and ~$46B of lost investor value. Notable casualties: **Nikola (bankruptcy 2025), Heliogen** (once ~$2B, sold for $10M), Electric Last Mile, Fast Radius. ([ctvc SPACback](https://www.ctvc.co/a-brief-climate-tech-spacback/); [Fortune SPACs](https://fortune.com/2023/12/27/spacs-investor-losses-ipo-wework-lordstown/); [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/issues/esg/climate-tech-investment-adaptation-ai.html))
- **2023 correction (ZIRP reversal):** With ZIRP over, capital-intensive "real asset" deployment looked expensive. H1'23 climate funding **$13.1B, down 40% YoY**; full-year 2023 **$32B, down 30%**. **Growth-stage funding collapsed ~64%** (vs ~30% for VC growth broadly). ([ctvc H1'23](https://www.ctvc.co/climate-tech-h1-2023-venture-funding/); [ctvc 2023](https://www.ctvc.co/32bn-and-30-drop-as-market-hits-pause-in-2023/))
- **The shift:** The recovery is concentrated — fewer, bigger rounds in **energy/firm power** specifically. The new wave is **demand-pulled by an identifiable, contracted buyer (hyperscalers with PPAs)** rather than ESG mandates and cheap money. This is the key durability argument: a Microsoft/Constellation or Amazon/X-energy 10–20-yr PPA is a hard demand signal that a 2021 hydrogen/EV SPAC never had.
- Caveat — SPACs are *back* (2025: $25.8B raised, ~3x 2024; deep-tech/nuclear candidates like Oklo, TAE/TMTG), which carries some echo of the 2021 froth. ([ctvc SPACback](https://www.ctvc.co/a-brief-climate-tech-spacback/))

## 7. Implied Thesis & Future Assumptions Investors Underwrite (Q3)

1. **Electricity demand supercycle.** Decades of flat US power demand are over; AI + electrification drive a structural step-change (data-centre demand doubling by 2030; 241+ GW global data-center pipeline by end-2025, +159% YoY). ([Energy IB](https://ibinterviewquestions.com/guides/energy-investment-banking/data-center-power-boom-ai-demand-hyperscaler); [IEA](https://www.iea.org/news/data-centre-electricity-use-surged-in-2025-even-with-tightening-bottlenecks-driving-a-scramble-for-solutions))
2. **Firm/baseload clean power wins.** Intermittent solar+wind can't supply 24/7 AI load; the premium is on **firm, dispatchable, carbon-free** power — nuclear (fission/fusion), geothermal, long-duration storage. Hyperscalers' carbon-free-energy goals + 24/7 needs make nuclear/geothermal the natural answer.
3. **Energy is THE bottleneck to AI.** Compute is gated by power and grid interconnection, not chips alone — so whoever supplies firm power captures outsized value (utilities re-rated from "defensive" to "growth"). ([Morgan Stanley](https://www.morganstanley.com/insights/articles/powering-ai-energy-market-outlook-2026); [WEF](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2026/05/electricity-data-grid-connectivity-strategic-bottleneck-ai-transformation/))
4. **Policy is a durable tailwind, not a subsidy crutch.** Bipartisan **ADVANCE Act (2024)** + four **Trump nuclear EOs (May 2025)** target NRC reform and **400 GW nuclear by 2050** (4x from 100 GW in 2024); DOE Reactor Pilot Program, reopened ~$900M SMR power-purchase fund. IRA tech-neutral clean-power credits underpinned 2022–2024 economics. ([energy.gov](https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/9-key-takeaways-president-trumps-executive-orders-nuclear-energy); [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/trump-aims-for-400-gw-of-nuclear-by-2050-10-large-reactors-under-construct/749107/); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/05/president-trump-signs-4-executive-orders))
5. **Offtake before commercial proof.** Investors underwrite milestone/tranche structures (Pacific Fusion) and pre-revenue PPAs (Oklo/Equinix, CFS/Google+Eni, Helion/Microsoft) — betting that contracted demand bridges the multi-year tech-risk gap.

## 8. Durability Post-Correction (Q4)

**Evidence FOR durability:**
- Demand is real and contracted (PPAs with creditworthy hyperscalers), not ESG-mandate-driven; energy's share of climate funding rose even as deal count fell.
- Policy is bipartisan and supply-side (permitting reform), reducing a key historical risk for nuclear.
- Capital is concentrating in fewer, larger, better-capitalized leaders (CFS, Helion, Oklo, Fervo, Form, X-energy) rather than spraying across thin SPACs.
- Strategic corporates (Nvidia, Google, Amazon, SoftBank, Devon, Mitsubishi, Hitachi, NRG) are anchoring rounds — patient, strategic capital vs. 2021 momentum capital.
- Successful liquidity: Fervo (~$10B IPO, May 2026), X-energy (~$1B IPO), Oklo (~$10B+ public co.) show real exit paths.

**Risks / fragility:**
- **Timeline risk:** fission SMRs target 2027–2030+; fusion power ~2028+ (Helion) and ARC plants early-2030s — all *pre-revenue / pre-commercial*. Net energy, neutron-resilient materials, cycle durability, and tritium fuel cycle remain unsolved at scale for fusion. ([FIA/ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/2025-12-05/article-7599/report-funding-growth-for-private-fusion-companies/); [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2024-10-nuclear-fusion-energy-requires-resilient.html))
- **Valuation froth:** Oklo +22,000% on no commercial revenue; Fervo IPO pop; SPAC resurgence (TAE/TMTG) echoes 2021 — bubble risk if AI capex decelerates.
- **AI-demand dependency:** the entire thesis rides on AI capex continuing; an AI investment slowdown or efficiency gains (e.g., cheaper inference) could deflate the power-demand premium. ([IEA executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary))
- **Storage VC equity actually fell 60% in 2024** — not every "energy" subsector is durable; capital is rotating to project/debt finance.

**Net:** more durable than the 2021–2022 wave because anchored to contracted, identifiable demand and supply-side policy — but concentrated in a handful of pre-revenue, long-dated, high-valuation bets whose durability is hostage to (a) the AI capex cycle and (b) execution on hard physics/engineering milestones.
