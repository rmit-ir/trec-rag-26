## 0. The Macro Picture: "Physical AI" Surge (2024–2026)

- Robotics companies raised a **record ~$40.7B in 2025, ~9% of all global VC funding**, putting robotics/physical AI among the leading sectors alongside AI software. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/21/robots-physical-intelligence-ai)
- In the first nine months of 2025 alone, physical-AI scaleups raised **>$16B**. Funding for "world models" (models that predict spatial/physical interactions) jumped from ~€1.3B in 2024 to ~€6.5B in 2025. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/21/robots-physical-intelligence-ai)
- A separate tally puts 2025 robotics funding at **>$10.3B (highest since 2021)** — the apparent discrepancy reflects narrow "professional robotics" vs broad "physical AI incl. AVs/humanoids" definitions; both confirm a record year. [New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/robotics-funding-analysis)
- Concentration is extreme: top deal = 22.4% of capital, top 3 = 55.5%, top 10 = 89.0% of robotics capital over the trailing 12 months — a barbell of mega-rounds plus a long tail. [New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/robotics-funding-analysis)
- Nvidia framed "physical AI" in Jan 2026 as models that "understand the real world, reason, and plan actions," releasing open models/frameworks for robot learning & simulation — anchoring the thesis at the infrastructure layer. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/21/robots-physical-intelligence-ai)

- 2024 saw concerted reshoring push (tariffs, geopolitics, supply-chain resilience), but selective — mostly high-tech/EV/semis. [Altios](https://altios.com/publication/the-quiet-comeback-of-american-manufacturing-are-you-late-to-the-reshoring-game/)

### Semiconductors (mostly policy + capex, NOT classic VC)
- **CHIPS Act**: $50B total ($39B mfg incentives, $11B R&D); catalyzed >$500B private investment; domestic capacity projected to ~triple by 2032. Wafer-fab capacity is financed via state programs/corporate capex/balance sheets, **not venture equity**. [ainvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/reshoring-semiconductor-manufacturing-strategic-industrial-policy-long-term-competitive-advantage-2512/)
- Where VC IS active: adjacent chip tech — **Ayar Labs ~$680M across 3 rounds in 16 months** (optical I/O), **Recursive/Ricursive Intelligence $300M Series A** (AI chip-design automation), **Eridu $200M Series A** (AI-DC network switches). $100M+ chip rounds rose to **12 in Q4'25, 11 in Q3'25** vs ~6/quarter historically. [Semiconductor Engineering Q4'25](https://semiengineering.com/startup-funding-q4-2025/) · [New Market Pitch (semis)](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/semiconductor-funding-analysis)

## 7. The Implied Thesis & Future Assumptions Investors Underwrite

1. **"AI moving from bits to atoms"** — having largely solved digital intelligence (LLMs), the next trillion-dollar frontier is embodiment; foundation models generalize to physical action. (Figure/PI/Skild all sell a "robot brain" generalization story.) [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/21/robots-physical-intelligence-ai)
2. **Humanoids in factories/warehouses first, then homes** — general-purpose form factor fits human-designed environments; manufacturing/intralogistics are the wedge (Figure 100k units; Apptronik w/ Mercedes & GXO; 1X enterprise pivot). [Robotics 24/7](https://www.robotics247.com/article/2025-robotics-trends-humanoids-enter-commercial-use-logistics-and-automation-rise)
3. **Structural labor shortage = durable demand floor** — >600k US mfg + >1M logistics openings underwrite robots as additive, not just substitutive. [automate.org](https://www.automate.org/industry-insights/humanoid-robots-are-evolving)
4. **Autonomy reaches scale & unit economics** — Waymo's ride/revenue ramp is the proof that AV can commercialize; Aurora replicates in trucking. Investors underwrite the curve bending from "demo" to "fleet." [Driverless Digest](https://www.thedriverlessdigest.com/p/waymos-2025-year-in-review-the-year)
5. **National-security / reshoring imperative** — supply-chain resilience + a "generational fight" frame justifies industrial-scale capex into domestic automated manufacturing & defense autonomy (Anduril, Hadrian, CHIPS). [CNBC/Anduril](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/anduril-valuation-defense-tech-funding-boom.html) · [TechCrunch/Hadrian](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/17/hadrian-raises-260m-to-build-out-automated-factories-for-space-and-defense-parts/)
6. **Compute/infra picks-and-shovels** — Nvidia's strategic-investor role across Figure, Skild, Wayve, Nuro, Waabi reflects an underwrite that physical AI is the next compute demand engine. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/02/nvidias-ai-empire-a-look-at-its-top-startup-investments/)

## 8. Durability Post-Correction (the prior AV winter)

- **The cautionary precedent (2022–2024 "AV winter")**: Ford & VW shut **Argo AI** (Oct 2022) after >$3.6B invested; Ford took a $2.7B impairment, VW €1.9B — driven by escalating costs, slow commercialization, no new investors. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/26/ford-vw-backed-argo-ai-is-shutting-down/) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2022/10/27/ford-volkswagen-pull-plug-robocar-unit-argo-ai-major-setback-self-driving-plans/)
- **GM shut Cruise** (late 2024), ending its robotaxi ambitions after a 2023 pedestrian-dragging incident & permit suspension. [Road to Autonomy](https://www.roadtoautonomy.com/gm-pulls-plug-cruise/)
- **Durability read**: the current wave is more durable than the 2022 cycle because (a) at least one player (Waymo) has demonstrated real commercial scale & 200M+ driverless miles, settling the core tech question; (b) foundation-model maturity + cheaper hardware created a genuine capability step-change in 2025; (c) labor-shortage + reshoring tailwinds provide demand that didn't exist in the 2018–22 hype. BUT extreme valuation concentration (top 10 = 89% of capital), pre-revenue humanoid valuations ($39B Figure, $14B Skild), and Tesla Optimus execution slippage are clear bubble-risk markers. [New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/robotics-funding-analysis) · [futuresearch.ai/Waymo](https://futuresearch.ai/waymo-financial-forecast/)
