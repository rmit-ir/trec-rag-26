Sub-agent strand: AI application/agent layer. All figures are as reported by cited sources; cross-check before final use. "ARR" figures are mostly annualized run-rate self-reported by companies/secondary trackers and should be treated as directional.

## 0. Headline thesis — the 2025–2026 shift from "AI infra" to "AI application/agent" investing

- VCs are explicitly "spreading bets beyond model companies" into the application/agent layer — framing used by CNBC in covering Harvey's $11B round. ([CNBC, Mar 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/25/legal-ai-startup-harvey-raises-200-million-at-11-billion-valuation.html))
- "Service-as-software" is the dominant pricing thesis: when agents deliver end-to-end outcomes (a resolved ticket, a processed invoice, a qualified lead), the pricing unit shifts from **seats/logins to outcomes/usage**. Bain sizes the US "cross-system labor" opportunity at ~$100B, of which only ~$4–6B is currently captured (>90% untapped). ([Bain & Company](https://www.bain.com/insights/100-billion-saas-opportunity-hiding-in-cross-system-labor/))
- 354 companies in the agentic-AI space had collectively raised ~$228B (publicly disclosed) as of Nov 2025; investors increasingly reward "measurable workflow replacement, security, execution infrastructure, and enterprise control points" and are "less patient with generic agent wrappers." ([New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/agentic-ai-funding-trends))
- Bessemer frames the frontier as "AI systems of action" / "digital labor" — agents that *do work*, not just assist. ([Bessemer "Roadmap: AI systems of action"](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/roadmap-ai-systems-of-action))
- 2026 widely branded "the year of agentic AI"; agentic market projected ~$7.6B (2025) → ~$10.8B (2026) → $139–196B by 2034. ([Nextgov](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2025/12/2026-set-be-year-agentic-ai-industry-predicts/410324/); [Svitla](https://svitla.com/blog/agentic-ai-market-trends-2026/))

## 6. ARR-multiple / valuation behavior (signal #2)
- Most AI startups: **10–50x** revenue; median ~20–30x. Gen-AI/LLM/core infra 40–50x; rare 100x outliers (OpenAI, Anthropic). ([qubit.capital](https://qubit.capital/blog/ai-startup-valuation-multiples); [Beyond Elevation](https://beyondelevation.com/blog/posts/ai-startup-revenue-multiples-by-stage-2026/))
- Application-layer leaders carry very high implied multiples on run-rate ARR: e.g. Cursor ~$50B / ~$2B ARR ≈ 25x; Sierra ~$15.8B / ~$200M ≈ ~80x; Harvey $11B / ~$190M ≈ ~58x; Decagon $4.5B / small ARR = triple-digit. (Computed from cited valuation + ARR figures above — directional only.)
- **Bifurcation (2026):** foundation-model multiples compressed from 60–100x toward 15–50x as revenue caught up; "real bubble risk" concentrated in (a) robotics on demo hype (Figure AI ~400x), and (b) thin app "wrappers" without proprietary data/distribution, which saw 50–70% multiple compression. **Defensibility overtook growth rate** as the primary valuation driver. ([valueaddvc](https://valueaddvc.com/ai-valuations); [Beyond Elevation](https://beyondelevation.com/blog/posts/ai-startup-revenue-multiples-by-stage-2026/))

## 7. Implied thesis / future assumptions investors underwrite (signal #3)
1. **Labor/seat replacement → "service-as-software":** value priced on *work done* (resolved tickets, drafted contracts, written code, clinical notes), tapping labor budgets far larger than software budgets. ([Bain](https://www.bain.com/insights/100-billion-saas-opportunity-hiding-in-cross-system-labor/))
2. **Agents do end-to-end work autonomously** ("systems of action" / "digital labor"), not just assist. ([Bessemer](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/roadmap-ai-systems-of-action))
3. **Vertical/domain depth + proprietary data + enterprise control points = durable moat** vs. thin wrappers. ([New Market Pitch](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/agentic-ai-funding-trends); [valueaddvc](https://valueaddvc.com/ai-valuations))
4. **Continued hyper-growth justifies premium multiples** — underwriting ARR roughly doubling annually (Cursor, Glean, Sierra, Harvey all cited doubling/tripling).
