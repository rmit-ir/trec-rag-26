## 1. The Rise of "American Dynamism" as an Explicit VC Category

- **a16z's American Dynamism (AD) practice** was launched as the firm's explicit category for companies "supporting the national interest" across aerospace, defense, manufacturing, energy, public safety, supply chain, and critical infrastructure — companies that view government as a customer, competitor, or key stakeholder. Co-founded by **Katherine Boyle** and **David Ulevitch**; Boyle's 2022 "Building American Dynamism" thesis became its philosophical anchor. [a16z](https://a16z.com/american-dynamism/), [Acquired](https://www.acquired.fm/episodes/american-dynamism-with-katherine-boyle)
- The thesis crystallized from a realization inside a16z that portfolio companies like **Anduril, Shield AI, and SpaceX** — which sell to the federal government — shared deep similarities and didn't fit "typical enterprise or consumer" boxes. [Acquired](https://www.acquired.fm/episodes/american-dynamism-with-katherine-boyle)
- **Dedicated AD fund: $600M**, launched ~2023. In the **January 2026** raise, a16z's $7.2B haul again allocated **$600M to American Dynamism** (alongside Apps $1B, Games $600M, Infrastructure $1.25B, Growth $3.75B) — fund size held consistent. [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/a16z-15b-new-funds-american-dynamism-ben-horowitz/), [a16z New Funds](https://a16z.com/new-funds-new-era/)
- AD early/anchor investments: **Anduril, Flock Safety, Hadrian, Shield AI, SpaceX**. [Sourcery](https://www.sourcery.vc/p/635m-exit-600m-fund-the-critical)
- **Defense is now a fundable VC sector** ("neoprime" thesis): a class of software-first startups (Anduril, Palantir, Saronic, Shield AI) challenging legacy primes Lockheed/Boeing/Raytheon. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/03/silicon-valley-defense-tech-startups-war-lockheed-boeing-raytheon-anduril-palantir-mva-milvet.html), [Cipher Brief](https://www.thecipherbrief.com/defense-neoprime-innovation)

## 7. Implied Thesis & Future Assumptions Investors Underwrite

1. **Great-power competition is durable** (China/Russia) → sustained, rising defense budgets for ≥a decade.
2. **Software-defined / hardware-enabled warfare:** software is the moat; hardware realizes it. Counter-positions legacy "hardware-defined, software-enabled" primes. [Syntax](https://www.syntaxdata.com/research/anduril-the-software-first-prime-defense-contractor-defining-the-modern-frontline), [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/anduril)
3. **Mass + attrition + autonomy** define future conflict → attritable autonomous systems (drones, ships, CCA) at scale; AI fleets across all domains. [Bessemer](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/defense-tech-roadmap-five-frontiers-for-2026)
4. **Reindustrialization:** domestic hyperscale manufacturing (Arsenal-1, Port Alpha, Project Ranger, Hadrian) as a moat and policy mandate. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/18/palmer-luckey-anduril-arsenal-drones)
5. **Government as anchor customer:** the neoprimes win prime-like contract share from incumbents.

## 8. Durability Post-Correction

- Unlike the 2022 broad-tech correction, defense valuations have **risen sharply through 2024–2026**: Anduril $14B (2024) → $30.5B (2025) → ~$60B (2026 rumored); Shield AI $5.3B → $12.7B (+140% in a year); Saronic $1B → $4B → $9.25B; Apex to $2.3B.
- **SpaceX secondaries doubled** ($400B Jul 2025 → ~$800B Dec 2025) with IPO chatter at $1.75–2T.
- **New entrants keep raising mega-rounds** (Stoke, K2, Castelion, Hadrian), and **crossover/PE capital** (Advent, Blackstone, JPMorgan, Kleiner, Thrive) is institutionalizing the category — a durability signal beyond core defense VCs.
- **Fund formation** confirms staying power: Lux $1.5B Fund IX + dedicated defense fund; a16z AD re-funded at $600M.
