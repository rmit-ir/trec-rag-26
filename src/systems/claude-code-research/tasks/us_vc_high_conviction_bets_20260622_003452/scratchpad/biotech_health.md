# US VC/Growth Investment in Biotech / Healthcare / TechBio (Jun 2021–Jun 2026)

Scope: US venture & growth investment, weighted to Jun 2023–Jun 2026. Segments: AI drug discovery/"TechBio", AI in healthcare delivery, digital health, longevity, biomanufacturing. Captures the AI×biology convergence thesis, 2021-bubble vs 2024–2026 contrast, implied future assumptions, durability post-correction, and a confidence note.

---

## 1. AI Drug Discovery / "TechBio"

The defining high-conviction theme of 2023–2026: using AI/ML (protein language models, structure prediction, generative chemistry, perturbation models) to compress drug discovery timelines and cost. Capital concentrated in a few very large, scientist-founder-led platforms.

### Xaira Therapeutics — the flagship bet
- **$1B launch (April 2024)** — largest initial commitment in ARCH Venture Partners history; co-incubated by **ARCH** and **Foresite Labs** (Foresite). [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/new-ai-drug-discovery-powerhouse-xaira-rises-1b-funding), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/24/xaira-an-ai-drug-discovery-startup-launches-with-a-massive-1b-says-its-ready-to-start-developing-drugs/)
- Investor syndicate: F-Prime, NEA, Sequoia Capital, Lux Capital, Lightspeed, Menlo Ventures, Two Sigma Ventures, SV Angel. [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/new-ai-drug-discovery-powerhouse-xaira-rises-1b-funding)
- Co-founded by **David Baker** (Institute for Protein Design; RFdiffusion/RFantibody — 2024 Nobel Chemistry in protein design); CEO **Marc Tessier-Lavigne** (ex-Stanford president, ex-Genentech CSO). [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/24/xaira-an-ai-drug-discovery-startup-launches-with-a-massive-1b-says-its-ready-to-start-developing-drugs/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/04/25/ai-for-drug-discovery-xaira-therapeutics/)
- 2025 progress: built out leadership (CMO Paulo Fontoura ex-Roche; Bo Wang as Head of Biomedical AI); focus on inflammation/immunology + antibody therapeutics; relocated HQ to South San Francisco; revealed **X-Cell**, a virtual cell model trained on what it calls the largest-ever genome-wide perturbation dataset. Still operating on the single 2024 $1B round (no disclosed follow-on as of mid-2026; some trackers list ~$1.3B total raised). [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/ai-and-machine-learning/behind-curtain-what-xaira-building-after-its-1b-fundraise), [Tracxn/PitchBook profiles]

### Isomorphic Labs — Alphabet/DeepMind spinout
- **$600M first external round (March 31, 2025)** led by **Thrive Capital**, with **GV** and follow-on from existing investor **Alphabet**. [Isomorphic](https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/articles/isomorphic-labs-announces-600m-external-investment-round), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/31/alphabets-ai-drug-discovery-platform-isomorphic-labs-raises-600m-from-thrive/)
- Spun out of Google DeepMind (2021); founded by **Demis Hassabis**; built on AlphaFold. Hassabis + John Jumper won the **2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** — institutional validation of the platform's science. [pharmaphorum](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/alphabets-isomorphic-raises-600m-ai-drug-discovery), [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/alphabets-isomorphic-stacks-two-new-deals-lilly-novartis-worth-nearly-3b-ahead-buzzy-jpm)
- **Strategic pharma deals (Jan 2024):** Eli Lilly (up to **$1.7B**) and Novartis (up to **$1.2B**) — nearly **$3B** combined in potential milestones, ex-royalties; Novartis scope later expanded. [FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/alphabets-isomorphic-stacks-two-new-deals-lilly-novartis-worth-nearly-3b-ahead-buzzy-jpm)
- **2026 reports:** in advanced talks for a further round **>$2B** led by Thrive + Alphabet. [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/private-markets/26/05/52464001/thrive-capital-alphabet-to-lead-isomorphic-labs-2-billion-fundraising-goal)

### Recursion Pharmaceuticals (public; consolidation signal)
- **Recursion + Exscientia merger closed Nov 20, 2024** (~$565M–$688M all-stock; Exscientia became a subsidiary; EXAI delisted, 0.7729 RXRX/share). Created a consolidated AI drug-discovery platform. [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/11/20/2984282/0/en/Recursion-and-Exscientia-two-leaders-in-the-AI-drug-discovery-space-have-officially-combined-to-advance-the-industrialization-of-drug-discovery.html), [BioPharma/DrugDiscoveryTrends](https://www.drugdiscoverytrends.com/recursion-and-exscientia-merge-in-688-m-deal-to-create-consolidated-ai-driven-drug-discovery-platform/)
- Combined: ~800 employees, >60 petabytes proprietary data, >10 clinical/preclinical + >10 partnered programs; >$450M upfront/milestones realized of >$20B possible. Pharma partners: **Bayer (~$1.5B), Genentech, Sanofi, Merck**. [GlobeNewswire](https://ir.recursion.com/news-releases/news-release-details/recursion-and-exscientia-two-leaders-ai-drug-discovery-space), [Pharmaceutical Technology](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/analyst-comment/big-tech-meets-biotech-recursion-ai-gold-rush-pharma/)
- Signals a shift from "raise on a platform story" toward consolidation/industrialization and partnered validation.

### Genesis Therapeutics — generative chemistry
- **$200M Series B (Aug 2023)**, oversubscribed; co-led by **a16z Bio+Health** (which led the seed) with a US life-sciences investor. New investors: Fidelity, BlackRock, **NVIDIA NVentures**; existing: T. Rowe Price, Rock Springs Capital, Radical Ventures, Menlo. Total raised >$280M. [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230821419353/en/Genesis-Therapeutics-Closes-Oversubscribed-$200-Million-Series-B), [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/genesis-series-b-ai-drug-discovery/691348/), [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/ai-robotics/a16z-backed-ai-biotech-startup-genesis-therapeutics-raises-200m/)
- Platform: GEMS (Genesis Exploration of Molecular Space) — generative + predictive AI; advancing own pipeline to clinic.

### EvolutionaryScale — foundation model for biology
- **$142M+ seed (June 2024)** on launch from stealth, led by **Nat Friedman & Daniel Gross (NFDG)** and **Lux Capital**, with Amazon and **NVIDIA NVentures**. [Amazon Press](https://press.aboutamazon.com/aws/2024/6/evolutionaryscale-launches-with-esm3-a-milestone-ai-model-for-biology), [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/evolutionaryscale-emerges-from-stealth-with-esm3-the-largest-protein-language-model-and-142m-seed-round/)
- Released **ESM3**, then the largest protein language model (trained on 2.78B proteins); generated a novel GFP equivalent to "500M years of evolution." Founded by ex-Meta FAIR protein-modeling team. [Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.ads0018)
- Notable: a >$100M seed for a pre-revenue foundation-model company — pure conviction in AI-for-biology infrastructure.

**Convergence thesis (synthesized):** Investors underwrite that AI/ML can (a) predict structure/function (AlphaFold lineage), (b) generatively design proteins/antibodies/small molecules (Baker/RFdiffusion, ESM3, GEMS), and (c) model cellular response to perturbations (Xaira X-Cell), collectively compressing the multi-year, multi-billion-dollar discovery funnel and improving hit rates. Validation signals: 2024 Nobel in Chemistry (AlphaFold + protein design), and multi-billion-dollar pharma option/milestone deals that de-risk the platforms commercially. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/04/25/ai-for-drug-discovery-xaira-therapeutics/), [labiotech](https://www.labiotech.eu/best-biotech/ai-drug-discovery-companies/)

## 2. AI in Healthcare Delivery (clinical documentation / scribes, clinical decision, RCM)

The other 2024–2026 high-conviction theme — fastest-growing, clearest-ROI software in health. AI moved from "majority of digital health VC funding for the first time."

### Ambient AI clinical documentation / scribes
- Market: AI medical scribe market crossed **$1.5B in 2025**, growing ~25%/yr; **>$1B in VC** flowed into ambient scribe companies in 2025 alone. Market share (2025): **Microsoft/Nuance ~33%, Abridge ~30%, Ambience ~13%, Suki ~10%, Nabla ~4%, Freed ~4%**. [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/ambient-ai-scribes-by-market-share/)
- **Abridge** — defining scaling story:
  - **$250M Series D (Feb 2025)** at **$2.75B**; then **$300M Series E (June 2025)** led by **a16z** with Khosla Ventures et al. at **$5.3B** — valuation ~doubled in 4 months. [Abridge](https://www.abridge.com/blog/series-e), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/24/in-just-4-months-ai-medical-scribe-abridge-doubles-valuation-to-5-3b/), [STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2025/06/24/ai-clinical-documentation-ambient-scribe-abridge-raises-300-million/)
  - Then a reported **~$316M Series E extension (April 2026)** at $5.3B. [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/abridge-secures-300m-boosts-valuation-53b)
  - Traction: deployed in 150+ health systems; **~$100M ARR by May 2025** (up from $60M end-2024, per Sacra). Expanding into revenue-cycle intelligence & prior auth. [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/abridge/), [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/abridge-secures-300m-to-transform-clinical-documentation-and-revenue-cycle-management-2025-06-25)
  - Clinical evidence: Yale study, 263 physicians, 6 systems — burnout 51.9%→38.8% in 30 days. [Becker's]
- **Ambience Healthcare** — **$243M Series C (July 2025)** expanding into revenue integrity/coding. [Becker's market-share source]
- **Suki** — **$70M Series D (Oct 2024)** led by Hedosophia at ~$500M; total ~$165M. [Becker's / market guide]
- **Microsoft/Nuance** — incumbent; merged DAX ambient capture + Dragon Medical One into **Dragon Copilot (Mar 2025)**. [FierceHealthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/how-epics-ai-moves-could-shake-health-tech-market)

### Clinical decision support / "ChatGPT for doctors"
- **OpenEvidence** — fastest-growing physician app; ~**$700M raised across three rounds in ~12 months**:
  - **$210M Series B (July 2025)** at **$3.5B**, co-led by **GV** and **Kleiner Perkins**, Sequoia (Series A lead) following. [OpenEvidence](https://www.openevidence.com/announcements/openevidence-the-fastest-growing-application-for-physicians-in-history-announces-dollar210-million-round-at-dollar35-billion-valuation), [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/openevidence-raises-210m-unveils-ai-agents-built-advanced-medical-research)
  - **$200M Series C** at **$6B** led by GV (Sequoia, Kleiner, Blackstone, Thrive, Coatue, BOND, Craft). [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/openevidence/)
  - **$250M Series D (Jan 2026)** at **$12B** — valuation doubled again. [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/openevidence-ai-doctors-doubles-valuation-seriesd/), [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260121029132/en/OpenEvidence-Raises-$250-Million-to-Build-Medical-Superintelligence-for-Doctors)
  - Revenue: ~**$150M annualized in 2025**, up **1,803%** from $7.9M in 2024, ~90% gross margins. [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/openevidence/)

**Thesis (delivery):** AI software that removes administrative burden (documentation, coding, RCM, prior auth) and augments clinical judgment has fast, demonstrable ROI and viral bottom-up clinician adoption — closer to enterprise SaaS economics than to biotech R&D risk. This is why a16z Bio+Health, GV, Kleiner, Sequoia, Thrive compete for it at SaaS-like multiples.

## 3. Digital Health (broad)

- **2025 US digital health VC: $14.2B**, +35% over 2024's $10.5B; highest since 2022 — but **not** a return to the 2021 record. [Rock Health 2025 year-end](https://rockhealth.com/insights/2025-year-end-digital-health-funding-overview-a-tale-of-two-markets/)
- **AI dominance:** AI-enabled digital health captured **54% of total funding in 2025** (up from 37% in 2024), with ~19% deal-size premium; in H1 2025, AI-core companies were **62%** of funding — a majority for the first time. [Rock Health](https://rockhealth.com/insights/h1-2025-market-overview-proof-in-the-pudding/), [Modern Healthcare](https://www.modernhealthcare.com/health-tech/mh-ai-digital-health-2025-rock-health/)
- **Concentration / barbell:** mega-deals (>$100M) = **42%** of all 2025 funding (highest since 2021, ~double 2024's share). Removing the top 9 companies, total 2025 funding falls below 2024 — extreme dependence on a few winners. [Rock Health 2025 year-end](https://rockhealth.com/insights/2025-year-end-digital-health-funding-overview-a-tale-of-two-markets/)
- 2024 was the trough year: digital health funding fell again to $10.5B. [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/digital-health-funding-declines-2024-rock-health/737476/)

## 4. Longevity / Cellular Reprogramming

A distinct, billionaire-anchored high-conviction cluster betting on partial/epigenetic reprogramming to reverse cellular aging.

- **Altos Labs** — launched **Jan 2022 with $3B** initial financing (Bezos, Yuri Milner among backers) — most heavily funded biotech launch ever. In vivo cellular rejuvenation/reprogramming; founders Rick Klausner, Hans Bishop. Becoming less stealthy; possibly gearing toward clinical trials by 2026. [Longevity.Technology](https://longevity.technology/news/altos-labs-lands-3-billion-to-further-cellular-rejuvenation-programming/), [Fight Aging!](https://www.fightaging.org/archives/2022/01/altos-labs-officially-launches-with-3-billion-in-funding-to-tackle-in-vivo-reprogramming/), [MIT Tech Review (Jun 2026)](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/12/1138829/reprogramming-buzziest-approach-reversing-aging-right-now/)
- **Retro Biosciences** — Sam Altman-backed ($180M seed, 2022/23). Raising/closing a large **Series A**; **closed at a $1.8B (pre-money) valuation, May 2026** (had targeted ~$1B raise). Pursues in vivo gene therapy, cell replacement, plasma/autophagy approaches; goal +10 healthy years. [STAT (May 2026)](https://www.statnews.com/2026/05/22/retro-biosciences-longevity-valuation/), [TechCrunch (Jan 2025)](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/24/retro-biosciences-backed-by-sam-altman-is-raising-1-billion-to-extend-human-lifespan/)
- **NewLimit** — Brian Armstrong (Coinbase CEO)/Blake Byers/Jacob Kimmel. **$130M Series B (May 2025)** led by **Kleiner Perkins**, with NFDG (Nat Friedman/Daniel Gross), Khosla, Human Capital; returning Founders Fund, Dimension, Elad Gil, Garry Tan, Patrick Collison. Then **$435M round (June 2026)** tripling valuation to ~$3B, ahead of first clinical trial. Epigenetic reprogramming (liver-cell prototypes). [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/06/newlimit-founded-by-coinbase-ceo-brian-armstrong-raises-130m-to-develop-age-reversing-therapies/), [NewLimit blog](https://blog.newlimit.com/p/newlimit-raises-130-million-series), [STAT (Jun 2026)](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/02/longevity-startup-newlimit-announces-435-million-clinical-trial-financing/)

**Thesis (longevity):** "Programmable biology" — that aging is a reprogrammable cellular state (Yamanaka-factor / partial reprogramming) — is a long-horizon, founder/patron-capital bet (tech billionaires + crossover funds), largely outside conventional VC return windows. High capital, long timelines, scientific uncertainty; funded on conviction + patient capital rather than near-term ROI.

## 5. Biomanufacturing / Synthetic Biology

- The 2021-era poster child **Ginkgo Bioworks** (public) has struggled post-SPAC: cutting ~$200M costs/workforce realignment to reach EBITDA breakeven by 2026; ~$227M revenue (2024). Won a BARDA BioMaP contract (up to $22.2M) for domestic mAb biomanufacturing. [SynBioBeta](https://www.synbiobeta.com/read/if-biology-can-build-it-they-will-come-ginkgo-bioworks-is-laying-the-foundation-for-the-4-trillion-bioeconomy), [PRNewswire/BARDA](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ginkgo-bioworks-awarded-project-agreement-through-bardas-biomap-consortium-302600814.html), [ainvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/ginkgo-bioworks-navigating-turbulence-strategic-resilience-synthetic-biology-2509/)
- Macro: synthetic biology market ~19% CAGR toward ~$56B by 2031; a **~$15B US federal biomanufacturing commitment** + China's ~$4.17B (2024) anchor capacity build-out and national-security framing. AI-driven protein design + falling gene-synthesis costs cited as demand drivers. [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/synthetic-biology-market)
- Note: biomanufacturing is the **least frothy** of the five segments in pure-VC terms — more reliant on government/strategic capital than venture; the 2021 SPAC vehicles here largely underperformed.

## 6. 2021 Bubble (ZIRP/SPAC) vs 2024–2026 — what survived, what's newly funded

- **2021 peak:** ZIRP-fueled record activity; pharma/biotech VC deals approached **~$70B**; dedicated biotech VC funds raised **~$30.8B**; SPACs/IPOs abundant; "raise on a slide deck and a dream." [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/business/biotechs-nauseating-roller-coaster-repels-investors), [Alacrita](https://www.alacrita.com/whitepapers/biotech-vc-early-formation-underpriced)
- **Correction:** XBI fell ~61% from its Feb-2021 high through 2023 before bottoming late 2023. US life-sciences VC fell to ~$25B (2023), ~$27B (2024); dedicated biotech VC fundraising fell to **$11.7B (2024)**. Layoffs/pipeline cuts since 2023; "almost no new biotech offerings" by mid-2025. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/business/biotechs-nauseating-roller-coaster-repels-investors), [Alacrita](https://www.alacrita.com/whitepapers/biotech-vc-early-formation-underpriced), [Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/03/from-volatility-to-vitality-how-2025-reset-the-life-sciences-market-and-whats-next-for-2026)
- **What survived = "barbell" market:** ample capital for a few perceived winners (median US biotech venture round ~$100M Q1 2025; ~100 nine-figure rounds in 2024) and a long tail starved of capital. VCs migrated to **de-risked, later-stage, clinically validated (Phase II)** assets; "in 2025 you need proof, pedigree, and a plan." [Alacrita](https://www.alacrita.com/whitepapers/biotech-vc-early-formation-underpriced), [BDO](https://www.bdo.com/insights/industries/life-sciences/bdo-biotech-brief-summer-2025)
- **What's newly funded:** the AI×biology platforms (Xaira, Isomorphic, EvolutionaryScale, Genesis) and AI-delivery software (Abridge, OpenEvidence, Ambience) — i.e., the new money has an **AI thesis attached**, not generic platform biotech. Recovery signs: XBI +43% over six months; biotech venture financing +70.9% Q2→Q3 2025; M&A returned (private M&A outpaced IPOs). [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/business/biotech-industry-overcomes-slump-adapts-to-new-normal), [Pharmaceutical Technology](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/analyst-comment/biotech-recovery-q3-2025-venture-funding/)

## 7. Implied thesis & future assumptions investors underwrite

1. **AI compresses discovery** — better structure/function prediction + generative design + perturbation modeling raises hit rates and cuts the time/cost of getting to a clinical candidate (Xaira, Isomorphic, Genesis, EvolutionaryScale).
2. **AI-designed drugs reach the clinic & validate** — pharma option/milestone deals (Isomorphic–Lilly/Novartis ~$3B; Recursion–Bayer ~$1.5B) are treated as proof the models produce real, partnerable assets.
3. **AI healthcare software = enterprise SaaS economics in health** — fast adoption, high gross margins (OpenEvidence ~90%), clear admin-cost ROI; underwritten at SaaS-like multiples and rapid valuation step-ups.
4. **Programmable biology / aging is reprogrammable** — partial/epigenetic reprogramming can reverse cellular aging (Altos, Retro, NewLimit); long-horizon patron capital.
5. **Bioeconomy & biosecurity** — government-anchored biomanufacturing as strategic infrastructure (US ~$15B, China ~$4.17B).

## 8. Durability post-correction

- **Most durable:** AI-in-healthcare-delivery (Abridge, OpenEvidence, Ambience) — real revenue, real ARR growth, demonstrable clinician ROI, SaaS economics. Risk is competition/commoditization (Epic embedding scribe features; Microsoft incumbency) and valuations running ahead of revenue (OpenEvidence $12B on ~$150M ARR; Abridge $5.3B). [FierceHealthcare on Epic](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/how-epics-ai-moves-could-shake-health-tech-market)
- **Durable-but-unproven:** AI drug discovery platforms — well-capitalized, scientifically validated (Nobel), commercially de-risked by pharma deals, but **no AI-designed drug has yet delivered a major clinical/approval readout**; the multi-billion bets are still underwriting a future outcome. Consolidation (Recursion–Exscientia) shows the model is maturing/rationalizing.
- **Fragile / patron-dependent:** longevity (long timelines, scientific uncertainty, mostly billionaire/crossover capital, minimal near-term ROI) and biomanufacturing/synbio (2021 SPAC cohort underperformed; Ginkgo restructuring; reliant on government capital).
- **Structural shift:** the broad biotech market remains a barbell — capital is durable for the de-risked few and the AI-narrative winners, fragile for the early-stage long tail. Recovery is real (XBI, M&A, Q3 2025 venture jump) but not a 2021-style return; "don't call it a comeback yet."

## 9. Confidence note

- **High confidence:** named rounds, lead investors, dates, valuations for Xaira, Isomorphic, Abridge, OpenEvidence, Genesis, EvolutionaryScale, NewLimit, Recursion–Exscientia — corroborated by primary sources (company/fund announcements) and reputable trade press (FierceBiotech, BioPharma Dive, STAT, TechCrunch, Rock Health, Crunchbase News).
- **Medium confidence:** exact ARR/valuation figures from secondary estimators (Sacra) and rapidly-moving 2026 datapoints (Isomorphic >$2B talks, Retro $1.8B, NewLimit $435M, Abridge Series E extension) — directionally well-sourced but some are reported/in-progress rather than fully closed-and-audited; market-share splits are analyst estimates.
- **Lower confidence:** biomanufacturing/synbio market-size CAGR and aggregate federal-spend figures (single industry-report sources, definitional variation); some 2021-peak aggregate VC totals vary by source/definition (life-sciences vs biotech-only).
- **Caveat:** the central durability question — whether AI-designed drugs deliver clinical/commercial outcomes, and whether AI-delivery valuations are justified by future revenue — is genuinely unresolved as of June 2026 and the answer flags it as a forward-looking assumption rather than established fact.
