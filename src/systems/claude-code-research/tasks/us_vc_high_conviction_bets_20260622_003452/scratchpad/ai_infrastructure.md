## 0. Headline: AI as the dominant VC thesis

- **AI captured ~50% of all global VC in 2025.** AI venture funding reached **$211B in 2025, up 85% YoY from $114B in 2024** (Crunchbase); roughly half of all global VC went to AI-related companies. [Crunchbase via aifundingtracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/q4-2025-global-ai-funding/), [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/foundational-ai-startup-funding-doubled-openai-anthropic-xai-q1-2026/)
- **Extreme concentration.** Five companies — OpenAI, Scale AI, Anthropic, Project Prometheus, and xAI — captured **$84B in 2025, ~20% of all global VC** that year. [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/foundational-ai-startup-funding-doubled-openai-anthropic-xai-q1-2026/)
- **US record.** PitchBook reported US venture funding surged to a record **$267B** with OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI dominating. [SiliconANGLE/PitchBook](https://siliconangle.com/2026/04/03/pitchbook-us-venture-funding-surges-record-267b-openai-anthropic-xai-dominate-ai-deals/)
- **Q1 2026 acceleration:** Venture funding to foundational AI startups in Q1 2026 was *double* all of 2025. [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/foundational-ai-startup-funding-doubled-openai-anthropic-xai-q1-2026/)
- Thesis is durable post-2022–23 correction: round sizes and valuations have ratcheted UP each cycle (see follow-on patterns below), not down.

## 6. Investment thesis being underwritten

Investors writing these checks are underwriting (in roughly this order):
1. **Scaling laws hold** — more compute → better models → more revenue; justifies $10s of billions in pre-revenue/early-revenue labs (SSI, Thinking Machines funded on this alone).
2. **Compute scarcity rent** — GPU/cloud capacity is the bottleneck; neoclouds (CoreWeave, Crusoe, Lambda, Nebius) monetize the shortage; chip challengers (Cerebras, Groq) bet inference demand outgrows Nvidia supply.
3. **AGI/frontier optionality** — winner-take-most payoff on a handful of labs.
4. **Sovereign AI** — Gulf (Mubadala, MGX, QIA, Abu Dhabi), Singapore (GIC, Temasek), and national champions (Mistral/ASML/Bpifrance) fund national/regional compute independence.
5. **Energy is the next bottleneck** — power (esp. nuclear/SMR) is being pre-bought and pre-financed years ahead of demand.

## 7. Durability post-2022–23 correction

- Round sizes and valuations **ratcheted up every cycle**, not down: OpenAI $300B→$380B→ heading to ~$500B+ era; Anthropic $183B (Sep '25) → $350B (Nov '25) → $380B (Jan '26) → $965B (May '26); Crusoe $2.8B→$10B in 7 months; CoreWeave $7B→$19B→$23B→IPO.
- **Follow-on is rapid and oversubscribed** (xAI targeted $15B, took $20B; Groq raised more than planned).
- **Exit window reopened:** CoreWeave (Mar 2025) and Cerebras (May 2026) IPOs; Nebius public; Anthropic pre-IPO.
- Revenue is now real and compounding for the leaders (Anthropic $1B→$47B run-rate in ~18 months; CoreWeave $12–13B 2026 guide), distinguishing this from the 2021 froth.
- Counter-signal: pervasive **circular-financing** warnings and bubble concern; concentration risk (5 names = 20% of global VC).

## 8. Confidence note

- **High confidence** on headline mega-round facts (OpenAI $40B, Anthropic Series F/H, CoreWeave IPO, xAI $20B, Stargate, Nvidia–OpenAI $100B) — multiple top-tier sources (company press releases, SEC/IPO filings, CNBC, Bloomberg, Reuters, TechCrunch) cross-confirm.
- **High confidence** on chip/neocloud rounds (Groq, Cerebras, Lambda, Crusoe) — primary company newsrooms + S-1/IPO data.
- **Medium confidence** on exact valuations for opaque pre-revenue labs (SSI, Thinking Machines) — reported, not always company-confirmed; strategic-investor amounts (Nvidia/Alphabet in SSI) undisclosed.
- **Medium confidence** on some 2026 figures (Anthropic $965B Series H, OpenAI $120B, Cerebras IPO pricing) — recent and partly forward-looking; values may settle differently.
- Deal transparency is **higher than typical VC** because so many actors are public (Nvidia, Microsoft, Oracle, AMD, ASML, CoreWeave, Nebius, Cerebras) and disclose via filings/PPAs; the murkiest area is the true economic substance of circular vendor-investor arrangements.
- Cross-source consistency is **strong**; primary divergence is interpretive (bubble vs. durable), not factual.
