# Who is the President of the United States?

**Donald John Trump** is the current President of the United States. He is the
47th president, having been sworn into office on **January 20, 2025**, for a
term running 2025–2029.[^1][^2][^3]

His Vice President is **JD Vance**.[^2]

## Notes

- Trump previously served as the 45th president (2017–2021), making his current
  term non-consecutive.[^3]
- As of the date of this answer (2026-06-14), no event (succession, removal, or
  the next presidential election) has changed this. The next U.S. presidential
  election is in November 2028; the 2026 elections are midterm (congressional)
  elections only.[^4]
