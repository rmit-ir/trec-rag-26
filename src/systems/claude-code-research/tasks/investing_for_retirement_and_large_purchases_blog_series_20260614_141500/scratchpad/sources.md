## Retirement accounts & 2026 limits (PRIMARY: IRS)
- IRS newsroom: https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500
  - 401(k)/403(b)/457/TSP deferral 2026 = $24,500
  - 401(k) catch-up 50+ = $8,000; ages 60–63 special catch-up = $11,250
  - IRA limit 2026 = $7,500; IRA catch-up 50+ = $1,100
  - Roth IRA phase-out: single/HoH $153,000–$168,000; MFJ $242,000–$252,000
- FINRA Beginner's Guide to 401(k)s: https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/beginners-guide-401ks
  - employer match "free money"; dollar-for-dollar match on 3% "literally doubles" that savings; traditional reduces taxable income; tax-deferred until withdrawal; Roth = after-tax.

## Index funds vs active / fees
- SPIVA (S&P) finding: large majority of active funds underperform their benchmark over 10+ yrs.
- Wikipedia (secondary) Expense ratio / Passive management: typical US stock fund ~1%; index funds far lower.
- Self-computed fee example: $10,000, 30 yrs, 7% gross: ~$74,025 at 0.1% fee vs ~$57,435 at 1% fee → ~$16,600 difference (1.069^30 vs 1.06^30).

## Insurance
- FDIC = bank deposits up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category.
- SIPC = brokerage failure protection up to $500,000 (incl. $250,000 cash). Neither covers market losses.
- (sources: Fidelity SIPC explainer, Schwab MoneyWise, Marcus FDIC vs SIPC)
