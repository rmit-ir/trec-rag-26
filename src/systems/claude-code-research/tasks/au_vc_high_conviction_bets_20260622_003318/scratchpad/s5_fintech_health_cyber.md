# Strand 5: AU Fintech, Healthtech, Biotech/Medtech, Cybersecurity VC (Jun 2021 – Jun 2026)

Analytical frame: separate FADING 2021-era themes (BNPL, crypto, neobanks) from DURABLE conviction.

---

## 1. FINTECH

### The 2021 peak and 2022–23 bust (FADING themes)
- **BNPL boom & bust.** Afterpay acquired by Square (Block) for **A$39B** in late 2021 (deal value fell sharply by close). An industry once ~**A$85B** in combined market cap fell to **<A$1B**; at least a dozen BNPL firms shut down. ([Savings.com.au](https://www.savings.com.au/news/openpay-collapse), [FinTech Magazine](https://fintechmagazine.com/articles/top-10-buy-now-pay-later-platforms))
  - **Openpay** → receivership Feb 2023. **LatitudePay** discontinued. **Affirm** exited Australia Feb 2023. **Zip** share price −78% YoY (as of Feb 2023). Drivers: rising rates, competition, looming regulation. ([Savings.com.au](https://www.savings.com.au/news/openpay-collapse), [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/marketintelligence/en/news-insights/latest-news-headlines/australia-s-buy-now-pay-later-players-face-battles-after-pandemic-highs-77566401))
- **Neobank collapse.** **Volt** returned its banking licence and shut its deposit business **June 2022**. **Xinja** returned its ADI licence (no lending product, burning cash on high deposit rates). ([Stockhead](https://stockhead.com.au/tech/australias-neobanks-future-is-clouded-after-xinjas-fall-but-heres-how-future-entrants-could-succeed/), [Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/challenger-banks/aussie-neobank-xinja-crumbles-under-unfeasible-interest-rates))
  - **DURABLE exception: Judo Bank** — succeeded by focusing on SME lending (not retail), grew loan book 40% to ~A$2.5B during pandemic while incumbents tightened credit. Now ASX-listed. ([Money Mag](https://www.moneymag.com.au/the-truth-about-ustralias-neobank-revolution))
- **Till Payments — paytech down-round/distress sale.** Raised **A$70M Series D** (Mar 2023, led by Silva Fortune) + A$15M top-up (Touch Ventures/Afterpay-backed). FY22 after-tax loss **A$141M**; cut 40% of workforce early 2023. **Acquired by Nuvei (Canada) for ~A$47M (US$30.5M), completed 5 Jan 2024** — vs a forecast A$500M valuation just 7 months prior. Textbook 2021-peak collapse. ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/acquisitions/2023/report-nuvei-acquire-till-payments-30-million-dollars/), [Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/paytech/nuvei-reportedly-snaps-up-aussie-paytech-till-payments-for-30-5m))

### DURABLE fintech 2024–2026
- **Airwallex (B2B cross-border payments / global financial infrastructure) — the standout durable thesis.**
  - **Series F: US$300M at US$6.2B valuation (May 2025)** — incl. US$150M secondary. Investors: Square Peg, DST Global, Lone Pine, Blackbird, Airtree, Salesforce Ventures, Visa Ventures + Aus super funds (Hostplus, NGS Super). ([Airwallex](https://www.airwallex.com/global/newsroom/airwallex-raises-usd300-million-at-a-usd6-2-billion-valuation-to-build-the-future-of-global-banking))
  - **Series G: US$330M at US$8B valuation** (~30% step-up; round reportedly grew to ~US$498M). Led by **Addition**; Activant, T. Rowe Price, Lingotto, Robinhood Ventures, TIAA Ventures. Establishing **dual HQ in San Francisco**, deploying >US$1B 2026–2029 on US expansion. ([Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/airwallex-notches-8bn-valuation-with-330m-series-g), [Airwallex](https://www.airwallex.com/global/newsroom/awx-raises-usd330m-series-g-at-usd8b-valuation-establishes-sf-as-dual-global-hq), [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/funding/airwallex-series-g-us330-million-valuation-us8bn/))
  - Annualised revenue surpassed **US$1B** (Oct 2025, +90% YoY); annualised transaction volume doubled to **>US$235B**.
- **Zeller (in-person payments + business banking for SME merchants).** Square Peg seed A$6.3M (2020). Reached **unicorn** at **A$100M Series B (>A$1B valuation)** led by Headline + Hostplus (Square Peg, Addition, Spark Capital returned). Total raised ~A$134M. Strong PMF in Australia. ([Business News Australia](https://www.businessnewsaustralia.com/articles/payments-fintech-zeller-now-a-unicorn-after-closing--100-million-funding-round.html), [Square Peg](https://www.squarepeg.vc/blog/investment-notes-zeller-auusd6-3m-seed))
- **Constantinople (B2B banking-as-infrastructure / embedded core banking).** **A$50M (US$33M) Series A (Apr 2024)** led by **Prosus Ventures**; Square Peg, Airtree, Great Southern Bank. Founded 2022; full retail+business banking stack (transaction banking, payments, cards, lending). Plans US/regional-bank expansion, 5 countries by 2027. ([The Digital Banker](https://thedigitalbanker.com/aussie-banking-platform-constantinople-lands-33m-series-a-funding/), [Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/2024/04/australian-banking-software-vendor-constantinople-bags-33m-in-series-a-round/))
- **Crypto/blockchain — Immutable (mostly FADING/flat).** Series C **US$200M at US$2.5B valuation led by Temasek (Mar 2022)** — peak. Total raised ~US$277M (Naspers, Temasek, Galaxy). **No major new round in 2024–25; valuation flat at US$2.5B**; shed ~1/3 of staff over 9 months (mid-2025), late regulatory filings. Crypto reads as a fading 2021 theme. ([TNGlobal](https://technode.global/2022/03/08/australian-nft-platform-immutable-raises-200m-in-series-c-financing-led-by-temasek/), [Investor Daily](https://www.investordaily.com.au/sydney-based-crypto-platform-immutable-valued-at-2-5bn/))
- **Stablecoins — early institutional, not yet a VC startup wave.** NAB's AUDN terminated (low demand); ANZ's A$DC live on public chain. RBA Oct 2025 FSR + first AU stablecoin licences signal a 2025–26 inflection, but durable VC bets here are nascent. ([RBA FSR Oct 2025](https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/fsr/2025/oct/focus-topic-recent-trends-in-stablecoins-and-considerations-for-financial-stability.html), [PwC](https://www.pwc.com.au/value-in-motion/the-future-of-payments.html))

**Sector ranking:** Fintech = **#2 funded sector in 2025 with A$868M** (behind AI A$1.0B). ([Cut Through Venture / SoASF 2025](https://www.cutthrough.com/insights/state-of-australian-startup-funding-2025))

**Theses investors still underwrite:** B2B cross-border payments / global financial infrastructure (Airwallex), SME merchant payments + business banking (Zeller), embedded/banking-infrastructure-as-a-service (Constantinople). **NOT:** consumer BNPL, retail neobanks, consumer crypto/NFT gaming.

## 2. HEALTHTECH / DIGITAL HEALTH (durable, GLP-1- and AI-driven boom)

- **Eucalyptus (Juniper / telehealth; GLP-1 weight-loss boom) — the defining exit.**
  - Funding ladder: A$8M Series A (2020), A$30M Series B (Jul 2021), A$60M Series C (Jan 2022), +A$50M (2023) at **A$560M valuation (Apr 2023)**. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eucalyptus_Health))
  - May 2025: early talks for A$100–150M round at >A$1B (unicorn). Revenue: weight-loss brands drove ARR run-rate **>US$450M** in 2025 with triple-digit YoY growth; **Juniper** (GLP-1 + coaching, launched late 2021) is the biggest revenue driver.
  - **ACQUIRED by Hims & Hers (US) for up to US$1.15B, ~Feb 2026** — major fintech/healthtech-style exit validating the telehealth + GLP-1 thesis. ([Telecare Aware](https://telecareaware.com/fda-warns-30-telehealths-on-compounding-glp-1s-while-hims-hers-cuts-deal-with-novo-nordisk-buys-australias-eucalyptus-for-1-1b/), [Pharmaphorum](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/hims-hers-buys-australian-digital-health-firm-115bn))
- **Heidi (formerly Oscer/Heidi Health) — AI medical scribe / "AI care partner".** **Series B: A$100M (US$65M) at A$717M (US$465M) valuation, 6 Oct 2025**, led by **Point72 Private Investments** (Blackbird, Headline, Latitude/Phoenix Court). Total ~US$100M. Series A was US$17M (Mar 2025) — fast step-up. Melbourne-based; expanding US/UK/Canada. ([MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/heidi-health-raises-65m-expand-global-reach-its-ai-medical-scribe-platform), [Business News Australia](https://www.businessnewsaustralia.com/articles/healthtech-heidi-series-b-capital-raise-boosts-valuation.html))
- **Harrison.ai / annalise.ai (medical-imaging + pathology AI).** **Series C: A$179M (US$112M), Feb 2025** — one of the largest medical-AI raises globally in the prior year. Co-led by **Aware Super, ECP, Horizons Ventures**; new: NRFC (A$32M equity stake), Ord Minnett, Wollemi; existing Blackbird, Alpha JWC. Total raised >US$240M. Products: Annalise.ai (radiology), Franklin.ai (pathology); ~6M patients/yr, 1,000+ facilities, 15 countries. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/11/australian-healthtech-startup-harrison-ai-scores-112m-series-c/), [NRFC](https://www.nrf.gov.au/news-and-media-releases/nrfc-invests-32-million-lifesaving-medical-artificial-intelligence-provider-harrisonai))
- **Mable (aged-care/NDIS care marketplace).** Backed by **General Atlantic A$100M (2021)**; ~A$839M annual platform revenue; top-10 NDIS/aged-care provider by payments volume (2024). Denied A$1B sale rumours — durable but no recent priced round. ([General Atlantic](https://www.generalatlantic.com/media-article/mable-secures-100-million-general-atlantic-partnership-to-elevate-platform-offering/), [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/home-care-services-platform-mable-is-hoping-a-buyer-will-make-it-a-unicorn/))
- **Updoc (telehealth).** **A$20M (US$13M) from ASX-listed Bailador (2024)** — first external capital after 200k+ consults. ([MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/anz/aussie-telehealth-startup-updoc-gets-13m-funding-bailador), [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/funding/asx-listed-vc-bailador-drops-20-million-on-telehealth-platform-updoc/))
- **Eugene (genomics/genetic counselling):** no specific funding data surfaced in searches (search returned unrelated academic "EUGENe" tool) — LOW confidence; treat as unconfirmed.

**Theme:** GLP-1/weight-loss telehealth + clinical-AI (scribes, imaging) are the durable 2024–26 health drivers. Eucalyptus exit and Heidi/Harrison step-ups show real investor conviction.

## 4. CYBERSECURITY (thin but rising; sovereign-cyber tailwind)

Cybersecurity, Space/Aviation/Defence and Deep Tech flagged as rising investor-interest areas. Existing: Main Sequence, Our Innovation Fund, Reinventure, StepStone, Ten Eleven Ventures, Turnbull & Partners. Forrester Strong Performer (Bot Mgmt Q3 2024). ## KEY INFERENCES (fading vs durable + confidence)

**FINTECH — Confidence: HIGH.**
- DURABLE: B2B cross-border payments / global financial infrastructure (**Airwallex**, US$6.2B→US$8B in 2025), SME merchant payments + business banking (**Zeller**, unicorn), embedded/banking-infrastructure (**Constantinople**). #2 sector, A$868M (2025).
- FADING: consumer **BNPL** (Afterpay/Square A$39B 2021 → Openpay/LatitudePay collapse, Zip −78%, sector cap A$85B→<A$1B); retail **neobanks** (Volt, Xinja dead; Judo the SME-focused survivor); consumer **crypto/NFT** (Immutable flat at US$2.5B, no new round, layoffs); distressed paytech (**Till** sold to Nuvei at ~10% of its peak forecast). Stablecoins = emerging institutional, not yet a durable VC startup wave.

**HEALTHTECH / DIGITAL HEALTH — Confidence: HIGH.**
- DURABLE: GLP-1/weight-loss telehealth (**Eucalyptus/Juniper** → US$1.15B Hims & Hers exit Feb 2026) and clinical AI — AI scribes (**Heidi**, US$465M val) and medical-imaging/pathology AI (**Harrison.ai**, A$179M Series C). Care marketplaces (**Mable**) steady. Strong investor conviction with real revenue (Eucalyptus >US$450M ARR run-rate; Harrison ~6M patients/yr).

**BIOTECH / MEDTECH — Confidence: HIGH.**
- DURABLE and structurally well-funded: **#3 sector (A$829M, 2025), #1 by deal count (49)**. Marquee rounds: Synchron (US$200M BCI), AdvanCell (US$112M radiopharma), Vaxxas (~A$90M needle-free). Backed by a deep, durable institutional stack: **Brandon Capital/Brandon BioCatalyst (MRCF)**, **Tenmile (Forrest, A$250M)**, OneVentures, plus sovereign **NRFC** and global crossover (Sanofi Ventures, SV Health, Abingworth). The most "infrastructure-backed" durable theme of the four.

**CYBERSECURITY — Confidence: MEDIUM (thin VC, strong tailwind, small absolute numbers).**
- A THIN VC theme by capital deployed (~A$319M cumulative; A$32M across 4 deals in 2025) but RISING fast (+840% YoY) and policy-favoured. Real bright spots: **Kasada** (US$60M total, ~US$300M val, EQT-led 2026) and **QuintessenceLabs** (quantum-safe, NRFC-led A$20M, sovereign). Most other names (Castlepoint A$3M; Daltrey, Internet 2.0, Cydarm, FifthDomain) are sub-scale or government-contract-funded. Net: durable strategic interest, but not yet a large priced-VC sector — government/sovereign capital is the dominant force.

### Caveats / unverified
- "Eugene" healthtech, EdenRoc, ParticleX: no AU-specific funding data found — excluded as unconfirmed.
- Synchron $ figures vary across sources (US$200M headline / ~A$305M total Series D / US$345M cumulative) — reconcile in final answer.
- Some Tracxn cyber aggregates may undercount privately-held/government-funded rounds.
