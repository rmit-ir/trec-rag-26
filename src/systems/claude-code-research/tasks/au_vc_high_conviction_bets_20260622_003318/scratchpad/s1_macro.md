## 1. TOTAL ANNUAL CAPITAL & DEALS (the boom → correction → recovery arc)

| Year | Total raised | Deals | YoY | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|-----|-------|
| **2021** | **~$10.6B** | **731** | record peak | 54 deals >$50M; boom peak |
| **2022** | **$7.4B** | **712** | -30% | mega-deals collapsed (28 vs 54); early-stage hit records |
| **2023** | **$3.5B** | **413** | **-54%** | trough; "mirrored 2020 levels"; only 15 deals >$50M; no new unicorns |
| **2024** | **$4.0B** | **414** | +11% (capital) | recovery begins; 22 deals >$50M; early-stage medians record-high |
| **2025** | **$5.4–5.48B** | **390** | +24–31% (capital), **-17 to -20% deals** | 3rd-largest year on record; capital UP but deal COUNT DOWN |
| **Q1 2026** | **$1.8B** | 81 venture + 26 accelerator | +63% vs Q1'25 | strongest start since 2022 boom |
| **1H2026 (to ~June)** | **~$1.77B** in 82 equity rounds | — | +42% vs same period 2025 (one tracker) |

**Shape of the arc:** Peak 2021 ($10.6B) → sharp 2022 correction (-30%) → deep 2023 trough (-54%, $3.5B) → gradual 2024 recovery ($4.0B) → strong 2025 rebound ($5.4B) → accelerating 2026 (Q1 $1.8B, +63% YoY). The recovery is **concentration-driven, not breadth-driven**: 2025 raised MORE money across FEWER deals than 2024. Q4 2025 alone was ~$2B (strongest quarter since Q4 2021).

**Concentration metric (key signal):**
- 2025: Top 20 deals = **58%** of all capital (higher than 2023 & 2024).
- Q1 2026: Top 10 deals = **59%**; Top 20 = **79%** of all capital. Sub-$5M deals hit lowest quarterly level since 2020.

## 2. STAGE BREAKDOWN TREND (where conviction sits by stage)

**Median round sizes (Cut Through):**
| Stage | 2024 median | 2025 median |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| Angel/Pre-seed | $1.0M (record) | $1.0M |
| Seed | $3.0M (record) | $2.5M |
| Series A | — | $11.0M |
| Series B+ | — | $30.0M |

**Trajectory:**
- **2021 boom** was driven by **late-stage / mega-deals** (54 deals >$50M; growth-stage outliers).
- **2022 correction** = mega-deals collapsed first (28 deals >$50M, -48%), but **early-stage (pre-seed/seed) hit RECORD levels** — capital rotated down the stack. Record 428 early-stage deals.
- **2023 trough** = only 15 deals >$50M; later-stage froze hardest; early-stage held up best ("early-stage startups fared the best").
- **2024–2025 recovery** = pre-seed and seed medians hit **record highs** (pre-seed $1M, seed $3M in 2024). Large deals returned (22 deals >$50M in 2024; mega-rounds in 2025).
- **2025–Q1 2026** = conviction now bifurcated: strong **early-stage competition** (59% of pre-seed/seed deals "competitive" in 2025, up from 30%) AND a **return of very large late-stage rounds** — but middle (Series A→B progression) remains thin; "uneven progression into scaled funding," "constrained by local later-stage cheque depth."

**Where conviction concentrates NOW vs 2021:** In 2021 conviction = broad + late-stage mega-rounds. In 2025/26 conviction = (a) highly selective early-stage (competitive seed) and (b) a handful of very large, often AI/deep-tech/defence growth rounds. The "messy middle" (Series A/B for non-AI companies) is where capital is scarce; founders go offshore for >$20M cheques.

## 3. SECTOR MIX TREND (ranking shift 2021→2026)

**2022 top sectors:** 1. Fintech $1.3B; 2. Enterprise/Business Software $1.2B; 3. Hardware/Robotics/IoT $554M; then Health/Biotech, ClimateTech.

**2023 top sector:** Enterprise Software led ($505M). (Fintech/crypto faded.)

**2024 top sectors:** 1. **Fintech $947M**; 2. **Climate Tech/CleanTech $609M** (55 deals — most by deal volume); 3. Biotech & MedTech $347M. Rising interest: Space/Aviation/Defence, Cybersecurity, Deep Tech. AI "embedded" in SaaS/Fintech/HealthTech (no standalone figure yet).

**2025 top sectors:** 1. **Artificial Intelligence ~$1.0B** (now a standalone #1); 2. Fintech $868M; 3. Biotech/Medtech $829M; 4. Climate Tech/Cleantech $585M; 5. Hardware/Robotics/IoT $297M; 6. Healthtech $271M. **AI pervasiveness: 61% of every dollar invested went to companies using AI somewhere in the stack; ~62% of rounds had AI participation.**

**Q1 2026 sectors:** AI-enabled = 42% of venture dollars / 60%+ by count. Vertical SaaS $800M (59 deals); Hardware/robotics/sensors $303M; **Space & defence $229M (3 deals, ~$76M avg)**; Cybersecurity $125M (UpGuard $105M Series C); AI infrastructure $100M (Firmus). Note: "Five years ago these [defence/space/hardware/climate infra] barely registered… now they account for the plurality of capital — a genuine structural shift."

**Ranking shift summary:**
- **RISING:** AI (from embedded → standalone #1 by 2025); Climate/Energy/Cleantech (steady top-3 by 2024–25); Defence/Space/Dual-use (near-zero 2021 → plurality of biggest 2026 rounds); Deep tech/hardware/robotics; Cybersecurity.
- **DECLINING / 2021-era themes faded:** Crypto/Web3 (near-absent from rankings by 2023+); Quick-commerce/consumer delivery (a 2021 boom theme, gone); Fintech relatively (still large in $ but lost #1 crown to AI by 2025); pure consumer.
- Australia "over-indexes globally in Food, Energy, and Space" (Dealroom).

## 4. CAPITAL SOURCE STRUCTURE

### A. Superannuation funds (the structural backbone of AU VC)
- **Blackbird Ventures 2022 fund: ~$1.0B** (country's largest-ever VC fund) — LPs include **AustralianSuper, Hostplus, HESTA, Aware Super, Telstra Super, NGS Super, and the Future Fund.** Source: https://www.investordaily.com.au/australiansuper-hostplus-among-investors-in-record-1bn-vc-fund/
- **Hostplus** = the single most important super LP to VC: commitments to Australian VCs total **>$350M** across M.H. Carnegie & Co., Brandon Capital, Blackbird, Square Peg. Hostplus has been the anchor LP of the local ecosystem. Sources: https://newsroom.hostplus.com.au/hostplus-expands-venture-capital-commitment/ ; https://www.privateequityinternational.com/australias-hostplus-piling-venture-capital/
- Super-fund VC "conviction held up amid 2022 tech selloff" (AusSuper, Hostplus) — they did not retreat during the correction. https://www.investmentmagazine.com.au/2022/11/aussuper-hostplus-vc-conviction-holding-up-amid-tech-selloff/
- 2024–25: major super funds (AustralianSuper, Hostplus) **increasing VC allocations** after regulatory adjustments (Your Future Your Super performance-test treatment had earlier been a headwind). https://cockatoo.com.au/venture-capital-funds-australia-2025/
- AU VC industry AUM: **A$65B+**, deploying ~A$5B+/yr (2024). https://waveup.com/blog/venture-capital-firms-australia-vc-ecosystem-overview/

### B. Corporate venture arms (CVC)
- **Main Sequence (CSIRO)** — deep-tech CVC/government-backed; **Fund III A$450M** first close. LPs: Hostplus, NGS Super, Australian Ethical, LGT Crestone, Morgan Stanley Wealth, **Temasek (Singapore sovereign)**, **Daiwa Securities (Japan)**, Grantham Foundation. CSIRO commitment part-funded by federal Australia's Economic Accelerator. Sources: https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/venture-capital/csiros-deep-tech-vc-main-sequence-bags-450-million-third-fund/ ; https://www.mseq.vc/
- **Telstra Ventures** — Telstra's CVC arm (now also backed by HarbourVest). https://waveup.com/blog/venture-capital-firms-australia-vc-ecosystem-overview/
- **Firemark Ventures (IAG)** — insurer CVC, seed→late, insurance/safety/climate-risk focus. https://au.linkedin.com/company/firemarkventures
- **Macquarie Capital VC** — 30-yr track record, early-stage software/internet.
- (NAB Ventures wound down its dedicated fund earlier; Woodside has energy-tech/decarbonisation investing — less documented in sources retrieved; LOW confidence on current activity.)

### C. Foreign / offshore co-investors (rising fast — the headline structural shift)
- **International investor participation in deals: 2024 = 57% → 2025 = 66%** (up 9pp). Sources: https://www.scalesuite.com.au/resources/state-of-australian-startup-funding ; https://www.cutthrough.com/insights/state-of-australian-startup-funding-2024
- 70% of local investors (2023 report) expected greater international participation in 2024 — it materialised.
- Offshore capital concentrated at **late/growth stage**: when founders need >$20M (Series B+), US and Asian (Singapore, Hong Kong) funds provide faster large cheques + sovereign capital pools. Many AU startups raise overseas due to thin local late-stage depth. Sources: https://www.capitaly.vc/blog/how-australian-startups-should-raise-capital-from-us-investors-in-2025 ; https://inspirepreneurmagazine.com/technology/start-ups/why-australian-startups-are-raising-capital-overseas-instead-of-locally/
- Australia's share of Asia-Pacific VC funding rose **1.1% (2016) → 3.1% (2026)**. Dealroom: 2025 ~$2.9B; 1H2026 $1.8B in 5 months → full-year run-rate ~$4.3B (+47% vs 2025). https://dealroom.co/guides/australia
- KPMG Venture Pulse: Australia ~$1B/quarter VC (Q3'25); Firmus (AI infra) raised US$541M in Q4'25 — a major foreign-capital-backed AI round. https://kpmg.com/cn/en/insights/2026/02/venture-pulse-q4-25.html

### D. Family offices & angels
- Angel/pre-seed median $1.0M (record). Competitive pre-seed/seed deals rose to 59% in 2025 (from ~30%), implying healthy angel/syndicate + micro-VC demand at the bottom of the stack.
- Family offices increasingly active fillers of the local late-stage gap (qualitative; LOW-MEDIUM confidence, not quantified in sources retrieved).

### E. Government as capital catalyst (2026 structural addition)
- **Defence VC co-investment plan (2026): Government invites VCs to co-invest up to AU$500M, with total funding up to AU$1B**, targeting cyber, AI, autonomous systems, electronic warfare, quantum, undersea warfare. Direct driver of the defence/dual-use funding surge. Source: https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/australia/defence-meets-venture-capital-australias-au$500-million-co-investment-plan (page paywalled on fetch; confirmed via search snippet)
- Australia's Economic Accelerator (federal) co-funds deep-tech (e.g., Main Sequence).

## KEY INFERENCES — what these macro shifts imply about investor future-assumptions

1. **Capital is consolidating around fewer, bigger, higher-conviction bets.** Rising $ + falling deal count + top-20 = 58% (2025) and top-20 = 79% (Q1'26) means investors are concentrating conviction, not spraying. Implies a belief that returns will come from a small number of category winners, not a broad portfolio. **[High]**

2. **AI is now the default thesis, not a sector.** 61% of every 2025 dollar went to AI-touching companies. Investors assume AI capability is table-stakes; "non-AI" is an implicit discount. **[High]**

3. **Hard-tech / sovereignty themes (defence, space, energy, deep-tech, cyber) are the new high-conviction frontier** — from ~0% of 2021 portfolios to the plurality of 2026's biggest rounds, turbo-charged by the AU$500M–$1B government defence co-investment scheme. Investors are betting on geopolitical/sovereign-capability tailwinds and government as a co-underwriter of risk. **[High]**

4. **2021-era consumer/crypto/quick-commerce/fintech-first theses are out of favour.** Fintech still large in absolute $ but lost the #1 crown; crypto/web3 and consumer delivery essentially vanished from rankings. Investors no longer assume cheap-capital, growth-at-all-costs consumer playbooks work. **[High]**

5. **The local market structurally lacks late-stage depth; conviction at growth stage is effectively outsourced to foreign capital.** International participation 57%→66% of deals; founders go offshore for >$20M. Implies AU funds concentrate conviction at seed/early-A, while US/Asian/sovereign funds (Temasek, Daiwa, US growth funds) underwrite the scale-ups. **[High]**

6. **Super funds are the patient, counter-cyclical anchor** — they held/grew VC conviction through the 2022–23 selloff and are increasing allocations post-regulatory-clarity. This supports a structurally larger, more durable local VC base than the 2021 cycle. **[Medium-High]** (super commitments well evidenced; "increasing allocations 2024-25" partly secondary-sourced.)

7. **Early-stage selectivity has risen sharply** (competitive seed 30%→59%): conviction is concentrated even at entry, with record median round sizes but fewer total deals. Implies a "fund the best, ignore the rest" assumption taking hold earlier in the funnel. **[High]**
