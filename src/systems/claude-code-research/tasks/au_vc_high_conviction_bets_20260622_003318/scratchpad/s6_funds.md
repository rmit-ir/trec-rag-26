# Strand 6: Australian VC FUND Landscape (Supply Side of Conviction), June 2021–June 2026

Mapping the supply side: who leads which thesis, fund-close sizes (2021–2026 vintages), AUM, and the flagship bets that signal high conviction. Confidence flagged per claim.

[Capital Brief](https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/blackbird-secures-700-million-in-first-close-for-its-sixth-fund-244620fc-a330-4cec-b904-6daff98091c9/); [VC Journal](https://www.venturecapitaljournal.com/blackbirds-sixth-flagship-fund-begins-takeoff/) — **High**

Deployed **$357m in 2025** (20 new + 31 follow-on rounds), +55% on 2024. [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/venture-capital/vc-blackbirds-now-invested-1-15-billion-in-the-last-5-years-and-delivered-just-as-much-back-to-investors-in-2024/); [Blackbird 2025 Year in Review](https://www.blackbird.vc/blog/2025-year-in-review) — **High**

### Fundraising environment 2023–2026 (macro)
- **2023:** Capital raised fell **54% YoY to A$3.5bn**; 413 deals (vs 712 in 2022); only 15 deals >A$50m (vs 28). Series B+ valuations **fell 41%**. ASX 45 listings (vs 5-yr avg 120) — exits frozen. [Gilbert+Tobin](https://www.gtlaw.com.au/insights/venture-capital-2024-australia) — **High**
- **2024:** VC **fundraising hit lowest level in a decade** even as startup funding partially recovered. >50% of AU VCs reported a portfolio company failed in 2024 ("new valley of death"). [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/a-new-valley-of-death-more-than-half-of-australian-vcs-said-a-startup-they-invested-in-failed-in-2024/) — **High**
- **2025–2026:** "Capital is flowing again" narrative; Blackbird Fund VI A$700m first close (Jan 2026) and King River A$157m signal a top-of-market recovery concentrated in established managers. [Standard Ledger](https://www.standardledger.co/article/the-state-of-the-australian-market-heading-into-2026-capital-is-flowing-again/) — **Medium**

## 7. Repeat-investor / follow-on / co-investment patterns

- **"Big 3" syndication:** **Blackbird, AirTree, Square Peg** repeatedly co-invest and hold the same marquee assets — most visibly **all three carry Canva** and **all three cut Canva's valuation ~36%/by a third in 2023** in lockstep. Australia's market is "tightly knit—a handful of funds lead most deals, and syndication is common." [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/venture-capital/airtree-joins-leading-vcs-square-peg-and-blackbird-in-cutting-canvas-valuation-by-a-third/); [easyvc](https://easyvc.ai/blog/top-vcs-in-australia-the-leading-funds-for-ambitious-startups/) — **High**
- **Deep-tech cluster:** **Main Sequence + Blackbird + Square Peg** repeatedly co-invest in deep tech — e.g. **Q-CTRL** (Square Peg led Series A, Main Sequence + Sequoia + Horizons participated); Main Sequence + Temasek co-led **Samsara Eco** (Jun 2024). Gilmour Space carried by both Blackbird and Main Sequence. — **High**
- **Family offices alongside funds:** **Skip Capital** co-invests with the Big 3 (e.g. Neara round alongside Square Peg). **Grok** and **Tenmile/Tattarang** lead/anchor in climate and biotech respectively, often with CEFC/Temasek. — **Medium**
- **State/strategic co-investors recurring:** **CEFC** anchors multiple climate vehicles (Virescent, Tenacious, Investible Climate, CTP); **Temasek** recurs in deep tech (Main Sequence Fund III LP + Samsara/Q-CTRL co-investor); super funds (Hostplus, NGS) anchor institutional fund LP bases. — **High**
- **Follow-on / "double down" discipline:** Square Peg's Opportunities Funds and Blackbird's dedicated follow-on vehicle (A$668m) are purpose-built to keep backing winners (Canva, Airwallex) round after round — structural repeat-backing of proven founders. — **High**

## Key inferences

1. **Thesis leadership map (who owns which conviction lane):**
   - **Generalist tech / scale (the conviction core):** Blackbird (leader, ~$9.9B portfolio, Fund VI raising) > Square Peg (global, US$3.6bn AUM) ≈ AirTree. These three define and concentrate AU venture conviction; Canva is the shared trophy and the IRR engine.
   - **Deep tech / science / space / quantum:** Main Sequence (CSIRO, >A$1bn) is the undisputed leader; co-founds rather than just funds.
   - **Climate:** Virescent (largest specialist, CEFC) leads pooled-fund climate; Grok leads family-office climate (largest cheque); Tenacious leads agtech-climate; Investible/CTP, Giant Leap fill impact/early.
   - **Biotech / health:** Brandon Capital/BioCatalyst (largest, institutional MRCF network) leads; Tenmile (Forrest) is the deep-pocketed challenger.
   - **AI software:** King River explicitly repositioned its sixth fund to AI software (AU+US).
   - **Defence / sovereign capability:** Salus Ventures (specialist) + the new govt Advanced Capability Investment Fund (~A$500m–$1bn) — the emerging frontier of 2025–26.

2. **High-conviction signals from fund $ and bets:** The biggest fund-$ concentration sits in (a) Blackbird's follow-on capacity to ride Canva/Airwallex to IPO, (b) Main Sequence's >A$1bn deep-tech co-founding, and (c) Brandon's institutional biotech vehicle. Conviction is increasingly **specialist and thesis-dedicated** (climate, biotech, defence) rather than purely generalist.

3. **What 2023–2026 fundraising signals:** A sharp **2023–2024 down market** (capital raised −54% in 2023, decade-low VC fundraising in 2024, frozen ASX exits, "zombie VC" risk, Tenacious-style target cuts) winnowed the field — capital concentrated into established managers and government-anchored / strategic-LP vehicles (CEFC, Temasek, super funds, Defence). The **2025–26 recovery is top-heavy**: Blackbird's A$700m and King River's A$157m closes show LPs re-backing proven brands and clear theses (AI, defence, climate) while first-time/sub-scale managers still struggle. Net: the supply of conviction capital is **more concentrated, more thesis-specialised, and more state-co-invested** in 2026 than in 2021.
