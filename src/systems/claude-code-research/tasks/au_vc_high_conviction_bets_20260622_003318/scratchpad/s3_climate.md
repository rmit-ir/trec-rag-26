## 1. Climate/cleantech funding totals & trajectory

**Climate Salad Australian Climate Tech Industry Report (annual survey of self-reported climate tech cos):**
- 2021: **$338M** raised
- 2022: **$553M** raised (+63% YoY); cumulative >$1.4B raised to date, >4,000 jobs. Agrifood led ($182M), data/finance ($133M), renewables ($90M). [Climate Salad via TechCrunch 2023-12-28](https://techcrunch.com/2023/12/28/australia-climate-tech-industry-booming/); [businessnewsaustralia](https://www.businessnewsaustralia.com/articles/australian-climate-tech-founders-aim-to-raise--1-5-billion-in-12-months.html)
- 2025: **>$680M VC across 69 deals**; ~$957M total climate tech funding identified; >7,000 employed; placed climate tech among **Australia's top 3 funded sectors** (per Climate Salad). Private VC funds increased their capital pool by ~$310M (~50%) — e.g. Wollemi Capital $230M committed by REST Super. [Climate Salad 2025 report](https://www.climatesalad.com/posts/introducing-the-2025-australian-climate-tech-industry-report); [Startup Daily 2025](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/climate-tech/2025-australian-climate-tech-industry-report/); [CEFC](https://www.cefc.com.au/insights/market-reports/2025-australian-climate-tech-industry-report/)
- Report flags $164B+ estimated public funds available (CEFC, ARENA, NRF, FMIA); ~$10B new public funding online in last 18 months (Future Made in Australia, ARENA, National Reconstruction Fund). 2035 target 62–70% emissions cut sharpens demand.

**Cut Through Venture (State of Australian Startup Funding 2025, w/ Folklore):**
- Total Australian startup funding 2025: **$5.48B across 390 deals** (+31% on 2024, 3rd-largest year on record). [scalesuite](https://www.scalesuite.com.au/resources/state-of-australian-startup-funding)
- **Climate Tech / Cleantech ranked 4th-largest funded sector in 2025, raising $585M** (after AI, Fintech, Biotech/Medtech). Maintained top-5 funding status for 5th consecutive quarter. Strength in energy storage, carbon capture, green hydrogen, sustainable agriculture; many deals had govt co-investment. [Cut Through 1Q/2Q 2025](https://www.cutthrough.com/insights/cut-through-quarterly-1q-2025)

NOTE: Cut Through ($585M, by deal-tracking) and Climate Salad ($680M, by survey) differ in methodology but agree on order of magnitude (~$585–680M in 2025) and on climate being a top-3/top-4 sector. Trajectory: $338M (2021) → $553M (2022) → ~$585–680M (2025). Steady, NOT explosive — durable plateau above 2021 levels rather than 2021-style hype spike. (2023/2024 annual climate totals not cleanly isolated in sources; ~$500–600M range implied.)

**Is climate the fastest-growing sector by capital?** Not the single fastest (AI dominated 2025), but it is a **consistent top-3/top-4 sector with the most durable govt-backed capital base.** Confidence: High that it is top-tier; Medium on exact "fastest-growing" ranking.

## 4. Durable conviction vs. 2021 hype

**Signals of DURABLE conviction (not hype):**
- **Dedicated, growing climate funds raised in the 2023–25 down-market:** Virescent Fund II ($100M+ first close 2024), Main Sequence Fund III ($450M, decarb-focused, 2023), Wollemi Capital ($230M REST commitment), Grok's $1.5B all-climate pivot. Raising new climate-dedicated capital in a tighter market is the strongest durability signal.
- **Repeat/follow-on rounds & valuation step-ups:** Samsara Eco ($54M Series A 2022 → $100M extension 2024), Loam Bio ($40M A → $105M B), MGA Thermal (multiple rounds 2023→2024→2026), Hysata (large Series B 2024 w/ blue-chip strategics bp/POSCO/Vestas).
- **Manufacturing scale-up capital + offtakes:** SunDrive (commercial copper-plating JDA 2025), 5B (Solar SunShot $46M manufacturing), Sicona (US anode plant), Samsara (Woolworths/lululemon offtakes). Capital moving from R&D to factories = beyond-hype maturity.
- **Blended/strategic co-investment is structural:** nearly every flagship round pairs CEFC/ARENA/Virescent or Main Sequence with strategic corporates (bp, Origin, Shell, NRG, Temasek, Woolworths, POSCO). CEFC's $4.14 crowd-in ratio institutionalises this.

**Signals of 2021-style hype that COOLED:**
- The 2022 Climate Salad aspiration to raise $1.5B/year (3x actual $553M) was never met — actual annual VC stayed ~$585–680M, i.e. a plateau not a 3x boom.
- Fortescue/some green-hydrogen mega-targets were pared back in 2024 (selective conviction).
- Headline-grabbing 2021/22 deals gave way to more disciplined, govt-anchored, milestone-gated capital.

## Key inferences (with confidence)

1. **Climate tech is a durable top-3/top-4 Australian VC sector (~$585–680M/yr in 2025), not the single fastest-growing (AI leads), but with the most durable, govt-anchored capital base.** Confidence: **High.**
2. **The defining feature of AU climate conviction is blended capital** — CEFC ($4.7B/yr, $32.5B capacity), Virescent (Fund II ~$125M+), ARENA, Main Sequence (decarb Fund III $450M) anchoring nearly every flagship private round. This de-risks deep/capital-intensive tech and is structurally sticky. Confidence: **High.**
3. **Billionaire family offices (Cannon-Brookes/Grok all-climate pivot ~$1.5B; Forrest/Squadron 14GW pipeline) are a uniquely large, patient AU climate capital source**, blurring VC and infrastructure. Confidence: **High.**
4. **Highest-conviction verticals by repeat capital: green hydrogen electrolysers (Hysata), long-duration/flow & thermal storage (Allegro, MGA, Relectrify), enzymatic recycling (Samsara), soil carbon (Loam), copper solar (SunDrive), and lithium-processing tech (Novalith).** Storage + electrolysers + circular economy show the clearest follow-on / scale-up pattern. Confidence: **High** for named rounds; **Medium** on which will reach commercial scale.
5. **2021/22 hype partially cooled** — the 3x funding-growth ambition didn't materialise; capital consolidated into fewer, larger, milestone-gated, manufacturing-stage rounds. The market matured rather than collapsed. Confidence: **Medium-High.**
6. **Critical-minerals *processing tech* (vs. mining) is an emerging but still thin VC vertical** — Novalith is the clearest pure-play tech round; most critical-minerals capital flows through listed miners/govt (FMIA), not classic VC. Confidence: **Medium** (limited VC-stage deal flow found).

### Gaps / lower confidence
- Clean 2023 & 2024 *annual* climate-VC totals not isolated (sources jump from 2022 $553M to 2025 ~$585–680M); trajectory inferred as a plateau. Confidence: **Medium.**
- Element 25 (manganese), Sparc Hydrogen, Goterra, RecycleSmart specific recent rounds not located in this pass — likely smaller/grant-stage. Confidence: **Low** (absence of data, not confirmed absence of deals).
