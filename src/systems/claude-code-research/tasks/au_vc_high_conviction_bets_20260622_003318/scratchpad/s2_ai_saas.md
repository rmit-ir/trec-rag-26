# S2: Australian AI, Data Infra, Dev Tools & Vertical SaaS VC (Jun 2021–Jun 2026)

Scope weighted 2023–2026. All figures AUD unless noted USD. Inline source URLs + dates.

## 0. MACRO CONTEXT — AU funding & AI dominance

- 2025 was AU startups' **strongest year since the post-2022 correction**: **$5.1B** raised, third-largest on record, but masking a sharp fall in deal *count*. [SmartCompany, Dec 2025](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/australia-startups-2025-funding-report/)
- **61% of all 2025 AU startup capital (~$3.1B)** went to startups citing AI in their offering; AI **de-throned fintech** as #1 sector. ~$1.0B went *purely* into AI. [SmartCompany biggest raises 2025](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/biggest-australian-startup-raises-2025/); [ScaleSuite](https://www.scalesuite.com.au/resources/1-billion-into-ai)
- 2024 baseline: AU raised **$4.0B across 414 deals** (capital consolidating into AI, fintech, climate, health). [Waveup 2026 guide](https://waveup.com/blog/venture-capital-firms-australia-vc-ecosystem-overview/)
- Victoria overtook NSW for VC crown in 2025 ($2.2B surge). [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/victoria-venture-capital-funding-2025-report/)
- "Big 3" (Blackbird, Square Peg, AirTree) AUM: Blackbird **$1.24B** committed (largest AU/NZ VC; raised record ~$1B fund), Square Peg **$2.9B** AUM, AirTree **$1.3B** AUM. [easyvc](https://easyvc.ai/blog/top-vcs-in-australia-the-leading-funds-for-ambitious-startups/)
- Warning sign: "AI-first companies priced at a premium, raising faster and often at prices reminiscent of 2021" — some big AI rounds done quietly at 2021-style valuations (CTV's Chris Gillings). [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/venture-capital/ai-cut-through-venture-australian-vc/)

## 5. FADING 2021 HYPE vs DURABLE AI/SaaS CONVICTION

### FADING (2021-vintage burn-down):
- **BNPL** — the archetypal AU bust. Sector mkt cap **~$85B → <$1B** (industry est.). **Openpay collapsed (receivership Feb 2023)**; **Zip** down ~78% over 12 months, exited 10 of 14 intl markets after A$240M FY22 loss, share −95% from Feb 2021 peak; Sezzle −72%, MoneyMe −85%; Zebit/Payright/Splitit/IOUPay/INKPAY delisted or shut. [SmartCompany Openpay](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/finance/openpay-receivership-buy-now-pay-later/); [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/marketintelligence/en/news-insights/latest-news-headlines/australia-s-buy-now-pay-later-players-face-battles-after-pandemic-highs-77566401)
- **High-burn consumer/SaaS over-raisers**: Go1 (5x layoffs 2023 despite $3B paper mark); Culture Amp & Dovetail holding flat 2021 valuations with modest ARR (~A$227M and ~A$5M resp. — Dovetail especially over-valued vs ARR); Spriggy/consumer fintech stalled (no capital since 2022).
- Canva itself took a deep 2022–24 markdown (~$40B → ~$26–32B) before recovering.

### DURABLE / now being underwritten:
1. **AI infrastructure & compute / data centres** — the single biggest AU thesis (Firmus US$5.5B; "sovereign"/Australian-made compute; Nvidia/Coatue/Blackstone capital). Microsoft also committing US$18B to AU AI footprint (Apr 2026). [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/23/microsoft-expands-ai-footprint-in-australia-with-18-billion-investment.html)
2. **AI applications / AI agents (enterprise)** — Relevance AI, Lorikeet (the consensus "all-3-VCs" bet). Agentic / "AI workforce" framing.
3. **AI-for-vertical (esp. healthcare)** — Harrison.ai, Heidi Health: deep proprietary data + regulated industry = defensibility. Healthcare AI is the clearest "durable vertical AI" conviction (Blackbird heavily across both).
4. **Foundation models** — largely NOT underwritten by AU VC. Maincode (Matilda) is self-funded, not VC; CSIRO/ATSE note FMs "rarely made in Australia." AU funds explicitly concentrate on **infra + application layer**, not building frontier models. [CSIRO FM report](https://www.csiro.au/en/research/technology-space/ai/ai-foundation-models-report); [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/venture-capital/ai-cut-through-venture-australian-vc/)
- Thesis quotes: King River raising US$100M fund "making AI investments every month"; one VC argues **"SaaS is dead"** / bullish AU AI (Capital Brief, paywalled headline). Folklore framing "right, not hype." Square Peg/Blackbird/AirTree convergence on single AI deals (Lorikeet, Airwallex) signals consensus high-conviction bets. [Capital Brief headline](https://www.capitalbrief.com/article/saas-is-dead-why-one-vc-is-bullish-on-australian-ai-startups-0b72c1e1-935e-4625-8e67-56d5df4b8917/); [King River fund](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/venture-capital/king-river-capital-is-building-a-157-million-sixth-vc-fund-to-back-ai/)

## KEY INFERENCES (with confidence)

1. **AI infra/compute is the dominant AU high-conviction bet 2025–26.** Firmus's rapid 3-raise escalation to US$5.5B + Microsoft's US$18B + Nvidia/Coatue/Blackstone money make sovereign/Australian-made AI data-centre capacity the largest single capital sink. **Confidence: High.**

2. **Enterprise AI agents/applications are the consensus venture thesis** (Relevance AI, Lorikeet), evidenced by the rare convergence of all 3 big AU VCs on the same deals (Lorikeet = first since Canva). **Confidence: High.**

3. **Healthcare is the clearest durable "AI-for-vertical" conviction** (Harrison.ai US$112M Series C; Heidi US$65M Series B at US$465M). Proprietary regulated data = moat. Blackbird is across both. **Confidence: High.**

4. **AU VCs are NOT underwriting foundation models.** Frontier model building is left to self-funded outliers (Maincode/Matilda) or absent; AU plays infra + app layer. **Confidence: High.**

5. **Canva is the anchor and the bellwether**: its 2022–24 markdown then 2025 recovery (~$26B → ~$49B+) tracks the whole AU SaaS cycle; serial AI acquirer (Leonardo.AI). IPO path is the key 2026 liquidity event. **Confidence: High.**

6. **2021-vintage hype clearly faded**: BNPL imploded ($85B → <$1B), and several SaaS darlings (Go1, Spriggy, arguably Dovetail/Culture Amp) are flat/retrenching vs their 2021–22 marks. The durable SaaS survivors (Employment Hero, SafetyCulture, Deputy) kept raising up-rounds by embedding AI + real ARR. **Confidence: High.**

7. **Valuation-froth caution is real and acknowledged by insiders** — some AU AI rounds done "quietly at 2021 valuations" (CTV); bifurcation between AI premium and everything else. **Confidence: Medium.**

8. **Some brief targets unconfirmed**: "Cosmos" AU data-infra (not found), "Klátia" (not found/typo), SafetyCulture "$3.7B" (latest verified A$2.7B Nov 2024 — newer raise possible but unconfirmed). **Confidence: Medium** these are gaps not contradictions.
