# S4: Australian Defence / Dual-Use, Space & Robotics VC (Jun 2021–Jun 2026)

Strand focus: the post-2022 (post-Ukraine, post-AUKUS) momentum shift. One of the strongest NEW themes in AU VC.

---

## 1. The structural shift: from near-uninvestable to top VC theme

**AUKUS (2021 / 2023).** Pillar II focuses on advanced capabilities: hypersonics, undersea/UUVs, electronic warfare, cyber, AI, quantum — exactly the dual-use stack VCs can back. Partners explicitly working "with investors, private capital, and traditional and non-traditional industry." First Pillar II signature project (UUV payloads, delivery from 2027) announced 30 May 2026. [minister.defence.gov.au AUKUS DM statement 2024-04-09](https://www.minister.defence.gov.au/statements/2024-04-09/aukus-defence-ministers-joint-statement); [defence.gov.au AUKUS advanced capabilities](https://www.defence.gov.au/defence-activities/programs-initiatives/aukus-advanced-capabilities)

**2024 National Defence Strategy + 2024 IIP.** Added ~A$60B to the Integrated Investment Program over a decade → A$330B total. FY2024-25 defence budget ~A$55.7B (~2.0% GDP); rising toward ~A$100B/yr (~2.4% GDP) by FY2033-34. [globalsecurity.org 2024 NDS PDF](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/policy/int/2024-australia-national-defence-strategy_20240417.pdf)

**2026 National Defence Strategy + 2026 IIP (16 Apr 2026).** ~A$425B over the decade in the IIP. Additional **A$14B over 4 yrs / A$53B over the decade**. Cumulative additional investment from 2024+2026 NDS = **A$30B over 4 yrs / A$117B over the decade to 2035-36**. Defence spending to rise to **3% of GDP by 2033** (NATO approach). [minister.defence.gov.au 2026-04-16](https://www.minister.defence.gov.au/media-releases/2026-04-16/2026-national-defence-strategy-integrated-investment-program); [defence.gov.au 2026 NDS/IIP](https://www.defence.gov.au/about/strategic-planning/2026-national-defence-strategy-2026-integrated-investment-program); [defensenews.com 2026-04-17](https://www.defensenews.com/global/asia-pacific/2026/04/17/australia-refines-its-defense-strategy-and-investment-plan/)

**ASCA (Advanced Strategic Capabilities Accelerator), live mid-2023.** A$748M over 4 yrs / A$3.4B over the decade (A$591M above prior innovation spend). Mission-based, fast-tracks asymmetric capability. Has signed EDT-program contracts in information warfare & quantum (e.g. +A$9M on a A$60M/3yr program), plus 14 new EDT contracts across air/land/sea/space/cyber. A$39M via ASCA into quantum/disruptive tech (incl. Nomad Atomics). [asca.gov.au](https://www.asca.gov.au/); [minister.defence.gov.au 2023-07-01](https://www.minister.defence.gov.au/media-releases/2023-07-01/advanced-strategic-capabilities-accelerator-up-running-drive-defence-innovation); [defence.gov.au 2025-08-25](https://www.defence.gov.au/news-events/releases/2025-08-25/defence-backs-local-next-generation-innovation)

**The 2026 ACI Fund — the marquee VC catalyst.** Advanced Capabilities Investment (ACI) Fund: govt seeks **VC co-investment partners**; up to **A$500M govt co-contribution boosted to ≥A$1B with private capital**. Targets defence + dual-use SMEs/startups in cyber, AI & autonomy, electronic warfare, quantum, undersea warfare — with export focus. REOI opened ~18 Feb 2026 (DEFENCE/REOI/61094 on AusTender); responses due 4pm 30 Apr 2026. Roots in the 2024 Defence Industry Development Strategy. [minister.defence.gov.au 2026-02-18](https://www.minister.defence.gov.au/media-releases/2026-02-18/australian-government-seeking-proposals-co-invest-aussie-ingenuity-through-venture-capital-market); [startupdaily.net $1B defence fund](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/venture-capital/the-government-wants-vcs-to-co-invest-in-a-1-billion-defence-industry-fund/); [twobirds.com AU$500M co-investment](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/australia/defence-meets-venture-capital-australia's-au$500-million-co-investment-plan); [govmarket.com.au tender](https://govmarket.com.au/tender/establish-aci-fund-s-to-invest-in-australian-defence-and-dual-use-smes-up-to-500m-government-co-investment); [aspistrategist.org.au "Making the ACI Fund count"](https://www.aspistrategist.org.au/making-the-advanced-capability-investment-fund-count/)

**Why "near-uninvestable → top theme":** (a) Ukraine war proved cheap autonomy/drones at scale; (b) AUKUS Pillar II opened tech-sharing + procurement pull for sovereign dual-use; (c) IIP money + ASCA + NRFC equity de-risked the path to revenue; (d) 2026 ACI Fund explicitly pulls VCs in via govt co-investment. Global tailwind: VC defence-tech deal value hit a record **US$49.1B in 2025 (up from US$27.2B in 2024)**; equity funding for defence-tech startups >doubled to **US$17.9B (from US$7.3B in 2024)**. [news.crunchbase.com defense record](https://news.crunchbase.com/defense-tech/startup-venture-funding-all-time-record-ai-anduril/); [defensenews.com 2026-01-20](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/2026/01/20/defense-tech-startups-had-their-best-funding-year-ever-in-2025/)

**State-of-funding context:** AU startup funding 2025 = ~A$5.48B across 390 deals (3rd-largest year on record); Hardware/Robotics/IoT category got ~A$297M. [scalesuite.com.au](https://www.scalesuite.com.au/resources/state-of-australian-startup-funding); [cutthrough.com 2025](https://www.cutthrough.com/insights/state-of-australian-startup-funding-2025)

## Key inferences

1. **Defence/dual-use is the single strongest NEW momentum theme in AU VC**, and it is overwhelmingly post-2022. The trigger stack is unambiguous: Ukraine (cheap autonomy proof) + AUKUS Pillar II (tech-sharing + procurement pull) + 2024/2026 NDS money (IIP to ~A$425B, +A$117B/decade, 3% GDP by 2033) + ASCA + the 2026 ACI Fund. **Confidence: High.**
2. **The 2026 ACI Fund (up to A$500M govt → ≥A$1B with private capital) is the structural inflection** that converts defence from "uninvestable" to a govt-co-funded VC category — explicitly designed to pull VCs into cyber/AI-autonomy/EW/quantum/undersea. Still at REOI stage (due 30 Apr 2026), so deployment is forward-looking. **Confidence: High on the policy; Medium on near-term deployment.**
3. **Marquee, well-sourced rounds (2024–2026):** Gilmour A$217M Series E (NRFC+Hostplus); Fleet Space A$150M Series D @ A$800M+ val (Ontario Teachers'/Blackbird); Q-CTRL US$113M Series B (GP Bullhound, Lockheed Martin Ventures); Advanced Navigation A$110M Series C (Airtree/NRFC, In-Q-Tel backer); Hypersonix A$46M Series A; Arkeus A$25M Series A (QIC Ventures). **Confidence: High.**
4. **Anduril's A$1.7B Ghost Shark program of record is the anchor demand-signal** (procurement, not VC) proving sovereign autonomous undersea at scale — the clearest "this market is real" proof for investors. **Confidence: High.**
5. **Capital sources are unusually broad & sovereign-heavy:** super funds (Hostplus/HESTA/Future Fund), state SWFs (QIC, Funds SA), the govt NRFC, US strategics (In-Q-Tel, Lockheed Martin Ventures, Bessemer), plus the first dedicated AU defence VC (Beaten Zone). Main Sequence + Blackbird are the deep-tech leads. **Confidence: High.**
6. **Dual-use is the bridge:** the same PNT/autonomy/quantum-sensing/space stack (Advanced Navigation, Fleet Space, Nomad Atomics, Q-CTRL) monetises in mining/critical-minerals AND defence — which is exactly why generalist VCs and super funds can underwrite it. **Confidence: High.**
7. **Unconfirmed names:** "Conscious Capital," "Kelvin," "Dingo," standalone "HyperSpace" not verified as AU defence-VC rounds — likely mis-specified or minor; do not assert. **Confidence: Low (i.e., high confidence they should be omitted/flagged).**
