# What China's Venture Capital Is Betting the Future Looks Like (June 2021 – June 2026)

*A data-grounded reading of where Chinese investors are placing high-conviction early-to-growth-stage bets — and the forward-looking beliefs those bets imply. Weighted toward June 2023 – June 2026. Chinese-language sources prioritized; English used as cross-check. Compiled 2026-06-22.*

---

## 1. The one-paragraph answer

By mid-2026, Chinese venture capital is no longer betting on **consumer-internet business-model innovation (模式创新)**; that entire cluster has collapsed (some tracks down >95%). Capital has migrated, decisively and durably, into **"hard technology" (硬科技)** aligned with the state's **"new quality productive forces" (新质生产力)** doctrine. The dominant forward-looking belief embedded in the allocation is this: **the future will be defined by a contest for technological sovereignty — physical AI, domestic compute, and frontier infrastructure — that the state will underwrite and that China intends to win regardless of market cycles.** The single clearest high-conviction bets, ranked by intensity of capital concentration, follow-on velocity, quality-investor participation, policy alignment, and continued funding *through* corrections, are: **(1) embodied AI / humanoid robots (具身智能/人形机器人); (2) domestic AI compute & the semiconductor supply chain (国产GPU/半导体国产替代); (3) sovereign AI foundation models, agents & AI-native hardware (大模型/AI Agent/AI硬件); (4) commercial spaceflight & the low-altitude economy (商业航天/低空经济); (5) global-facing innovative drugs (创新药 license-out); and (6) frontier energy & materials — nuclear fusion, solid-state batteries, semiconductor materials (可控核聚变/固态电池/新材料).** Underneath all six sits one meta-bet: **alignment with national strategic priorities is now the strongest predictor of where capital flows, because the state is both the largest investor and the guaranteed customer.**

---

## 2. How to read this report (method & confidence)

- **Discovery, not assumption.** Sectors were inferred from the data — repeated capital concentration, rising deal volume, larger rounds, follow-on funding, participation by high-quality investors, policy/procurement alignment, and (critically) continued funding *after* market corrections — not chosen in advance. The anchor datasets are 36氪's "资本大迁徙" five-year track migration study, 清科 (Zero2IPO) and 投中 (ChinaVenture) market data, IT桔子 (ITjuzi) deal data, and the 界面 (Jiemian) / IT桔子 institutional-investor rankings, supplemented by ~40 company-, fund-, and filing-level sources.
- **Five-year vs three-year.** Section 4 contrasts 2021 with 2023–2026 to separate *faded hype* from *new momentum*. The six themes in Section 5 are all **new or intensifying** in the recent three years; Section 6 lists what has cooled.
- **Confidence.** Each theme carries a confidence rating based on source quality, deal transparency, and cross-source consistency. Aggregate deal totals vary by tracker and definition (e.g. "embodied AI only" vs "robotics-wide"), so figures are given as ranges where sources diverge. Valuations are round-implied/paper, not audited. Where a striking number rests on a single source, it is flagged.

## 3. The structural backdrop: who is actually investing

You cannot read China's high-conviction bets without first seeing **who holds the chips**. The defining structural fact of 2021–2026 is the **near-total capture of venture funding by state capital**:

- By 2024, **state-owned capital + government-guided funds (政府引导基金)** accounted for **¥1,254.3 billion — 81.58% of all committed LP capital (认缴出资)**; state-nature capital was ~88.8% of the LP structure ([新浪/南方财经](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-01-18/doc-inefihkp4898699.shtml)). In 2025 Q1, government-type LPs were **73.8%** of institutional-LP commitments (93.1% including state-controlled bodies) ([新浪](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-04-30/doc-ineuxmqz4330351.shtml)). State-controlled GP share rose from **27% (2015) to 81% (2025)** ([新浪](https://finance.sina.com.cn/money/bond/market/2025-05-13/doc-inewmhry1974929.shtml)). USD funds have largely retreated.
- Two state vehicles now **set the agenda**:
  - The **National Venture Capital Guidance Fund (国家创业投资引导基金)**, launched December 2025: ¥100B central fiscal seed (via ultra-long special treasury bonds) leveraged toward a **¥1 trillion** market scale, 20-year life, ≥70% to seed/early stage, explicitly mobilizing insurance capital (险资) and bank AIC equity as "patient capital (耐心资本)," targeting integrated circuits, quantum, biomedicine, brain-computer interface, aerospace, and hydrogen/storage ([发改委](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/wszb/cytzyd/); [21财经](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20251226/herald/77edc9e0ebc4a6d3f5142136d78e3d57.html)).
  - The **National IC Fund Phase III (国家大基金三期)**, May 2024: **¥344B registered capital — larger than Phases I + II combined** — targeting semiconductor manufacturing/equipment/materials plus, newly, **AI/compute chips (算力芯片)** ([21财经](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20240601/2b22680b5ec153267a88e263dbf4e2c1.html)).
- **Middle-East sovereign funds (中东主权基金)** partially backfill departed USD capital: PIF's cumulative China investment reached ~$22B by mid-2024; ME SWFs put **$7B into China from June 2023, ~5× the prior year**, concentrated in semiconductors, biotech, and EVs ([新浪](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-07-21/doc-infheytw8157296.shtml)).
- **Most-active investors (2023–2026)** tilt hard-tech across the board. By 2024 deal count: **中金资本 (CICC Capital, 120 deals), 红杉中国 (HongShan/Sequoia China), 毅达资本, 中科创星 (CAS-linked deep-tech), 高瓴 (Hillhouse), 顺为资本 (Shunwei/Xiaomi), 深创投 (Shenzhen Capital Group), 达晨财智** ([新浪](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-01-06/doc-ineczhrz2583513.shtml)). 界面's 2025 top-five VC ranking: **IDG资本, 红杉中国, 深创投, 金沙江创投 (GSR), 经纬创投 (Matrix)** ([界面](https://m.jiemian.com/article/13791896.html)).

**Why this matters for "beliefs":** when the marginal investor is a government-guided fund or SOE, "high conviction" reflects *national industrial will* as much as private return-seeking. The state both **de-risks the bet** (anchor LP + lead investor) and often **guarantees the demand** (procurement). Private VC, CVC, and Middle-East money increasingly tuck in *behind* that direction. This is the lens for every theme below.

The macro market also turned up in 2025: ~**10,007 investment events Jan–Nov (+30.3% YoY), ~¥1,193.0 billion deployed (+31.5%)**, with new-fund formation +16.7% ([界面](https://www.jiemian.com/article/13826664.html)) — the recovery is real, but it is a **hard-tech, state-led** recovery.

**Reading:** the 2021 imagination engine — *traffic, consumption, model innovation, "burn cash for growth" (烧钱换增长)* — has been replaced by *technology that creates value, self-sufficiency, and national-strategic frontiers*. The themes that **rose in the last three years specifically** (drones, embodied AI, aerospace, AI compute, fusion, license-out drugs) are the subject of Section 5. Critically, several rose **through** the 2022–2024 downturn — the test the prompt asks for.

## 5. The six high-conviction themes

### Theme 1 — Embodied AI & humanoid robots (具身智能 / 人形机器人)
**The single most concentrated, fastest-accelerating bet of the period.**

- **The numbers (high-conviction signal).** Funding exploded from **103 deals / ¥9.4B in 2024** to (narrow "embodied-only" definition) **325 deals / ¥39.8B in 2025 (+~216% deals, +~326% capital)**, or **¥58.8–73.5B on a robotics-wide basis** ([36氪 资本大迁徙](https://36kr.com/p/3647528460144521); [投中](https://m.chinaventure.com.cn/news/80-20251218-389389.html)). It then **accelerated into 2026**: January and February each saw >¥15–16B; "≥¥500M/day"; **9 mega-rounds (≥¥1B) by early March 2026 vs 6 in all of 2025** ([界面](https://m.jiemian.com/article/14126992.html)). Capital is **stratifying**: the top-10 companies took **~41% (¥13.47B)** of 2025 funding, and a "¥10B-valuation club (百亿俱乐部)" of 10+ unicorns formed within ~24 months ([投中](https://m.chinaventure.com.cn/news/80-20251218-389389.html)).
- **Representative startups.** Humanoid whole-body: **宇树科技 (Unitree)** — profitable leader, IPO filing targeting ~¥42B valuation ([经观](https://www.eeo.com.cn/2025/0903/750699.shtml)); **智元机器人 (AgiBot)**, **银河通用 (Galaxy General)** — highest-valued unlisted player at ~¥21B; **星动纪元 (Robot Era)**, **逐际动力 (LimX Dynamics)**, **众擎 (EngineAI)**. "Robot-brain" VLA models: **自变量 (X Square Robot)**, **千寻智能 (Spirit AI)**, **它石智航 (It Stone)**, **智平方 (AI2Robotics)**. Dexterous hands/components: **灵心巧手 (LinkerHand)**, **因时 (Yinshi)**, **帕西尼 (Paxini)**.
- **Notable rounds & investors.** **银河通用** ¥2.5B (2 Mar 2026) led by the **National AI Industry Investment Fund (国家人工智能产业投资基金 / "大基金三期")** with **中国石化 (Sinopec), 中信 (CITIC), 上汽金控 (SAIC)** ([财新](https://m.caixin.com/m/2026-03-02/102418619.html)) — the National AI Fund's first embodied-AI bet. **自变量** A++ ¥~1B (阿里云/Alibaba Cloud, 国科, 国开, 红杉中国) then a ~¥2B B round (小米/Xiaomi, 红杉) ([InfoQ](https://www.infoq.cn/article/whqdfyiibg8burjxppyh)). **千寻智能** ~¥2B (云锋/Yunfeng, 混沌投资, 红杉) ([新浪](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-06-03/doc-iniacxkk4007887.shtml)). **它石智航** $120M (蓝驰/BlueRun + 启明/Qiming co-lead, 高瓴) ~50 days after founding. **众擎** A1 led by **京东 (JD)** with **宁德时代 (CATL)** ([艾邦](https://www.aibangbots.com/a/2543)). Most-active funds: **英诺天使 (Innoangel, 19 deals), 北京国管 (Beijing state, 23 transactions/17 cos), 深创投, 高瓴创投, 百度风投 (Baidu Ventures)**; strategics **腾讯, 阿里, 京东, 美团, 蚂蚁, 小米, 宁德时代** recur throughout.
- **Conviction-through-correction.** When 朱啸虎 (Zhu Xiaohu) publicly exited humanoid bets in early 2025 citing unclear commercialization, funding *accelerated* rather than stopped — skepticism merely **concentrated capital in the leaders** ([投中](https://m.chinaventure.com.cn/news/80-20250402-385742.html)). Follow-on is heavy (高瓴/IDG/BV re-upping into 星海图; 自变量 8 rounds; 智元 10 rounds).
- **Policy & procurement.** "Embodied intelligence" entered the **2025 Government Work Report (政府工作报告) for the first time** ([新华网](http://www.news.cn/tech/20250311/70c6793481a94810a31a5835672ce718/c.html)). Real demand: Unitree's 2026 target of 10,000 units; a ¥124M humanoid tender won by 智元+宇树 ([财联社](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2083429)).
- **Implied thesis & future assumptions.** *Own the physical-instantiation layer of AGI before it scales.* Investors are underwriting: (a) **China's manufacturing edge** — a complete supply chain (motors, reducers, sensors, dexterous hands) makes China the cheapest place to scale embodied hardware; (b) **labor-shortage / aging demographics** filled by humanoids, factories first; (c) **embodied-AGI inevitability** — VLA "robot brains" will generalize like LLMs; (d) **state backing as a demand floor**. Entry thresholds rose so fast that "there is no angel round" — investors pay up early to secure platform positions.
- **Confidence: HIGH** on the conviction conclusion (corroborated across 投中, 36氪, 界面, 财新, 雷峰网, 新华网, IT桔子); **MEDIUM** on exact aggregates (definitions vary) and on valuations (paper). Bear case is explicit in the sources: valuations detached from revenue; one B-round priced at a $300M valuation on ~$8M revenue.

### Theme 2 — Domestic AI compute & the semiconductor supply chain (国产GPU / 半导体国产替代)
**The most state-anchored bet, and the one with the clearest exit wave.**

- **The headline event** is the **"domestic GPU four dragons (国产GPU四小龙)" IPO cluster**: **摩尔线程 (Moore Threads)** listed on the STAR Market (科创板) **5 Dec 2025, raising ¥8.0B**, market cap >¥282B ([stcn](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/3519905.html)); **沐曦 (MetaX/Muxi)** listed **17 Dec 2025, +693% on day one**, ~¥332B cap, at a pre-IPO valuation of ¥210.7B ([观察者网](https://www.guancha.cn/economy/2025_12_17_800691.shtml)); **壁仞科技 (Biren)** listed on HKEX **2 Jan 2026**; **燧原科技 (Enflame)** filed (¥7B cumulative funding, **Tencent + Big Fund II** backed) ([经观](http://www.eeo.com.cn/2025/0625/734299.shtml)). Capital markets absorbed deeply loss-making firms because they are deemed **strategic**.
- **State funds anchor the thesis.** **Big Fund III (大基金三期, ¥344B)** made a first **¥164B** deployment, targeting **70% equipment/materials localization + 30% advanced packaging & AI memory (AI存储)** ([第一财经](https://www.yicai.com/news/102427078.html)). Other backers: the National AI Industry Fund (沐曦), Shanghai/Beijing AI funds, China Internet Investment Fund (中网投), plus 红杉/高瓴/IDG and CVCs (Tencent; Baidu's 昆仑芯/Kunlunxin).
- **Other funded sub-segments.** Advanced packaging (**盛合晶微 / SJ Semiconductor**, $700M round, >$2B cumulative, STAR IPO Apr 2026); RISC-V (**进迭时空 / SpacemiT**, Series B, backed by 中网投 and bank AICs); semiconductor materials (**奕斯伟材料 / ESWIN**, ~¥4B C round with Big Fund II at 65%, STAR IPO approved); EDA (an M&A wave — 华大九天, 概伦电子 — plus primary rounds for 芯华章); third-gen semiconductors/SiC (**百识电子**).
- **Conviction-through-correction.** Overall 2024 semiconductor investment *fell −41.6%*, yet capital **rotated into** AI-compute, EDA, and advanced packaging — the strategic chokepoints — even as the broad sector cooled. Domestic equipment localization rose **15% → 35% (2024 → early 2026)**.
- **Procurement pull.** 昆仑芯 won a ¥1B+ China Mobile inference-compute tender; Huawei 昇腾 (Ascend) shipments rose 64万→81.2万 units (2024→2025); Nvidia's China share fell 70%→55%.
- **Implied thesis & future assumptions.** *Domestic substitution (国产替代 / 自主可控) of the AI-compute stack is a national-strategic, demand-guaranteed market, not a cyclical bet.* Assumptions underwritten: **US export controls persist or tighten** → structural demand for domestic GPUs; **domestic AI-compute demand compounds 40–70%/yr**; **state capital underwrites long-payback chokepoints**; and **STAR/HKEX reopen as exits** for loss-making but strategic firms.
- **Confidence: HIGH.** Headline events are heavily documented and consistent across 新浪, 投中, 第一财经, 财联社, 观察者网, 量子位. Minor caveats on some pre-IPO round figures (note: 沐曦's raise is ~¥4.2B, not ¥42B — one source garbled this).

### Theme 3 — Sovereign AI foundation models, agents & AI-native hardware (大模型 / AI Agent / AI硬件)
**A bifurcated bet: base models became a state-backed sovereign race, while market VC rotated to apps, agents, and hardware.**

- **The model layer.** The **"Six Dragons (六小龙)"** — **智谱 (Zhipu), 月之暗面/Kimi (Moonshot), MiniMax (稀宇), 百川智能 (Baichuan), 阶跃星辰 (StepFun), 零一万物 (01.AI)** — plus open-source disruptor **DeepSeek (深度求索)** anchor the layer. **Zhipu IPO'd in Hong Kong on 8 Jan 2026** (code 2513, +13% day one, ~HK$58B cap; backers 红杉中国, 高瓴, 阿里, 腾讯, 美团, state funds, and Saudi Aramco's **Prosperity7**) ([InfoQ](https://www.infoq.cn/article/AoGqcFOV2vfaoLHmIzaT)); **MiniMax IPO'd the next day, +100%+** ([21经济](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260114/herald/90319a3b75011021941567b4d10621c1.html)). **StepFun raised a >¥5B B+ round (Jan 2026) — the largest single China LLM round in the trailing 12 months** — led by Shanghai state capital with Tencent and 启明 ([财经](https://m.caijing.com.cn/s/202602/5143534)). **DeepSeek** was re-rated from ~$100B to a reported ~$450–500B within weeks of opening its first external raise.
- **The application/agent layer.** Agent winner **Manus (蝴蝶效应 / Butterfly Effect)** raised a Benchmark-led round, then was **acquired by Meta for ~$2B (Dec 2025)** — a controversial "exit to the US" ([虎嗅](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4824081.html)). AI-native hardware: AI glasses **Rokid, XREAL, 雷鸟/RayNeo** (the 2024 cohort was **~80% state-funded** — e.g. Hefei municipal capital led Rokid); AI toys **跃然创新/Haivivi** (BubblePal, ¥200M A led by **中金资本 + 红杉中国**) and **珞博智能/Robopoet** (Sequoia China exclusive lead).
- **Conviction-through-correction — and a key nuance.** After the 2024–2025 "大模型降温" (model cool-down) and price war, the **base-model layer shrank to just 22 deals / ¥9.4B in 2025** — and the investors who kept funding it were predominantly **local state-investment funds (地方国投) and Middle-East/sovereign capital, not market VC** ([新浪](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/stockzmt/2025-03-20/doc-ineqhwik4830391.shtml)). Market VC rotated to apps/agents/hardware, where monetization is now real (MiniMax 9-month revenue +170% YoY, >70% overseas; Kimi paid-user and overseas-API growth surging).
- **Implied thesis & future assumptions.** Two distinct beliefs: (a) at the model layer, *China must field sovereign frontier models to avoid dependence on US labs* — hence state capital, HK listings, and **"de-Nvidia-fication (去英伟达化)"** / Huawei-Ascend compatibility (DeepSeek V4) as a hedge against export controls; (b) at the app layer, *value now accrues to agents, vertical apps, and AI-native devices*, because open-source DeepSeek collapsed base-model differentiation. AI glasses/toys are bets on a **"next-iPhone" form factor**.
- **Confidence: HIGH** overall (cross-confirmed across 投中, 36氪, 21经济, 新浪, InfoQ); **MEDIUM** on the fastest-moving valuations (Kimi ~$30B, DeepSeek $450–500B) — reported but fluid.

### Theme 4 — Commercial spaceflight & the low-altitude economy (商业航天 / 低空经济)
**Sovereign infrastructure bets with state capital crowding in and an imminent IPO wave.**

- **Commercial spaceflight.** ~**¥20.2B (2024, record)** and **¥18.6B (2025, +32% YoY)** across ~140 deals/year; **Jan–Feb 2026 alone hit ~¥20B** — re-accelerating ([投中](https://www.chinaventure.com.cn/news/114-20260225-390222.html)). Round sizes escalated from millions to billions: **垣信卫星 (SpaceSail)**, operator of the **千帆 (Qianfan/G60) LEO constellation**, raised a record **¥6.7B A** (2024) and a fresh ¥5–6B round at a **>¥40B valuation**; **星际荣耀** raised a ¥5.04B round (2026); **蓝箭航天 (LandSpace)** completed STAR-board IPO coaching (~¥20B valuation, "商业航天第一股") after its **朱雀三号 (Zhuque-3) reusable-rocket recovery test (Dec 2025)** ([新浪](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-12-24/doc-inhcxizc9648434.shtml)). Government-fund share of space deals rose **41% → ~60% YoY**, anchored by the **National Manufacturing Transformation & Upgrade Fund (国家制造业转型升级基金)** and local SASAC platforms.
- **Low-altitude / eVTOL.** **小鹏汇天 (XPeng AeroHT)** — ~$1B cumulative, HK IPO filed, backers 高瓴/红杉; **沃飞长空 (AeroFugia, Geely)** — ~¥1B growth round; **亿航 (EHang)** — Nasdaq-listed, holds full airworthiness certificates (TC+PC+AC). Written into the **2024 Government Work Report as a "new growth engine"**; 30 provinces listed 低空经济 in 2025 plans.
- **Implied thesis & future assumptions.** *Sovereign LEO-internet race vs Starlink* (千帆 + 国网/GW dual mega-constellations as a spectrum/orbital land-grab — 千帆 targets 15,000 satellites by 2030); *reusable-rocket cost curve* (China replicates SpaceX to slash $/kg); *urban air mobility* as a new sovereign mobility layer leveraging the auto supply chain (CATL batteries). The structural pattern: **government funds de-risk early, VCs ride alongside, AMCs/sovereign funds consolidate winners pre-IPO.**
- **Confidence: HIGH for spaceflight, MEDIUM-HIGH for eVTOL.** Caveat: EHang remains loss-making (9M-2025 loss ¥242M) and **>90% of eVTOL "orders" are non-binding LOIs**; valuations (小鹏 theoretical ¥240B) are aspirational.

### Theme 5 — Globally-validated innovative drugs (创新药 / license-out)
**A bet validated not by IPOs but by global pharma writing checks.**

- **The defining 2024–2025 trend is the record license-out / BD wave (对外授权/出海).** China cross-border out-licensing hit **~$135.7–137.7B in 2025 (vs $51.9B in 2024)** across ~157–186 deals — **China is now ~49% of global out-licensing deal value, surpassing the US** ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3339011/chinese-drug-makers-strike-record-us136-billion-out-licensing-deals-2025); [PharmaSource](https://pharmasource.global/content/china-biopharma-out-licensing-surges-to-record-137-7b-in-2025-2026-on-pace-to-break-it-again/)). Average upfronts rose $102M → $141M ("the bargain era is over") ([PharmaVoice](https://www.pharmavoice.com/news/china-biotech-recognize-value-bargain-over/814979/)).
- **Marquee deals.** **三生制药 (3SBio) → Pfizer**, PD-1/VEGF bispecific, **$1.25B upfront / $6.05B total** (May 2025); **石药 (CSPC) → AstraZeneca**, obesity, up to **$18.5B**; **恒瑞 (Hengrui) → GSK**, $500M up / up to $12B, plus a ~$6B GLP-1 **NewCo** with Kailera; **荣昌 (RemeGen) → AbbVie**, up to $5.6B. Hot modalities: PD-(L)1/VEGF bispecifics, ADC, GLP-1/obesity.
- **Representative companies & investors.** Drugs: **信达 (Innovent)** — world-first approved GCG/GLP-1 dual agonist mazdutide; **恒瑞, 三生, 康宁杰瑞 (Alphamab), 百利天恒 (BaiLi)**. AI-pharma: **晶泰 (XtalPi)** (HK IPO 2024), **英矽智能 (Insilico Medicine)** (HK IPO 30 Dec 2025, +45% day one). Synthetic bio: **蓝晶微生物 (Bluepha), 微元合成 (WeyMicro)**. Most-active medical investors: **启明 (Qiming), 礼来亚洲 (Lilly Asia Ventures), 元生 (Yuansheng), 高瓴, 红杉, OrbiMed**, plus state/local funds.
- **Conviction-through-correction.** Biotech endured a **2022–2024 "winter"** (primary funding fell to ~1/5 of the 2021 peak; HK-18A IPOs dropped 20→4). The 2025 recovery is led by **BD validation + a HK IPO reopening (26 biopharma IPOs in 2025, proceeds +751.7% YoY)**, and H1-2025 saw 43 innovative-drug approvals (+59% YoY, 93% domestic), under explicit **"full-chain support (全链条支持)"** policy.
- **Implied thesis & future assumptions.** *China is being repriced as a global drug-innovation engine, not a CRO base* — built on an **"engineer dividend (工程师红利)"** and fast/cheap clinical trials that de-risk assets for Western pharma. License-out/BD is underwritten as the **primary exit and validation path** while domestic equity markets stay thin; GLP-1/obesity demand is assumed structurally huge.
- **Confidence: HIGH.** Record license-out and the funding recovery are corroborated across SCMP, PharmaVoice, PharmaSource, 投中, 医药魔方, 21经济. (Caveat: aggregate totals differ by tracker; one Chinese source's "600亿美元" for Q1-2026 means ~$60B, not $600B.)

### Theme 6 — Frontier energy & materials (可控核聚变 / 固态电池 / 新材料)
**The longest-horizon bets — where state and marquee CVC capital is "fighting for allocations."**

- **Controlled nuclear fusion (可控核聚变) — the hottest sub-segment by conviction intensity.** Record private rounds: **星环聚能 (Star Energy)** ¥1B A (Jan 2026, led by Shanghai SOEs + the 未来产业基金) — a record for a Chinese private fusion firm; **诺瓦聚变 (Nova Fusion)** ¥500M angel (Jul 2025, incl. **Alibaba, 君联**); **能量奇点 (Energy Singularity)** (backed by **miHoYo/米哈游 and NIO/蔚来资本**) built the world's first all-HTS tokamak (HH70). A national team, **中国聚变能源 (China Fusion Energy)**, formed with ¥11.49B. "Investment institutions are fighting for allocations (抢份额)" ([投中](https://www.chinaventure.com.cn/news/112-20251027-388574.html)).
- **Solid-state batteries (固态电池) — HOT, near-term catalyst.** **清陶能源 (QingTao)** ¥2.7B G+ (SAIC's 尚颀资本); **卫蓝 (WeLion)** at a ¥18.5B valuation with 48 backers (Huawei, Xiaomi, NIO), IPO-bound; **太蓝 (Tailan)** repeat rounds. OEMs lock in supply via equity ahead of a 2026–2027 mass-production inflection.
- **Semiconductor / new materials — HOT, strongest fundamentals.** **奕斯伟材料 (ESWIN)** ¥4B C round (Big Fund II = 65%), STAR IPO approved, with real revenue and customers (CXMT/华虹/BOE). Sector deal count rose +40% in 2024.
- **Implied thesis & future assumptions.** *Fusion* = the ultimate **"future industry (未来产业)"** energy moonshot — whoever leads commercial fusion (~2035) captures a civilization-scale market, made urgent by AI/compute energy demand; *solid-state* = extend China's Li-ion dominance into the next generation; *new materials* = **卡脖子 (chokepoint) self-sufficiency** as national security.
- **Confidence: HIGH/HOT for fusion, solid-state, and semiconductor materials; MEDIUM for 3D printing; MEDIUM-LOW/COOLING for hydrogen (氢能).** Fusion conviction is *narrative-driven* (pre-revenue, ≥10-year horizon); materials conviction is *fundamentals-backed*.

## 6. The counter-signal: what Chinese capital has *stopped* believing in

A complete reading of "beliefs" requires the negatives. The following were de-funded in the last three years (Section 4 dataset):

- **The entire consumer-internet / model-innovation cluster** — down >95% in aggregate: **二手电商 (−99.8%), 同城物流 (−99.7%), 汽车服务 (−99.0%), 服装 (−98.9%), 生鲜 (−96.5%), 食品饮料/餐饮 (−90% to −93%)**. Belief abandoned: *that traffic and burn-for-growth create durable value.*
- **Policy-killed tracks:** **在线教育 (online education, −96.8%)** after the 2021 "双减" policy; **互金/P2P (−95.6%)** after regulatory crackdown; **游戏/文娱 (−86.6%)** under licensing (版号) constraints.
- **SaaS / 企业服务 (−77% to −93%)** — 99% of Chinese SaaS remained loss-making; the US-style software-multiple thesis did not transfer.
- **元宇宙 / VR (Metaverse/VR)** — the 2021 bubble deflated; capital migrated to AI, which had clearer commercialization. Within hard-tech, **standalone autonomous driving (自动驾驶)** passed its hype peak and is now funded as a *feature* of robotics/embodied AI rather than its own story; **hydrogen fuel cells (氢能燃料电池)** are cooling (2024 financing −12.4%; leading fuel-cell firms lost >¥1.8B combined) as capital narrows to green-hydrogen *production*.

**What the negatives reveal:** Chinese investors no longer believe the future is won by *consumer attention and business-model cleverness*. They believe it is won by *defensible technology, hardware, and strategic infrastructure* — and they will not fund a theme the state has signaled against (education, fintech, gaming).

---

## 7. Synthesis: the future Chinese capital is underwriting

Stacking the six themes and the negatives, a coherent **forward-looking worldview** emerges. Chinese investors are, with real money, betting that:

1. **The future is a contest for technological sovereignty.** Across compute (国产GPU), models (sovereign LLMs), space (LEO constellations), and materials (卡脖子 substitution), the unifying assumption is **prolonged US-China decoupling** in which **self-sufficiency (自主可控) is survival, not optimization**. This is the strongest and most consistent signal in the data.

2. **Physical AI is the next platform.** The convergence of AI and hardware — humanoids, robots, drones, eVTOLs, AI glasses — is treated as the successor to the consumer internet as the "imagination engine." The bet is that China's **manufacturing supply chain converts an AI-software race into an embodied-hardware race China can win on cost and scale.**

3. **The state will manufacture the demand and de-risk the bet.** With state capital at >80% of LP money and SOEs/operators as guaranteed customers (compute tenders, satellite orders, humanoid procurement), investors are underwriting a world where **policy alignment substitutes for market validation** in the early years — "patient capital (耐心资本)" engineered to match long hard-tech payback.

4. **China's innovation goes global through licensing, not just products.** The biotech license-out wave shows a belief that China can be a **net exporter of R&D** to the world's incumbents, monetized through BD deals rather than domestic IPOs.

5. **Some bets are explicitly civilizational and long-dated.** Fusion (~2035) and frontier energy reveal a willingness to fund **decade-plus moonshots** as strategic options on the energy basis of an AI-powered economy — a confidence that the state will sustain funding across cycles.

**The honest caveats (and where this could be wrong).** The conviction is real but carries known fragilities the sources themselves flag: (a) **valuations are detached from fundamentals** in embodied AI and eVTOL (paper unicorns, LOI "order books," thin revenue); (b) **state direction can misallocate** — concentration risk and overcapacity/内卷 already visible in sodium-ion storage and consumer 3D printing; (c) **the model layer's private-market conviction has actually thinned** (base-model funding fell in 2025; survival is now state/sovereign-dependent), a reminder that "high conviction" is increasingly *the state's* conviction; (d) **exits remain uncertain** — the IPO wave (科创板/港股) is reopening but many listings trade below issue, and Manus's sale to Meta shows capital-flight pressure. **Overall confidence in the directional thesis: HIGH.** Confidence in any single valuation or aggregate figure: **MEDIUM** — treat the ranges, not the point estimates, as the signal.

> **Bottom line:** Where China's venture capital is placing its highest-conviction bets in 2023–2026 tells you it believes the future belongs to **technologically sovereign, state-aligned, hardware-and-frontier-science champions** — embodied AI, domestic compute, sovereign models, space and low-altitude infrastructure, globally-licensed drugs, and frontier energy/materials — and that the era of consumer-internet, traffic, and business-model innovation is decisively over.
