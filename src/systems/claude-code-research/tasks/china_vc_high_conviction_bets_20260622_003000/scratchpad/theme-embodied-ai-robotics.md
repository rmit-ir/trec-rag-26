- 2025 (broad robotics+embodied, year-end): **744 deals, ¥73.543bn (735.43亿)** → ~7x vs 2024 [sina][search]
- Alt 2025 count (机器人大讲堂 embodied subset): 298 deals, ¥32.9bn, +291% capital YoY; 168 funded cos (+87%) [leaderobot]
- H1 2025: >¥20bn already, head concentration evident [leiphone]
- 2026 YTD: Jan & Feb each **>¥15–16bn/month**; "≥¥500M/day"; **9 mega-rounds (≥¥1bn) by early March 2026 vs 6 in all of 2025** [jiemian][36kr]
- Q1 2026: **50+ events, 30+ cos, ~¥20bn, ~+60% YoY; 10+ cos valued >¥10bn (百亿俱乐部)** [huacheng]

**Capital concentration (stratification):** Top-10 companies took **~40.95% (¥13.472bn / 134.72亿)** of 2025 funding [chinaventure-389389]. "百亿俱乐部" of 10+ unicorns formed within ~24 months; 银河通用 ~¥21bn highest unlisted.

**Post-correction continued funding:** Despite 朱啸虎 (Zhu Xiaohu/Galileo) publicly批量退出 humanoid bets in early 2025 citing unclear commercialization, sector funding *accelerated* into 2026 (9 mega-rounds by March) — i.e. correction skepticism did not stop capital; it concentrated it in leaders [chinaventure-385742][jiemian].

**Policy alignment:** "具身智能" + "智能机器人" written into **2025 Government Work Report (政府工作报告) for the first time** (Mar 5 2025), as future industry under "AI+" [stcn][news.cn][thepaper]. Local: Shanghai targets "fastest mass-production city"; Shenzhen industrial plan [shanghai.gov.cn][fgw.sz].

**Procurement / customer traction:** Unitree first 1,000 Walker S2 industrial humanoids off line, 2025 deliveries >500, 2026 target 万台 (10k); year-end 2025 national humanoid orders ~5,700 robots → target 10k [news.cn]. 智元+宇树 won ¥124M (1.24亿) humanoid tender [cls]. Reported **State Grid 国家电网 ¥6.8bn (68亿) embodied plan**: 5,000 quadruped inspection dogs (¥1.5bn), 500 live-work humanoids (¥2.5bn), 3,000 dual-arm inspection robots (¥1.8bn) [csdn/news] — *single-source, treat as indicative*.

## 5. Implied investment thesis & future assumptions underwritten

The bet is to **own the physical-instantiation ("embodied") layer of AGI** before it scales. Investors are underwriting:
1. **China manufacturing edge** — complete supply chain (motors, reducers, sensors, dexterous hands) makes China the natural place to scale embodied hardware cheaply (Li Liang/银河通用) [chinaventure-385742].
2. **Demographic / labor-shortage** — humanoids fill aging-workforce & factory labor gaps; industrial deployment (汽车厂, 巡检) is first beachhead.
3. **Embodied AGI inevitability / scaling laws** — VLA "robot brains" (GO-1, WALL-B, Spirit v1, RDT) will generalize like LLMs; "once-in-a-lifetime" platform window.
4. **State backing de-risks** — policy + national fund + SOE procurement create demand floor and political tailwind.
5. **Winner-take-most** — capital concentrates in 10+ leaders pre-emptively; "no angel round" (entry threshold rose to **>$50M** in Q1 2025) means investors pay up early to secure platform positions.

**Bear/risk flagged in sources:** valuations detached from fundamentals — one B-round at $300M valn on only ~$8M revenue & $27M burn; only 14% of 23 surveyed cos showed quantifiable cost savings; burn ~$2.7M/mo vs 19% rev growth ("40% worse capital efficiency than bike-sharing bubble") [chinaventure-385742]. Zhu Xiaohu exit = explicit dissent.

- **Low/caveated** items: State Grid ¥68亿 plan (single source, "网传"); some English company-name mappings ambiguous (星海图, 至简动力, 智平方) — used pinyin + best-effort EN.
