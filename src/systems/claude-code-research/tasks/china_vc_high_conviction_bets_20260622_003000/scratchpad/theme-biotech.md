# Theme: China VC High-Conviction Bets — Biotech / Innovative Drugs / Synbio / AI-Pharma

Window: Jun 2021–Jun 2026 (weight on Jun 2023–Jun 2026). Compiled by sub-agent, 2026-06-22.

## 1. Headline thesis (TL;DR)
Chinese biotech moved from a 2022–2024 "winter" (primary-market funding fell to ~1/5 of the 2021 peak; HK-18A IPOs collapsed from 20 in 2021 to 4 in 2024) to a sharp 2025 recovery. The recovery is driven less by traditional equity rounds and more by a **record wave of license-out / BD deals to global pharma**, which both validates the science and creates a new exit/cash path. This is the core of investor conviction: China is being repriced as a global drug-*innovation* engine, not just a CRO/CDMO base.

## 2. The defining 2024–2025 trend: license-out / BD to global pharma

Chinese-language trackers cite **1,356.55亿美元 / ~$1357B total, 157 deals, >$70B aggregate upfront** for 2025. ## 5. High-conviction evidence
1. **Funding recovery 2025:** HK 26 biopharma IPOs in 2025 (vs 9 in 2024); proceeds HK$29.27B, +751.7% YoY; 16 unprofitable 18A cos raised HK$13.77B (47% of total). [sina](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-12-25/doc-inhcywap3629203.shtml)
2. **Record BD totals** ($135.7B+, see §2) = global pharma "buy-in" validation; 1/3 of all global licensing spend China-sourced.
3. **Rising upfronts + NewCo equity** → real cash returns to investors, partial exit path absent strong IPO market.
4. **Policy alignment (全链条支持):** "创新药" named a priority in 2025 govt work report; 16-measure NHSA package (Jul 2025) on pricing autonomy, faster reimbursement; NMPA clinical-trial approval cut to 30 days; STAR-Market 5th-set standard reopened (禾元生物 first through). [nhsa.gov.cn](https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2025/7/1/art_14_17065.html); [mee.gov.cn](https://www.mee.gov.cn/zcwj/zclcfh/202507/t20250702_1122645.shtml)
5. **Clinical output:** H1 2025 43 innovative drugs approved (+59% YoY, 5-yr high; 93% domestic). [bydrug](https://bydrug.pharmcube.com/news/detail/3a98b7ffb2f60e60d5a214b2ff656a71)

## 6. Implied thesis + future assumptions
- **China = global drug-innovation engine** ("工程师红利" / engineer dividend + low-cost fast clinical trials → de-risked assets at fraction of M&A cost). Western pharma treats China as "de-risked innovation hub." [pharmasource.global]
- **License-out/BD as the primary exit & validation path** while domestic IPO/equity markets remain thin — investors underwrite to a BD outcome, not just IPO.
- **GLP-1/obesity demand** assumed structurally huge → multiple Chinese dual/triple-agonist bets.
- **Biomanufacturing import substitution** + green/bio-based bulk chemicals thesis for synbio; state capital de-risks.
- **AI-pharma**: bet that AI compresses discovery cost/time (ISM001-055: target→PCC in 18 months, $2.6M) and that licensing royalties validate the platform.
- Risks flagged: HK "上市热→生存战" (IPO ≠ survival, 75%+ break-issue historically); AI-pharma profitability unproven ("death valley"); geopolitics/biosecure-act overhang on US-China BD.
