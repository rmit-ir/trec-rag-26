Confidence on overall thesis: **HIGH**. Specific deal-count figures: MED-HIGH. Cooling-sector %: HIGH (sourced to 36kr/澎湃 dataset).

## 1. STATE-CAPITAL QUANTIFICATION (WHO is investing)

### State LP dominance — the structural fact
- **2024**: 国资 + government-guided funds accounted for **¥1,254.277B (81.58%)** of total committed LP capital (认缴出资). [新浪/南方财经](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-01-18/doc-inefihkp4898699.shtml)
- State-nature capital ≈ **88.8%** of LP structure; government money (guided funds + local 国资) ≈ **52.5%**. [同上](https://www.sfccn.com/2025/1-18/zMMDE0MDRfMTk4NTMzMA.html)
- **2025**: 国资 ≥ **80%** of committed capital; 国资 LP share risen to ~**55%**; state-controlled share grew **27% (2015) → 81% (2025)**. Government money as share of institutional-LP commitments climbed **40.8% → 68.3%**. [新浪 行业研究](https://finance.sina.com.cn/money/bond/market/2025-05-13/doc-inewmhry1974929.shtml)
- **2025 Q1**: government-type LPs committed **¥418.8B = 73.8%** of all institutional-LP commitments; including state-controlled bodies, **93.1%**. [新浪](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-04-30/doc-ineuxmqz4330351.shtml)
- Takeaway: GP money is now overwhelmingly state-sourced; market-raised (USD-fund / private RMB) capital has been crowded down to a minority. USD funds largely retreating.

## 4. COOLING SECTORS w/ % + reason
- **消费互联网/模式创新 cluster** — overall >95% funding collapse; reason = end of "烧钱换增长", consumer contraction (1st-tier restaurant revenue −4.6/4.7%, e-comm −7% in 2024), no clear profit model. [36kr decline](https://36kr.com/p/3646209617416064)
- **在线教育/培训** −96.84% — 2021 "双减" policy banned for-profit K12 tutoring (policy-driven). 
- **互金/P2P/消费金融** −95.64% — regulatory crackdown + risk (policy+market).
- **二手电商 −99.83%, 同城物流 −99.65%, 汽车服务 −99.04%, 服装 −98.85%, 生鲜 −96.47%** — pure consumer/traffic models, market.
- **游戏/文娱** −86.59% — regulatory (版号) + market.
- **SaaS/企业服务** −77~93% — 99% of Chinese SaaS still loss-making.
- **医疗/创新药** −81~96% — 医保控费/集采扩围 (policy margin compression).
- **元宇宙/VR** — bubble deflated; capital rotated to AI (clearer commercialization). [新华网](https://www.news.cn/tech/20250910/6996880576cd41839582204cb321c25f/c.html), [澎湃](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32250602)
- Within hard-tech: 自动驾驶 passed its 2021 hype peak (still funded but as a feature of robotics/具身智能 rather than standalone story); pure 元宇宙/VR cooled.

## 5. IMPLIED META-THESIS
The rotation is a **paradigm shift, not a cycle**: from "模式创新/流量经济/讲故事/烧钱换增长" → "硬科技/技术创价值/拼硬功." What Chinese investors now believe about the future:
1. **自主可控 / 国产替代** (semis, optoelectronics, aerospace, materials) is the durable bet — survival-level strategic priority, de-risked by state co-investment.
2. **新质生产力 / 国家意志-aligned**: capital follows policy. ¥1T 国家创投引导基金 + ¥344B 大基金三期 set the agenda; private/CVC/中东 capital tucks in behind state direction toward seed/early hard-tech.
3. **具身智能/机器人/无人机/航空航天 = the new growth frontier** (AI×hardware convergence), replacing consumer internet as the imagination engine.
4. **Patient/long money** (耐心资本: 险资, AIC, 国资 with whole-lifecycle assessment) is being engineered to match long hard-tech payback — a deliberate institutional re-plumbing.
Net: alignment with national strategic priorities is now the single strongest predictor of where Chinese VC capital flows.
