# Theme: China VC in AI Foundation Models, Apps/Agents & AI-Native Hardware (Jun 2021–Jun 2026)

Scope: foundation models (大模型/基础模型), AI applications & agents (AI应用/AI Agent/智能体), AI-native consumer hardware (AI眼镜/AI玩具/AI硬件). Heavier weight Jun 2023–Jun 2026.

## 1. Representative Startups (≥6)

### Foundation-model companies — "AI 六小虎/六小龙" (the Six Tigers/Dragons)
1. **智谱AI / Zhipu AI (智谱华章)** — GLM models. Most institutionalized; first to IPO.
2. **月之暗面 / Moonshot AI (Kimi)** — founder 杨植麟 Yang Zhilin (Tsinghua). Consumer chatbot Kimi.
3. **MiniMax (稀宇科技)** — Talkie/星野 (companion apps), 海螺AI (Hailuo, video/audio gen).
4. **百川智能 / Baichuan AI** — founder 王慧文-adjacent; pivoted to vertical/medical AI.
5. **阶跃星辰 / StepFun** — "Shanghai's Nvidia"; multimodal + edge/端侧 models.
6. **零一万物 / 01.AI (Yi)** — founder 李开复 Kai-Fu Lee; abandoned pre-training of general models, pivot to apps.

### Open-source disruptor
7. **DeepSeek / 深度求索** — founder 梁文锋 Liang Wenfeng (High-Flyer/幻方 quant fund backed). Open-source R1/V3; reshaped the whole sector Jan 2025.

### Agent / application startups
8. **蝴蝶效应 / Butterfly Effect (Manus)** — founder 肖弘 Xiao Hong. General AI agent "Manus" launched Mar 2025.

### AI-native hardware
9. **Rokid** — AR/AI glasses. 10. **XREAL** — AR glasses. 11. **雷鸟创新 / RayNeo** — AI glasses. 12. **闪极科技 / Sharge** — AI glasses.

## 4. High-Conviction Evidence

- **Round sizes / valuations:** StepFun >50亿 B+ (largest in trailing 12mo) and DeepSeek's $100B→$500B re-rating in 3 weeks are the clearest conviction signals. Zhipu+MiniMax both IPO'd successfully (Jan 2026) with day-1 pops (+13%, +100%+).
- **Follow-on after the 2024–25 correction:** 2025 model-layer = only 22 deals, 94.16亿元 disclosed — **down YoY**, large rounds rare. ["大模型降温"] Capital rotated to **consumer apps, enterprise apps, AI infra** rather than base models. [新浪财经/21经济网]
- **Who keeps funding base models post-correction:** mainly **local state-investment funds (地方国投)** + Middle-East/sovereign capital — NOT purely market-driven VC. State has non-return objectives (sovereign-model, industrial policy). [https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/stockzmt/2025-03-20/doc-ineqhwik4830391.shtml]
- **Pre-training → application pivot:** 零一万物 and 百川智能 abandoned general-model pre-training, moved to AI apps / verticals. Moonshot+StepFun split toward deep-reasoning vs edge models. Monetization now real: MiniMax 9-mo revenue +170% YoY; Kimi paid users +170% MoM (Sep–Nov 2025), overseas API +4x; Moonshot earned ~2亿 from C-side.
- **Policy alignment:** "Six Dragons" framed as national/sovereign AI champions; state funds and HK IPOs (港股) as strategic listing venue amid US-China decoupling; DeepSeek's "去英伟达化"/Huawei Ascend compatibility is explicitly policy-aligned.
- **Cohort divergence ("洗牌分化"):** From "六小龙" to "AI双雄/四小强" — clear winnowing. Manus's Meta sale ($2B) signals exit-by-acquisition path + capital flight controversy.

## 5. Implied Thesis + Future Assumptions

- **AGI / frontier race:** China must field sovereign frontier models to avoid dependence on US labs; foundation-model bets are partly strategic-national, not pure ROI (hence state capital dominance).
- **Sovereign-model + compute-sovereignty need:** "去英伟达化", Huawei Ascend compatibility, domestic-chip alignment → models that run on Chinese silicon are a hedge against export controls.
- **Application/agent monetization is the new locus of conviction:** post-2025, market VC believes value accrues at the app/agent/infra layer (海螺, Kimi, Manus, enterprise APIs), not commoditized base models — DeepSeek open-source collapsed base-model differentiation/pricing.
- **Open-source DeepSeek effect:** cheap, open, GPT-class models compressed margins and forced a price war ("价格战"), pushing closed-model startups toward apps, edge devices, and overseas monetization; simultaneously made DeepSeek itself the prized asset ($100B–$500B).
- **AI-native hardware as "next iPhone moment":** AI glasses & toys are bets that AI needs a new consumer form factor; high gross margins (toys 50–90%) and state-backed manufacturing clusters (Hefei, etc.) underpin conviction, tempered by return-rate/retention risk.
- **HK IPO + Middle-East capital as the funding/exit channel** under US-China tech decoupling.
