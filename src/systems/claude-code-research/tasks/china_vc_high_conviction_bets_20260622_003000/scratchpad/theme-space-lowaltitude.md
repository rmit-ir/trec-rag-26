# China VC: Commercial Spaceflight (商业航天) & Low-Altitude Economy (低空经济)

Window: Jun 2021–Jun 2026, weighted Jun 2023–Jun 2026. Sub-agent scratchpad.

## 1. Representative Startups (≥6, EN + CN)

### Rockets (火箭)
- **LandSpace 蓝箭航天** — reusable liquid methalox; 朱雀三号 (Zhuque-3) reusable rocket first flight + first-stage recovery test Dec 3 2025. IPO candidate.
- **Tianbing Tech 天兵科技** — 天龙系列 liquid rockets.
- **Galactic Energy / StarShip Power 星河动力** — 谷神星一号 (Ceres-1) solid rocket, 23 cumulative launches; 谷神星二号 iterating after early-2025 failed maiden flight.
- **i-Space / StarJet 星际荣耀** (note: 投中 D++ "StarJet Cosmos" likely refers to 星际荣耀; large 50.37亿 D++ Jan–Feb 2026).
- **Orienspace 东方空间** — 引力一号 (Gravity-1), world's first all-solid strap-on medium rocket, 2 successful launches; 引力二号 reusable kerolox planned 2026.
- **CAS Space 中科宇航** (CAS-linked) — 力箭系列; IPO planning.

### Satellites / Satellite-Internet (卫星 / 卫星互联网)
- **Yuanxin / SpaceSail 垣信卫星** — operator of 千帆星座 (Qianfan / G60 "Spacesail") LEO constellation. Shanghai SASAC-controlled.
- **GeeSAT/GalaxySpace 银河航天** — LEO satellites; got commercial satellite-internet operating license.
- **GuoSi Aerospace 格思航天** — 千帆 satellite manufacturer (G60 digital factory).
- **GW / Guowang 星网集团 (China SatNet)** — state-owned operator of GW 国网 mega-constellation (~13,000 sats), competitor to 千帆.

### eVTOL / Flying cars / Drones (低空经济)
- **EHang 亿航智能** — Nasdaq: EH; EH216-S, only eVTOL with TC+PC+AC; operating cert Mar 28 2025.
- **Xpeng AeroHT 小鹏汇天** — flying car "陆地航母" (Land Aircraft Carrier); HK IPO secretly filed.
- **AeroFugia / Wofly 沃飞长空** (Geely 吉利) — AE200 eVTOL.
- **TCab Tech 时的科技** — E20 eVTOL.
- **Volant / Fengfei / others** — various eVTOL/drone makers.

## 3. Most Active / Repeat Investors & State-Fund Role

- **国家制造业转型升级基金 (National Manufacturing Transformation & Upgrade Fund)** — repeat anchor: led 垣信 ¥6.7bn A, LandSpace ¥900m. Key sovereign vehicle.
- **Local government platforms** dominate: Beijing 亦庄国投 (Yizhuang), Wuxi (8 collective deals; 君阳 3 rounds in 5 months), Sichuan/Chengdu, Guangdong (粤财/广州投资/广州数据), Shandong Haiyang (sea-launch port), 上海联和投资 (Shanghai SASAC, 垣信 founder/backer).
- **National AMCs** (China Citic, China Bank AMC, China Orient AMC) consolidating into sector leaders at C-round/Pre-IPO.
- **CAS-linked** 国科资本 (in 垣信).
- **Classic VCs**: Sequoia 红杉, Matrix 经纬, Hillhouse 高瓴, Gaorong 高榕, 达晨, 明势 — still present in rockets (LandSpace) and eVTOL (小鹏汇天).
- **Govt capital share in spaceflight rose from 41% → 59.8% YoY** (投中网).

## 4. High-Conviction Evidence

- **Volume/amount**: 2025 commercial-space ¥18.6bn total, **+32% YoY**; 140 financing events; rockets ¥7.115bn (44% of disclosed). 2024 was 138 events / ¥20.2bn (record). Jan–Feb 2026 alone: 99 events / ~¥20bn ("200亿+押注商业航天") — pace re-accelerating.
- **Round-size escalation**: early-2025 millions → mid-2025 hundreds of millions → late-2025/2026 billions. Single-round records keep breaking (垣信 ¥6.7bn, 星际 D++ ¥5.037bn).
- **Follow-on velocity**: 君阳 4 rounds (A→B) in 12 months (~90-day intervals); 沃飞 5 rounds.
- **IPO wave**: 蓝箭 completed IPO coaching Dec 23 2025 (CICC, ~¥20bn valuation, "商业航天第一股" STAR board); 天兵, 中科宇航 IPO-planning; 小鹏汇天 HK secret filing; STAR board issued commercial-rocket IPO rules Dec 2025. "2026 = China commercial spaceflight IPO debut year."
- **Policy alignment**: 商业航天 + 低空经济 written into 2024 政府工作报告 as "新增长引擎"; reaffirmed 2025 ("安全健康发展"). 30 provinces listed 低空经济 in 2025 plans. "新质生产力" framing. 十四五 / 国家综合立体交通网规划纲要.
- **Procurement / order signals**: 千帆 targets 648 sats by end-2025, 1,296 by 2027, 15,000 by 2030; GW constellation ~13,000. eVTOL: EHang TC+PC+AC+OC complete, 216 units delivered 2024 (+315%); large (mostly non-binding LOI) order book — Xpeng 600 (Mideast), Volant 500 (Thailand $1.75bn), TCab 350 (UAE $1bn).

## 5. Implied Thesis + Future Assumptions

- **China LEO sovereign-internet race vs Starlink**: 千帆 (G60) + 国网 GW dual mega-constellations = spectrum/orbital-slot land-grab; bet that China builds parallel sovereign space-internet infrastructure. State capital treats it as strategic infrastructure, not pure VC return.
- **Reusable-rocket cost curve**: 朱雀三号 recovery = bet China replicates SpaceX reusability to slash $/kg and enable constellation buildout (launch cadence is the bottleneck — "快速发射组网仍需持续降本").
- **Urban Air Mobility / sovereign air-traffic layer**: eVTOL airworthiness-cert + low-altitude airspace reform = bet on new mobility category; OEMs (Geely/沃飞, Xpeng/小鹏) leverage auto supply-chain (CATL batteries, motors) for cost.
- **Industrial-policy crowding-in**: government guidance funds de-risk early stage; classic VCs ride alongside; AMCs/sovereign funds consolidate winners pre-IPO. Bet that state backing guarantees demand (constellation orders, govt eVTOL procurement) and an IPO exit.

## 6. Confidence

**HIGH** for spaceflight; **MEDIUM-HIGH** for low-altitude/eVTOL.
- Rationale (high): multiple corroborating tier-1 sources (投中网, 界面, 新浪/财联社, 观察者网, 36氪) agree on direction — rising deal count/amount, record rounds, surging state-capital share (41→60%), concrete IPO pipeline, explicit policy designation. Quantitatively strong, cross-confirmed.
- Caveat (eVTOL medium): commercial viability unproven — EHang Q3 2025 net loss ¥82m (+70.7% YoY), 9M loss ¥242m despite orders; >90% of eVTOL "orders" are non-binding LOIs with no deposits. Funding conviction is real but cash-flow validation lags; valuations (小鹏 theoretical ¥240bn) are aspirational.
