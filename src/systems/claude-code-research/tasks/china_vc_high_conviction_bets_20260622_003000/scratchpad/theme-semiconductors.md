## 4. High-conviction evidence

- **Concentration in AI compute:** GPU "四小龙" all reached IPO/listing within ~Jun 2025–Jan 2026 — Moore Threads (Dec 5), Muxi (Dec 17), Biren (Jan 2 HKEX), Enflame (filed). Capital-market access despite heavy losses (Moore Threads ¥3B+ cumulative loss yet ¥8B raise).
- **Deal scale:** Big Fund III ¥344B corpus, first ¥164B deployment. 盛合晶微 >US$2B cumulative. Combined GPU IPO raises ~¥12B.
- **Follow-on / continued funding post-correction:** Although overall 2024 semiconductor project investment fell **-41.6% to ¥6,831B** ([sina](https://finance.sina.com.cn/7x24/2025-02-27/doc-inemwuet3485427.shtml)) and equipment deal count dipped -19% in 2024, 2025 deal count rebounded +3.1% to 66 (equipment); AI-compute and EDA saw *rising* primary-market heat even amid broad cooling — capital rotated INTO strategic chokepoints. 2025 total投资 projected ¥5,000–5,500B (narrower decline 20–25%).
- **Policy alignment (国产替代/自主可控):** Big Fund III explicitly targets equipment/materials localization + advanced packaging + AI memory. Domestic equipment localization rate rose 15%→35% (2024→early 2026); etch/deposition >40%.
- **Procurement / 订单 (demand pull):** China Mobile AI-server procurement; 昆仑芯 won ¥1B+ China Mobile 2025–26 inference-compute tender. Domestic AI-chip shipments: 昇腾 64万→81.2万 (2024→2025); Nvidia China share 70%→55%. Internet majors (Baidu, Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance) increasing domestic-chip procurement (though some specific order rumors denied).
- **Smart-compute demand:** China intelligent-compute scale 725.3 EFLOPS (2024, +74.1%) → projected 1037.3 EFLOPS (2025, +43%).

## 5. Implied thesis + future assumptions

**Implied thesis:** Capital is concentrating on **domestic substitution of the AI-compute and semiconductor-equipment value chain (国产替代/自主可控 2.0)**, treating it as a national-strategic, demand-guaranteed market rather than a normal cyclical bet.

**Future assumptions baked into the bets:**
1. **US export controls persist/tighten** → Nvidia/foreign supply remains constrained → structural demand for domestic GPU/AI accelerators (import substitution). 
2. **Domestic AI-compute demand keeps compounding** (LLM training/inference, sovereign/operator/cloud build-outs) — intelligent-compute scaling 40–70%/yr.
3. **State capital underwrites the chokepoints** — Big Fund III + government-guidance funds de-risk equipment/materials/advanced-packaging/AI-memory where commercial payback is long.
4. **Self-sufficiency timeline** — localization rate climbing (equipment 15%→35%); bets assume continued march toward 70%+ self-sufficiency in equipment/materials.
5. **Capital markets (STAR/HKEX) reopen as exit** for loss-making but strategic chip firms — IPO proceeds fund the next R&D cycle; profitability deferred (Muxi "2026 profitability" expectation priced in).
6. **Procurement guaranteed by SOEs/operators/cloud majors** sustains revenue ramp (订单 visibility).
