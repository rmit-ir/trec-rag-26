## 3. High-conviction evidence
- Record-breaking rounds despite 2022–24 broad VC contraction: fusion ¥1B (Star Energy), ¥500M (Nova); solid-state ¥2.7B (QingTao); materials ¥4B (ESWIN).
- Repeat/follow-on: ESWIN cumulative >¥10B; WeLion 9 rounds/48 backers; QingTao 11 rounds; Energy Singularity ~$1.1B cumulative claimed.
- Post-correction acceleration: fusion deals "起风" 2025; materials deal count +40% (2024); 5+ solid-state rounds early 2026.
- Policy alignment: fusion written into Atomic Energy Law + reported ¥200B "fusion industry fund"; 新质生产力/制造强国/双碳/未来产业; NDRC storage action plan 2025–2027; National Big Fund for materials self-sufficiency.
- Traction: ESWIN mass-produces 12-inch wafers (华虹/CXMT/BOE customers); WeLion 314Ah semi-solid mass-prod; Creality 27.9% global consumer-3D share.

## 4. Implied thesis + future assumptions
- **Fusion = the ultimate "未来产业" energy moonshot / AI-power bet.** Assumption: HTS-material breakthrough makes compact tokamaks viable; whoever leads commercial fusion (~2035) captures a civilization-scale energy market; AI/compute energy demand makes it strategic. Highest-conviction *narrative* despite ≥10-year horizon. State treats it as strategic, not purely financial.
- **Solid-state batteries = next-gen EV/storage leadership.** Assumption: China extends its Li-ion dominance into the solid-state generation; 2026–2027 mass-prod inflection; auto OEMs (SAIC/GAC/NIO) lock in supply via equity.
- **New materials = self-sufficiency / 卡脖子 substitution.** Assumption: import substitution in semiconductor wafers/films/carbon fiber is a national-security imperative; state capital de-risks long, capital-heavy R&D. China manufacturing upgrade (制造强国).
- **Storage/sodium = scale + cost / grid buildout** under 双碳, but commoditizing.
- **3D printing = consumer-hardware export + industrial 增材.** China-as-global-factory upgrade; consumer side scaling fast, profitability lagging.
- **Hydrogen = long-term decarbonization** but de-risking; capital now favors green-H2 production/electrolyzers over money-losing fuel cells.

## 5. Confidence & hot-vs-cooling
- Fusion: **HIGH conviction / HOT** — record rounds, heavy state + marquee CVC, "抢份额," law/fund backing. Caveat: pre-revenue, ≥10-yr horizon (narrative-driven, not fundamentals).
- Solid-state batteries: **HIGH / HOT** — huge rounds, OEM strategic capital, near-term (2026–27) mass-prod catalyst, IPO pipeline.
- Semiconductor/new materials: **HIGH / HOT** — Big Fund II anchoring, +40% deal growth, real revenue + customer traction; strongest fundamentals-backed conviction.
- Storage/sodium: **MED–HIGH / HOT but cyclical** — mega-capacity buildout, but overcapacity/internecine price pressure (内卷) risk.
- 3D printing: **MED / WARM** — deal-count high, big names entering, but consumer players loss-making (Creality −¥180M).
- Hydrogen: **MED–LOW / COOLING** — financing −12.4%, fuel-cell losses, policy dependence; selective bets on green-H2 production only.

**Single hottest sub-segment: controlled nuclear fusion (可控核聚变)** by conviction/intensity (record rounds, state + miHoYo/NIO/Alibaba, "投资机构抢份额"). For fundamentals-backed conviction, semiconductor/new materials (ESWIN, Big Fund II) is the most solid.
